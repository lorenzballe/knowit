# The faces of What founders read

Each face in this folder is a photo from Wikimedia Commons, free to use
under the licence named, cropped square to the head and shoulders and
made 256 by 256 pixels (WebP) for the app. Nothing else was changed. The
app says who took each photo, and its licence, on the person's page and
under their recommendation on a book's page (lib/data/founders.dart,
lib/explore/blocks/core/founders_block.dart). None of these people has
any connection with Astute.
