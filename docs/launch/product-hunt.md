## Nome

Astute

## Tagline (60 caratteri al massimo)

Five cards a day: answer, say how sure, see why

## Descrizione (260 caratteri al massimo)

Five cards every morning from the subjects you choose. Answer, say how sure you are, then turn the card over: the answer, the reasoning and a named source. Hundreds of cards are drawn to be played. Over the weeks you learn how far to trust your "sure".

## Il primo commento, del maker

Hi everyone, I'm Lorenzo and I made Astute.

The idea is small on purpose: five cards a morning, about two minutes. Some cards ask a question, and before the answer you say how sure you are, from 50% to 90%. Then the card turns over with the reasoning and where it comes from. After a few weeks your profile shows how often you are right when you say 70%, 80% or 90%, which is usually less often than we think.

A few things I want to be clear about:

- The cards are written ahead of time by an AI model, each from a named source you can open. A second model reviews most of them, and anything it cannot confirm goes to a person. You can flag a card, and a flagged card stops being shown until someone has checked it.
- Many of the drawn cards start from science explainer videos. Each names the video, usually with a link to the minute it comes from.
- The cards are in English for now; the app itself speaks 13 languages.
- The free plan is five cards a day and Explore, with today's challenge (five questions, the same for everyone) and its short exercises. Astute+ picks all five at your level and shows how you change week by week; the yearly plan starts with 14 days free.

What I would most like to hear: which card made you stop and think, and which one was wrong or dull.
