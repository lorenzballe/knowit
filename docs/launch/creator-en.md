# Email to a creator, in English

For Veritasium, written out; for any other creator, the same email with
their numbers from the table below.

## Prima di mandarla

- **Decidi se, a una loro richiesta, toglieresti le carte.** L’email lo
  offre: è la posizione onesta, perché 326 carte disegnate nascono dai video
  di Veritasium.
- **Crea il codice** per il loro pubblico (Play Console → Monetizza con
  Play → Codici promozionali, per esempio `VERITASIUM`) e provalo tu prima.
  Sull’iPhone, i codici offerta di App Store Connect, quando l’app c’è.
- **Carica il video** della carta sulle ossa in orbita
  (`space-bones-in-orbit`) su YouTube come «non in elenco», e metti il link
  al posto di [video link].
- **L’indirizzo giusto:** l’email per le collaborazioni nella pagina
  «About» del canale.
- **Una quota degli abbonamenti** fatti con il loro codice: solo se lo
  decidi tu, e allora va scritta nel secondo punto.
- **Mandala prima** di qualunque post pubblico che parli delle carte nate
  dai video.

## Subject

326 Astute cards start from your videos: a path made together?

## Text

Hi,

I'm Lorenzo, and I make Astute, an app that gives people five cards each morning to play with: a question, something to tap or drag, the answer and its source.

I'm writing because 326 of our drawn cards are built on Veritasium videos, from the world's longest straw to bone loss in orbit. Each card is in our own words, names the video it comes from, and in 260 cases links to the minute where you explain the point.

I wanted you to hear it from me before we talk about the app in public, and to suggest two things:

- a path made together: ten cards you choose from the ones based on your videos, from the easiest to the hardest, checked by your team and carrying your name;
- a code for your audience that opens Astute+ free for a period we agree on.

If you would rather the cards based on your videos were not in the app, tell me and I will take them out.

Here is one of them as a twenty-second video: [video link]. The app is free on Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.astuto.app

Thank you for the videos. I'm glad to talk whenever it suits you.

Lorenzo  
TheBaleCompany · https://astutetheapp.com
