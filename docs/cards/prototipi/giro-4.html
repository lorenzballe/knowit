ScienceWATCH

Drop a stone from twice as high. Does it take twice as long to land?

1 unit ≈ 4.9 m of fall · no air

Bet first. Then watch the stone fall, second by second.

Each second adds the next odd number, the L that makes the square one size bigger. So distance grows with time × time, and a fall from twice as high takes only 1.4 times as long.

WHAT TO KEEPWhen something keeps speeding up, double the distance is not double the time.
Source · Galileo, Two New Sciences (1638), Third Day
