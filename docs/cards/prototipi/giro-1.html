Language

How did a secret slang of gay men reach millions of BBC radio listeners in the 1960s?

Through a comedy show. In Round the Horne, from 1965, two camp characters, Julian and Sandy, spoke Polari, a slang built from Italian, Romani, back slang and theatre talk: bona for good, omi for man. Audiences laughed at words many did not know were code, and the exposure hastened the code’s decline.

MedicineTRUE OR FALSE

A test is 99% accurate and you test positive. So you are 99% likely to be ill.

False. Say the illness hits 1 in 1,000. Test 100,000 people: 99 of the 100 who are ill test positive, and so do about 1,000 healthy people the test gets wrong. Your positive is one of roughly 1,100, and only 99 of those are ill: about 9%.

WHAT TO KEEPHow rare something is matters as much as how good the test is.
