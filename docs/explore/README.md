# Explore, in pieces

The owner's design canvas for Explore is `prototypes.html` (a bundled page:
open it in a browser and it unpacks itself; `prototypes-unpacked.html` is the
same page already unpacked, to read and search). Every idea in it becomes a
block that the server can place, size, schedule and aim (docs/REMOTE.md,
`explore`), so a day's Explore can be any mix of them.

The canvas is in rounds, newest first. Each round's first paragraph says what
it is for; every design has its own anchor (`#131a`, …).

| Round | What it is | Designs | Family (`lib/explore/blocks/`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 131 | A shape for every kind of card: 131a is the screen as it shipped; b–f change only the shape of each shelf | 131a–f | `shapes` |
| 132 | One signature graphic per kind of shelf (struck-out quote for myths, ruler of years, the figure itself, two halves for a debate, a stamp for a place, a setting sun, two circles for a paradox), and the same signatures in other layouts | 132a–b | `shapes` |
| 133 | Shelves built on what Astute is for: what you will do with a card, committing before opening, one thinking move across subjects, shelves picked for you, forms for more themes | 133a–e | `purpose` |
| 138 | The bottom of Explore: by feeling, by where the trick hides, by questions about your own life, cards played on the shelf, shelves as sets to finish, the very bottom | 138a–f | `purpose` |
| 139 | Bets: estimate before you open — a range with a slip, stakes from a daily 100, steppers and the bigger of two, three steps of more or less, the bet of the day and the crowd | 139a–e | `bets` |
| 140 | Whole Explore screens, one idea each: the expert, made for you by mood and minutes, connecting the dots, a sixty-second round and series, finding again the questions you asked | 140a–e | `screens` |
| 141 | Six more screens: keep asking why, react to a fact, a ruler of time, where you stand before and after, lines to say at dinner, the day as a front page | 141a–f | `screens` |

Some of these are already in the app as shelves (Spot the false, Unmask the
chart, Mood minutes, Sixty seconds, Series, How sure, Work it out…): those are
the `core` family's, drawn as they are today.

The facts, chains and claims written into the canvas are there for the
prototype. A block never shows them as they are: its words and cards come
from the bank, or from the section in the remote document, checked like any
card.
