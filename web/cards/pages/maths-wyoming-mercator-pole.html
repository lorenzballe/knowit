What happens to Wyoming’s size when you slide it toward the North Pole?

Latitude 43°NDrawn ×1.0 bigger

Drag Wyoming up the Mercator map, toward the pole.

Same land, same square kilometres. But Mercator stretches every length at latitude φ by 1/cos φ, both ways, so area grows by that squared. Near the pole cos φ shrinks to zero: the stretch has no limit, and 90° N never fits on the map.

WHAT TO KEEPOn a Mercator map, the closer to a pole, the bigger things look.
