Chewing coca leaves, how many hours do Potosí miners go without food or water?

12 to 14 hours

In the Potosí silver mine, miners chew coca leaves to avoid eating and drinking: the leaves make hunger and thirst go away. So they work 12–14 hour shifts on nothing. Inner ring: your guess. Outer ring: the miners.

Tap the clock: each tap is one hour without food or water.

WHAT TO KEEPCoca doesn't feed the miners; it silences their hunger and thirst.
