ACQUA ALTA

Why is a flood in Venice never down to one cause?

All three, at the same time

Alone, each one leaves the street dry. The Moon and Sun pull the tide up, wind pushes water in and low pressure lifts the sea, while the ground slowly sinks. Stacked together, they top the pavement.

Pick what floods the street. Then watch the water.

WHAT TO KEEPA flood is the product of several causes acting at once.
