READ THE ROCKS

In Gubbio's rocks, how do the plankton shells change across the extinction?

Drag up through the pink layers: are each layer's shells still Cretaceous kinds? Mark from none to all.

A step: the plankton died almost all at once.

In the early 1960s Isabella Premoli Silva and a Swiss colleague dated Gubbio's rocks by their foraminifera, tiny plankton shells. The last Cretaceous layer and the first Paleogene one hold completely different species; between them lies a dark red clay with no foraminifera.

WHAT TO KEEP A slow cause leaves a slope in the record; a sudden one leaves a step.
