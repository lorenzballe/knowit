RELATIVITY

Why must GPS satellite clocks be corrected for Einstein's relativity?

Drag the right-hand clock up into orbit, then watch both clocks.

Einstein's special and general relativity say time passes at a different rate on the satellites than on Earth. Left alone, their atomic clocks slip out of step with ours and GPS loses accuracy, so they get slight corrections.

WHAT TO KEEPIn orbit time runs at a different pace, so GPS clocks are corrected.
