What is the 'vicious cycle' in science education?

Drag the slider to send the student round the loop.

Each time round, the student feels surer of the wrong idea. The surer they feel, the less of the correct explanation gets in, so the next round starts even surer.

WHAT TO KEEPConfidence in a wrong idea blocks the right one, so tackle the misconception first.
Source · Veritasium, “Khan Academy and the Effectiveness of Science Videos” (YouTube, 2011)
