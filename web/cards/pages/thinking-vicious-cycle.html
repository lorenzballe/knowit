MISCONCEPTIONS

What is the 'vicious cycle' in science education?

Each time round the loop, the student feels surer of the wrong idea, and the surer they feel, the less of the correct explanation gets in.

The student is sure humans lived with dinosaurs. Play them a clear video of the right answer.

WHAT TO KEEPConfidence in a wrong idea blocks the right one, so tackle the misconception first.
Source · Veritasium, “Khan Academy and the Effectiveness of Science Videos” (YouTube, 2011)
