How does a robot mouse drive down a diagonal?

Two infrared sensors on its nose measure how far the walls are on the left and on the right.

Tap the three runs in order and watch the red sensor beams.

WHAT TO KEEPA diagonal has no wall to follow, so it needs its own rule: blinders on.
