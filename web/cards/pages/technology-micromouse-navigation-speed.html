What is the true challenge of Micromouse?

Speed30%
Route found in a blink. Now drive it.

Set the speed, pick the turns, launch. Try to go fast.

Finding the route is the easy part.

The mouse knows the way almost at once. The hard part is driving it: flat out on the straights, clean through every turn, never touching a wall. That is why the robots keep getting smaller, faster, lighter.

WHAT TO KEEPIn Micromouse, solving is easy; navigating fast is the real race.
