What was Jack Foley's job before he made sounds for films?

He drew maps.

Jack Foley, born in New York in 1891, started out as a cartographer. He fell for the new world of cinema, which stayed silent until 1927, when Warner Bros brought out a film with sound. Then someone had to make every noise, like snapping celery for a breaking bone.
