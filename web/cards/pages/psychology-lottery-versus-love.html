POINT OF VIEW

One view: why risk 1‑in‑20,000 on lottery, not 1‑in‑10 on love?

The view: we dread a “no” more than losing money. Drag yourself to where you stand.

The odds aren’t the whole price.

For the view: love’s odds are far better, and a stranger’s “no” is gone by lunch. Passing on that is fear, not maths.

Against it: a ticket is lost cheaply and in private. A “no” from someone in your life can cost the friendship, so the fear can be fair.

ASK YOURSELF Is it the odds that stop me, or what this one “no” would really cost?
