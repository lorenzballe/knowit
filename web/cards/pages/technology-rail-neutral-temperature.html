RAILWAYS

Why do railways weld rails at as high a temperature as possible?

Drag the welding line up and down. Watch the two risks trade off.

A welded rail is relaxed only at the temperature it was welded at. Weld it hot, and it is rarely squeezed in summer, so it hardly ever buckles; it is pulled harder in winter instead. A winter crack is less of a problem than a summer buckle, and it is easier to find, by measuring the rail's conductivity.

WHAT TO KEEPWeld rails hot: a crack in winter beats a buckle in summer.
