RECYCLING

Why can glass be recycled almost forever?

1 kg in, 1 kg out. Every time.

Glass does not degrade when it melts. A kilogram of waste glass becomes a kilogram of new glass, so the yield is almost 100%, round after round.

Start with 1 kg of old glass. Drag right: how much is left after each melt?

WHAT TO KEEPMelting doesn't wear glass out, so the loop barely loses anything.
