WINTER HIDEOUT

Where does the common mosquito go in winter?

It doesn't die. It hides.

The adult common mosquito, Culex pipiens, survives winter. It shelters in protected spots like basements, where the temperature stays steady while it freezes outside.

Hold the button and watch where the mosquito goes as it gets cold.

WHAT TO KEEPCommon mosquitoes overwinter as adults, hidden in sheltered, steady-temperature places.
