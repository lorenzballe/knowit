VAN ALLEN BELTS

Why did Apollo astronauts survive crossing the Van Allen radiation belts?

Tap a route to fly it. Watch its radiation bar fill.

The belts are a donut around Earth’s middle, not a shell all around it. Apollo flew out across their thin outer edge, so it spent little time in strong radiation.

WHAT TO KEEPThe belts are a ring, not a wall: you can skirt the thick part.
