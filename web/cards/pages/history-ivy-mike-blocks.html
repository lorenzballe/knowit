DRAG TO GUESS

Ivy Mike yielded 10.4 megatons; Trinity, 25 kilotons. How many times stronger?

1 · Drag: how many Trinity yields add up to one Ivy Mike?

About 400 times. 10.4 megatons is 10,400 kilotons.

10.4 ÷ 25 ≈ 0.4, and mega is 1,000 × kilo, so 0.4 × 1,000 ≈ 400. The trap: 10.4 looks smaller than 25.

WHAT TO KEEP Compare the digits, then multiply by the ratio of the prefixes.
