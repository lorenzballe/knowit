DRAG THE ZOOM

Why do zoomed photos make this bridge look so steep?

Drag the zoom ring to 5×. The camera backs off to keep the bridge in frame.

A zoom sees a narrow angle, so you stand far back. Then foot and top are almost equally far: same size, stacked. The gentle ramp reads as a wall.

WHAT TO KEEPZoom squeezes distance: near and far look alike, so slopes look steeper.
