What does glycerol turn into when heated tobacco reaches 350 °C?

glycerol 14 formaldehyde 0 acetaldehyde 0 20 °C

Formaldehyde and acetaldehyde

Heated tobacco runs hotter than an e-cig, close to 350 °C. That heat speeds up dehydration: glycerol sheds water and splits into these two aldehydes, both toxic and carcinogenic.
