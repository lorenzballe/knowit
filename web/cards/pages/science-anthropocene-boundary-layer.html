What would the line between Holocene and Anthropocene look like in rock?

A new epoch needs one thin layer found in rock everywhere on Earth, laid down at the same moment. Here it would hold radioactive particles, plastics, and carbon and nitrogen thrown off their natural cycles, partly by fertilizers. Until that signal is confirmed, we are officially still in the Holocene.
