BOILING POINT

Why does water boil at 100 °C at 1 atm?

Drag the pot B line to pump air out, then heat both pots.

Water boils when its vapor pressure pushes as hard as the air pressing down on it. In open air that push is 1 atm, and water's vapor pressure only reaches it at 100 °C. Pump the air away and the fast molecules escape sooner: water can even boil at room temperature.

WHAT TO KEEPBoiling point isn't fixed: less pressure on top, cooler boil.
