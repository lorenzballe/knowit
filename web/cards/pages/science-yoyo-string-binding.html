YO-YO PHYSICS

What makes a yo-yo climb back up its string?

The string binds to the spool.

A tug pulls the string from one side of the spool to the other. The yo-yo keeps spinning the same way, so the spool grabs the string and rolls itself up it.

It is “sleeping”: spinning at the bottom of the string. Zoom in.

WHAT TO KEEPThe spin was there all along; the tug just lets the spool catch the string.
