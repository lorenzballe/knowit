GAS BLOWOUT

How did a pressure blowout during gas drilling form the Darvaza crater?

Turkmenistan, the desert near Darvaza. A crew is looking for a natural gas deposit.

The ground fell into the void.

The drill hit gas under high pressure. Its sudden rush upward set off the accident: a chasm opened under the crew's feet, and the ground and all the drilling gear collapsed into it.
