MALARIA

If you catch malaria, how do you smell to mosquitoes?

Better: they flock to you

The malaria parasite changes your body chemistry, so you give off a smell that draws mosquitoes. And mosquitoes are its ride to the next person.

Pick one, then watch the mosquitoes.

WHAT TO KEEPMalaria makes you smell better to the very mosquitoes that spread it.
