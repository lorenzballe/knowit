OCEAN SALT

If all the oceans' salt were one cube, how big would it be?

Drag the salt block onto Sicily and watch it rise.

265 km on each side

Pile up every grain of salt dissolved in the seas and you get a cube about as wide as all of Sicily. Standing beside Etna, you would look up at a wall of salt.

WHAT TO KEEPThe oceans hold an island-sized cube of salt: about 265 km a side.
