How much Carrara marble has been used for Ashgabat’s construction?

About 4.5 million m²

Turkmenistan’s capital clad more than 540 new buildings in very white Carrara marble, brought mainly from Italy, to make the city gleam. Laid flat, that is a square about 2.1 km on each side.
