FAULT LINES

A fault jumps 4 metres. What happens to a tunnel crossing it?

Cut like by a knife

In 1908 the Messina fault opened a step of two to four metres. A tunnel crossing it would be sliced: one half drops, the other stays. A bridge can absorb such a step far more easily.
