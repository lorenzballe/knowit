THE 10 A.M. RULE

Why does putting out every small fire make the big ones worse?

TREES 45%

Trees grow, lightning strikes, fire jumps to touching trees. Each bar is a fire: most small, a few run far, as in real records.

WHAT TO KEEPSmall fires keep a forest patchy. Put them all out and it fills up for one giant.
