How hot is it inside a volcano's eruption cloud?

500 °C to 1,200 °C

Cooler at the edges, hottest in the core. That is hot enough to instantly vaporize most materials the cloud meets.

Pick a range, then watch the probe cross the cloud.

WHAT TO KEEPAn eruption cloud runs from about 500 °C at its edge to 1,200 °C in its core.
