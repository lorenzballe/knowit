What is the West Melbury Marly Chalk, and why tunnel through it?

A chalk that is tight and the same all the way

The West Melbury Marly Chalk is a carbonate rock that lets very little water through, and it is compact and homogeneous. So the machine cuts the same rock from end to end, in the dry, with walls that hold.

Pick a layer to aim at, then hold the button to bore.

WHAT TO KEEPThe best rock to tunnel is the one that stays the same and keeps water out.
