OCEAN WAVES

Why do low-frequency waves travel farther than high-frequency ones?

Near the storm, both kinds of wave are strong. High-frequency waves lose their energy quickly, so they fade close to the storm. The fast, low-frequency swell keeps going and arrives hundreds of miles away.

Drag the boat away from the storm and watch which waves are still there.
