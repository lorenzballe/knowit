How much energy does solar return, compared with 1960s oil?

In the 1960s, the energy of one barrel got you a hundred barrels of oil. Solar panels today give back only 5 to 17 times the energy spent to make them. Far less surplus is left over for everything else.

Flip between oil in the 1960s and solar panels today.

WHAT TO KEEPEROI = energy you get back ÷ energy you spend to get it.
