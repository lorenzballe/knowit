FIRE FOAM

Why do firefighting planes use foam on fires above 1000°C?

Water alone can do very little.

Above 1000°C, plain water barely touches the fire. So the Canadair has a second tank of foaming agent: a surfactant, like those in household detergents, mixed into the water to make foam.

Tap each side to drop it on the fire.

WHAT TO KEEPToo hot for water? Add a surfactant and fight it with foam.
