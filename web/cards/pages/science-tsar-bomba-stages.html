TWO STAGES

The Tsar Bomba has two parts. Which one goes off first?

The plutonium sphere goes first.

Its explosive layers crush the plutonium core until its nuclei split: fission. That blast ignites the hydrogen in the cylinder, whose nuclei fuse: the second stage.
