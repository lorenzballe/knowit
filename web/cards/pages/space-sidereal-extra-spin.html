SIDEREAL YEAR

Why do the stars see Earth spin 366.24 times a year, not 365.24?

A toy year of 4 days. Drag Earth once round the Sun and count its spins two ways.

WHAT TO KEEPCounted against the stars (a sidereal year), Earth spins once more than it has days.
