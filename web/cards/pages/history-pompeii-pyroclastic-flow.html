VESUVIUS

What volcanic event actually buried Pompeii?

A pyroclastic flow: a burning cloud of ash, pumice and gas.

In 79 AD a violent eruption of Vesuvius sent it racing down the slope at incredible speed, destroying everything. Then the material it carried settled, little by little, until the whole city was covered.
