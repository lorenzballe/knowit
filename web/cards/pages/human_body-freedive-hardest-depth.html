FREEDIVING

At what depth does a freedive become the most tiring?

Around 35–40 m, on the way up

There the water's pressure has made the body dense enough to be pulled toward the bottom. To climb back, the diver must beat that pull, burning energy and oxygen, and hypoxia begins.
