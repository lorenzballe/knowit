What is a ‘template horizon’ in drawing a geological cross-section?

START1234

A blank section: only the ground surface is known.

The template horizon is a key layer, like a particular sandstone or limestone, found more or less across the whole section.

You draw it first. Every other horizon is then built from it, so the layers stay continuous and consistent.

Drag the slider to build the section, step by step.

WHAT TO KEEPDraw one key layer first, and let it guide all the rest.
