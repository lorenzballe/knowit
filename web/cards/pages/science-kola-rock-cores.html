How much rock was actually collected from the Kola borehole?

Drag the Kola column to guess how many meters of rock cores came up.

3,700.1 m of cores

Oil wells usually core only the reservoir rock: tens of meters, a few hundred at most. Kola brought up 3,700.1 m, dozens of times more, and all of it had to be studied. It was one of the reasons the project lasted 25 years.
