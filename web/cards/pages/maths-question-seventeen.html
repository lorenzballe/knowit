ROLL THE COIN

Why did nobody answer question 17 correctly?

Q17: coin A has a third of coin B's radius and rolls once around B. How many times does A turn? The key said B: 3. Tap to roll.

4 turns, and 4 wasn't a choice.

Coin A turns 3 times to cover the length of B's edge, plus once more for going all the way around B. The test writers thought 3 too, so the correct answer wasn't on the test.

WHAT TO KEEPWhen everyone gets it wrong, check the answer key too.
