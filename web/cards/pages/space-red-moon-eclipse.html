LUNAR ECLIPSE

Why does the Moon turn red during a lunar eclipse?

Earth sits right between the Sun and the Moon. Tap each part to see what it does.

Bent, filtered sunlight.

In an eclipse no direct sunlight reaches the Moon. Earth's atmosphere bends sunlight into the shadow and filters it, letting through only the red. That red light is what paints the Moon.

Tap the Sun, the atmosphere and the Moon.

WHAT TO KEEPThe red Moon is lit by sunlight bent and filtered by Earth's air.
