TORQUE

What happens to the globe’s acceleration when you increase the applied force?

Drag back from the globe to wind up a push, then let go. Try three different forces.

Your push at the edge is a torque. The globe floats on water, so friction is tiny, and its resistance to spinning, I, never changes. So α = τ ÷ I: twice the push, twice the spin-up.

WHAT TO KEEPSame object, more torque, more angular acceleration: τ = Iα.
