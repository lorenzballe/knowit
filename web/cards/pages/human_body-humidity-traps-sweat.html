How does high humidity prevent sweat from cooling us?

DryHumidity 40%Muggy
Sweat evaporating
Heat trapped in body

Sweat cools you only when it evaporates. How easily it evaporates depends on how much vapor is already in the air.

In muggy air sweat stays liquid on the skin, and the body traps its heat.

Drag the humidity dial from dry to muggy and watch the sweat.

WHAT TO KEEPSame temperature, more humidity: less sweat evaporates, so you suffer more.
