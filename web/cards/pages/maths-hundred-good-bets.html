MANY BETS

Win $20 on heads, lose $10 on tails. What do 100 tosses bring?

$0Each line is one run of 100 bets

Tap “Toss 100” a few times and watch where each run lands.

One toss is pure luck: +$20 or −$10. But heads pays twice what tails costs, so each bet is worth +$5 on average. In 100 tosses heads comes up about 45 to 55 times, so every run lands near +$500. Losing any money at all: about 1 in 2300.

WHAT TO KEEPOne good bet is a gamble; a hundred of them is close to a sure win.
