NUCLEAR AFTERMATH

How long did the radiation linger in Nagasaki?

The bomb burst high in the air, held only a modest amount of nuclear material, and its isotopes were short-lived. A reactor like Chernobyl holds tonnes of uranium.

So 80% of the residual radiation was spent in the first day, and within a month both cities were near normal.
