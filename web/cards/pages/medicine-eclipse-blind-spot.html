SOLAR RETINOPATHY

A 20-year-old stared at the 2017 eclipse. How long until permanent damage?

Six seconds was enough. The eclipse burned the centre of her retina for good (solar retinopathy): a scotoma, a black spot right where she looks.

Drag the pin to how long you think she stared, then say how sure you are.
