SOFT ROBOTS

How does a growing robot push its way through sand?

Grow it without air. Then grow it with the air jet.

Air loosens the sand.

The robot blows a jet of air out of its front. That loosens the sand, so the sand pushes back much less. Then the robot just extends its tip and makes its way through.

WHAT TO KEEPLoosen the sand with air, and plain tip growth gets through.
