CENTRE OF MASS

Drop a hanging slinky. When does its centre of mass start falling?

The instant it's let go

Gravity is the only outside force on the slinky, and it was pulling all along; the hand was cancelling it. Let go and the red centre of mass drops on a curve at once, even while the bottom hangs still.

Pick one, then say how sure you are. The red dot is the centre of mass.

WHAT TO KEEPFor the centre of mass, only outside forces count; inside ones cancel.
