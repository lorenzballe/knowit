DRIVE THE HOIST

Why did the oxen lifting stone for Florence's cathedral dome never need turning round?

Drag round the ring to walk the oxen and lift the stone.

Because the hoist could reverse. Brunelleschi’s gearing could be shifted to wind the rope the other way, so oxen walking the same circle raised or lowered a load. Turning the team between lifts would have cost time on every load.

WHAT TO KEEPBefore speeding up the work, look at the time lost between jobs.
