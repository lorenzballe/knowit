How much of a pyramid's stone sits in its top third?

Top of height33%
Share of stone?

Only about 4%

A pyramid narrows in both width and depth as it rises, so the top third is a third as tall, wide and deep: under 4% of the stone. That's why levers are suspected for the top levels.
