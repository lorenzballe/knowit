DEEP SEA

How do animals survive in the deep, cold, food-poor trench?

At the bottom of the trench the water is very cold. Undersea volcanoes erupt and warm it, and the material they expel carries the essential elements abyssal species need to survive.

Tap the volcano three times to add each layer.
