TAP A LAYER

Heavy rain soaks a tilted, layered hillside. Which layer will it slide on?

Hint: think about where the water goes, not how weak the rock looks.

Tap a layer, then say how sure you are.

The clay: water stops on it.

The rock above the clay soaks up rain and gets heavier. The clay lets no water through, so water reaching it forms a slippery film. On a tilted slope, the layers above slide down on it.

WHAT TO KEEP A slope gives way where water stops, not where it gets in.
