MAKE IT RAIN

How can a thin layer of clay make a whole hillside slide?

Tap the sky to make it rain. Watch where the water goes.

Clay lets no water through, so the rain that sinks down stops on it and makes a slippery film. The rock above soaks up water and gets heavier. On tilted layers, it breaks off and slides downhill on the clay.

WHAT TO KEEPWet clay is a lubricant: the rock above it rides down the slope.
