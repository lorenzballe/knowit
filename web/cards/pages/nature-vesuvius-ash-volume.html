How much material did Vesuvius emit in the first ten days of 1944?

Turn the needle to your guess. The ash cube shows how big that is.

More than 245 million m³ in about ten days, some 280 m³ every second. Packed into one cube: about 626 m on each side. Ash and lapilli fell on Pompei, Nocera, Scafati and Poggiomarino.
