What drag coefficient does a projectile wrapped in air get underwater?

dropair shellracemeasure

The water-repelling sphere carries a shell of air, so it moves as a smooth torpedo of gas, not a solid. Researchers measured it in 2017 (Science Advances): a drag coefficient of only 0.02 to 0.03, ten times lower than the same shape made solid.
