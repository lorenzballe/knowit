Why is a radioactive atom far worse inside you than outside?

Drag the glowing atom down: breathe it in, all the way into a cell.

Outside, the dust's radiation spends itself on the air and your skin. Breathed in or eaten, the atom fires from right beside your cells, so its energy lands straight on their molecules, DNA included.

WHAT TO KEEPRadioactive dust does its damage once it is inside you, next to your cells.
