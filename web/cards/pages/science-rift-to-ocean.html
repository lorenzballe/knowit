How does a continent splitting in two make an ocean?

A crack the sea moved into.

The two halves keep drifting apart for millions of years, and the sea follows them in. Along the middle, a chain of volcanoes builds new sea floor. That is what makes it an ocean, not a sea, and why the Red Sea is one.

Turn the brass dial to run time forward, through millions of years.
