What colour is denim yarn at the moment it comes out of the indigo dye bath?

Hold your finger on the picture to lift the yarn out and keep it up. The clock runs thirty times fast.

Yellowish green. Indigo won’t dissolve in water, so dyers change it into a soluble yellow-green form that soaks into the cotton. In the air it turns back to blue, locking the dye in.

WHAT TO KEEPSome finished colours exist only after the air has had its turn.
