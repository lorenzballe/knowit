Why does Venezuela's extra-heavy oil need steam to come out of the ground?

SteamOFF
Pump running · oil too thick to move

Heat makes it flow.

Orinoco oil is so thick it can't move to the well on its own. High-pressure steam (or solvents) makes it more fluid, so it flows and can be pumped. That costs huge energy, constant maintenance and big investment, and much of the oil still stays put.

Pump is on, nothing comes up. Slide the steam up.
