HOLD TO BOIL

Why did Chernobyl's core suddenly make 10 times its normal power?

Steam set the neutrons free.

Liquid water soaked up neutrons; steam barely does. More steam meant more fissions, more heat, more steam. The surge broke the control rods, jamming them a third of the way in, and power hit 30 GW: 10 times nominal.

Hold the core to boil its water into steam. Watch the power.
