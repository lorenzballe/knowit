ASHGABAT

What symbol do Ashgabat's big marble buildings all share?

One star, eight points

All three of Ashgabat's landmark marble buildings, the Wedding Palace among them, carry the same eight-pointed star: the rub el hizb. It is a religious symbol and the symbol of the government at once.

This star sits on every landmark. Tap each of its points to count them.
