FRICTION

Why is there very little friction on the globe’s bottom?

A thin film of water

The globe is supported on a thin layer of water, not on its base. The water acts as a lubricant, so friction drops dramatically and a small push keeps it spinning.

Pick one, then watch the globe on its base.
