Why does a candle flame grow longer at 1.8 g?

0 g 1 g 1.8 g

Drag gravity from 0 g up to 1.8 g and watch the flame.

Hot air is lighter than the cool air around it, so gravity pulls the cool air down harder and the hot air gets pushed up. Make gravity almost twice as strong and that difference grows: the hot air rises faster and stretches the flame. In zero-g there is barely any lift, so the flame burns poorly and smokes.

WHAT TO KEEPFlames rise because gravity sinks cool air: more gravity, longer flame.
