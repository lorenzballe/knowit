What are the exact dimensions of a 'cage home'?

1.5 m²: barely a mattress

A cage home is a metal cage of 1.5 m², just big enough for a mattress. The cages are stacked on top of each other in tiny rooms, with one bathroom shared by about 20 people, and more than 220,000 people in Hong Kong live this way.
