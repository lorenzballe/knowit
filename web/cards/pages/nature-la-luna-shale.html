What Cretaceous rock is the main source of Venezuela's oil?

Organic-rich marine shale

120–90 million years ago, shallow, warm, life-filled seas covered much of northern Venezuela. Dead plankton, algae and microbes piled up on the seabed in thick, organic-rich layers: the La Luna formation, the oil's source rock.
