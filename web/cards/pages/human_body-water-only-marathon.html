SWEAT & SALT

Why is drinking only water dangerous for marathon runners?

Diluted: hyponatremia

Hours and hours of running cost water and lots of mineral salts in sweat. Water alone puts back only the water, so body fluids get too diluted: hyperhydration and hyponatremia. A one-hour jog doesn't need this.

The runner sweats every hour. At each station, pick the drink.

WHAT TO KEEPOn very long runs, put back the salts you sweat, not just the water.
