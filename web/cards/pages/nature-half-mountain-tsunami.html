TSUNAMI

What happens if half a mountain falls into the sea?

Pick what falls from Etna's flank.

Flip between the two and watch the far coast.

The rock shoves aside as much water as its own volume. A boulder moves a little; half a mountain pushes a huge mass of water at once, and it leaves as a system of tsunami waves that can reach distant coasts. How big it would be can't be known, but the risk is real.

WHAT TO KEEPWater pushed aside by a falling mass doesn't vanish: it travels as a tsunami.
