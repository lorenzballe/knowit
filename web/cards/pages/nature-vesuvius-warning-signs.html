How long before Vesuvius erupts do quakes and ground deformation begin?

Its steam vents become more visible months ahead. Drag the pin to when quakes and ground deformation start.

Steam vents show months ahead. Quakes and ground deformation, which signal the magma rising, come only in the last 1–2 weeks.
