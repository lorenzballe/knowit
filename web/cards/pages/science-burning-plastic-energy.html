RECYCLING

Does burning plastic give back the energy it takes to make new plastic?

No: it gives back less.

Burning plastic does release energy, but less than the energy spent making new plastic from fossil fuels. Burn it, and that plastic must be made all over again; recycle it well, and those fossil fuels are spared.
