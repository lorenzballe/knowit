How does IT Control keep a pan at an exact temperature?

Measure, then adjust

Temperature sensors on the coil read the heat all the time. The hob turns power down as it nears the setting and back up when it drops, so the temperature stays constant and food doesn't burn.

Heat the oil to 180°C: first with the sensor off, then on.
