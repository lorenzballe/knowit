How many countries have the death penalty for homosexuality?

Of the 62 countries that make homosexuality a crime, 7 set death by law, and in 5 more the law is unclear. Many never carry it out: according to ILGA, 4 do so regularly, including Iran, Saudi Arabia and Sudan.
