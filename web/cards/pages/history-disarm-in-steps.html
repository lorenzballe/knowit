GAME THEORY

From the late '80s, the US and USSR cut their nukes. Which plan did they use?

Tap the plan you would bet on.

Small steps, checked every year.

One deal to scrap everything would be a single prisoner's dilemma, with the trap of defecting at once. After building nukes from 1950 to 1986, the US and USSR instead cut a few each year, checked that both had cooperated, and repeated.

WHAT TO KEEP If one betrayal could cost everything, take steps small enough to check.
