POINT OF VIEW

One view: show what makes you odd, even if most people leave?

Drag to set how far you would open the door. Then commit.

Showing it swaps many maybes for fewer yeses.

For: hiding it puts no one off, but it never draws the people searching for exactly that, and they are the ones who stay.

Against: many traits push away far more people than they pull in, and plenty of big audiences are built on being easy to relate to.

ASK YOURSELF Is what I hide rare and wanted, or only rare?
