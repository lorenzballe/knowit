NAME ORIGIN

Who coined the name “Bermuda Triangle”, and when?

A newspaper, in 1964

Journalist Edward Van Winkle Jones kept claiming that strange things happened there, all from one mysterious cause. In ’64 another newspaper, citing Jones, introduced the name “Bermuda Triangle”, and the legend was born.
