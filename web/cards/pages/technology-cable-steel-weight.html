BRIDGE STEEL

How much steel is in one main cable of the Messina bridge?

13,000 tons

Each strand is 127 steel wires, each 5.40 mm thick. Many strands are bundled into one main cable, and all that thin steel adds up to 13,000 tons.

WHAT TO KEEPThin wires, bundled in huge numbers, make one 13,000-ton cable.
