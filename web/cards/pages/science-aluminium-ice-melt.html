HEAT FLOW

Why does ice melt faster on aluminium than on plastic?

NowLater

Drag time forward and watch where the heat flows.

Both plates are at room temperature, warmer than ice. Aluminium conducts heat much better, so it pours heat into the ice far faster. That same fast flow, out of your hand, is why it feels colder.

WHAT TO KEEP“Feels cold” means heat leaves you fast, not a lower temperature.
