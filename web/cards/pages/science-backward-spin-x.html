Press the X: which way does it spin against the tube?

Backwards

Pressing on the X makes it spin backwards relative to the tube's horizontal rotation. When the X faces up, that backward spin works against the tube's sweep, so the X moves slowly and you can see it. The other symbol moves fast and blurs.

WHAT TO KEEPBackward spin cancels the forward sweep, so the pressed symbol looks still.
