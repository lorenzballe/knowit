STEEL ATOMS

How do tiny carbon atoms make iron so much harder?

Carbon atoms are much smaller than iron atoms, so they fit inside the iron's crystal. Trapped there, they push the lattice outward and put the steel under stress: more carbon, harder and more rigid steel. The cost: it also turns brittle.
