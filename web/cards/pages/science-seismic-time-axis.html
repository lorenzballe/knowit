What does the vertical axis of a raw seismic section measure?

Time, not depth

Each wiggle is a wave that went down, bounced off a layer and came back up to be recorded. The section shows how long that took. Depths come later, through a time-to-depth conversion.

Pick what the “?” axis shows, then say how sure you are.

WHAT TO KEEPSeismic sections are in seconds of travel time, not metres.
