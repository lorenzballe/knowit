PICK ONE

A car and a growing robot meet sticky tape. Which one gets across?

It never rolls. It grows.

Compressed air pushes new skin out at the tip, so the part already lying on the tape never moves again: watch the stripes stay put. The car must pull its wheels off the glue, and the glue wins.

Pick who makes it over the tape, then say how sure you are.
