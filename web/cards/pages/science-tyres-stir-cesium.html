How can heavy vehicles reactivate old radiation in Belarus?

Chernobyl's Cesium-137 fell on the land in 1986 and, over decades, settled into the soil. Heavy tyres churn that soil up, and the cesium rides the dust back into the air. With the war with Russia, heavy vehicles have done exactly this in recent years.
