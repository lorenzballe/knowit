SPIN IT, LET GO

A spinning wheel held by one end floats instead of falling. Does it get lighter?

A 2 kg wheel hangs from a rope tied to one end of its axle. It isn't spinning. Let go of the other end.

WHAT TO KEEPThe weight's twist turns the spin sideways, so it swings round instead of tipping.
