GYROSCOPES

A spinning wheel hangs from one end of its axle. Where does it go?

Drag the arrow to where it goes once let go.

Round, not down

The weight's twist points sideways, at right angles to the spin arrow. So the arrow swings that way, without growing or dropping. The twist swings with it, so round it goes.

WHAT TO KEEPTwist a spinning wheel at right angles and it swings round, not down.
