Can grandparents' lives affect your DNA activity?

Yes, a little.

Marks on DNA turn genes up or down without changing the code. Some of your ancestors' marks get passed down, so their behaviour subtly tunes how your genes work.

Rub along the top strand to live Grandparent's life. Each stroke leaves a mark on her DNA.
