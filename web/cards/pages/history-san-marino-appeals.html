How did tiny San Marino dodge taxes from neighbouring feudal lords?

A neighbouring lord sends a tax bill: “this land is under my control.”

Lords used taxes as a way to say San Marino's land was theirs, so paying meant giving in. Instead the Sammarinese took each dispute to the court of a third power: even the Pope, and then the Holy Roman Empire. They won their autonomy in court, not on the battlefield.
