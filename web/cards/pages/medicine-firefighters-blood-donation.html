PFAS

Firefighters donate blood often for a year. What happens to their PFAS?

Drag your finger from “Today” to “1 year” to draw your guess.

Up to 30% lower in a year

Their gear and foams are laced with PFAS. Each blood or plasma donation (red drops) physically carries some of the chemicals out of the body. In a 2022 study, frequent donors cut their levels by up to 30% within a year.
