EARTH'S SPIN

How did the 2011 Japan earthquake change the way Earth spins?

Tap to shake Earth, then keep zooming in.

The magnitude 9 quake off Miyagi, Japan, was so violent that, according to NASA, it moved Earth's axis by 17 centimetres.

Earth has spun a tiny bit faster ever since: every day is now 1.8 microseconds shorter.
