GEM TEST

How does the condensation on a diamond react to heat?

Stone B: its fog vanished at once

On a real diamond, the fog from your breath disappears immediately. On a fake it stays for several seconds. Fakes now look very close to real gems, so this quick test helps.

Tap each stone to breathe on it. Watch the fog.
