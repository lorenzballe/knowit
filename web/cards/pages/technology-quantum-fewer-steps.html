What is the real advantage of a quantum computer?

Drag the dial to make the problem bigger. Both runners step at the same pace.

Not faster steps: far fewer of them.

Each quantum operation is no quicker. The number of operations needed to reach the result is exponentially smaller, but only for particular kinds of calculations and algorithms.

WHAT TO KEEPThe win is fewer operations, not faster ones, and only for some problems.
