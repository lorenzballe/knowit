SPOOF NUMBERS

What are “spoof” numbers, and what do they say about odd perfect numbers?

Descartes’ number looks perfect. Tap the dark piece, 22021, to lift its mask.

Treat 22021 as prime and σ(n) comes out exactly 2n: perfect. But 22021 is 19²·61, and the true σ(n) is too big. Near-misses like this are spoofs; an odd perfect number would be a spoof too, so a property that rules out spoofs would rule it out.
