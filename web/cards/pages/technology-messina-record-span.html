ONE SPAN

The Messina Bridge would hang in one span. How long, against today's record?

Drag the red tower out to where Messina's single span would end.

About 1.7 times the record

The longest single span today is about 1,900 m. To cross the Strait in one leap, Messina would need 3,300 m: more than 1 km longer.
