FIRE SAFETY

In a flashover, the whole room ignites. How fast can it happen?

As little as ten seconds

A flashover sets everything that can burn in the room alight at once. It can build in about ten seconds, which leaves no time to get out. It kills even experienced firefighters.

Guess how long it takes. Then watch the clock.

WHAT TO KEEPIn a flashover, escape time is counted in seconds, not minutes.
