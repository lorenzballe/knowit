RED SEA

Why is the Red Sea called red, when it looks blue?

Tap the sea six times to let the algae bloom.

Algae paint it pink.

In some periods of the year, especially summer, a tiny cyanobacterium called Trichodesmium blooms massively. Its reddish-pink mats float on the water and colour it. That is the most scientific explanation for the name.

WHAT TO KEEPThe Red Sea is named after algae that tint it reddish-pink.
