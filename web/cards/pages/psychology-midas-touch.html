THE MIDAS TOUCH

Does a light touch from your waiter change how much you tip?

Mississippi, 1984. The waitress hands back the change. Drag her hand onto the diner, or tap a receipt.

Waitresses who repeated the order word for word also got bigger tips (van Baaren, 2003).

WHAT TO KEEPTouched diners tipped more but rated the waitress no higher. Money moved; opinions didn’t.
Source · Crusco & Wetzel, Pers. Soc. Psychol. Bull. 1984 · Lynn, Le & Sherwyn, Cornell HRA Q. 1998 · Lynn, Int. J. Hosp. Manag. 2018
