How many expert eruption forecasts from 1990–2013 turned out correct?

Only 19%

A study checked what scientists had forecast for 190 eruptions between 1990 and 2013. Only about 1 forecast in 5 proved right. So today there is no single reliable way to say when a volcano will erupt.
