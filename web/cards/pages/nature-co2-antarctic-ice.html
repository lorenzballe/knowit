How did falling CO₂ give Antarctica its ice sheet?

CO₂ 1000 ppmWinter snow melts every summer

Less CO₂, a colder world, snow that stays.

About 32–33 million years ago CO₂ slid from about 1000 to 600 ppm. Less CO₂ trapped less heat, so Antarctic snow stopped melting in summer and piled up into an ice sheet. As CO₂ kept falling, the ice grew thicker.
