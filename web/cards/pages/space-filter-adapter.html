How did Apollo 13 fit square CO2 filters into round LEM slots?

CO2 is building up in the LEM. Drag the slider to turn the adapter's square end into a round one.

The LEM's own CO2 filters couldn't clean the air for three people over four days. The third stage's spares didn't fit its slots, so Mission Control improvised an adapter from the little the crew had on board.
