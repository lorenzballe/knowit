How did an ice age help close the Strait of Gibraltar?

Less water, less weight: the land rises

Growing ice sheets pulled water out of the sea, so sea level fell. With less water pressing down, the ground at Gibraltar rose like an unloaded buoy. With tectonics, that sealed the strait and the Mediterranean dried out.
