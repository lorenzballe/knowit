TINY MAGNETS

Why do the hydrogen protons in your body act like magnets in an MRI?

You are mostly water · H₂O · every hydrogen nucleus holds 1 proton

Drag the magnet around the proton and watch it turn.

The proton in each hydrogen nucleus spins on its own axis and behaves like a tiny magnet, with a north and a south end.

So an outside magnetic field can grab it and turn it, and that is what an MRI machine works with.

WHAT TO KEEPEach hydrogen proton is a spinning mini‑magnet, so magnetic fields can steer it.
