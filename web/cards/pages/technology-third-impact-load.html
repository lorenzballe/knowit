THREE LOADS

Besides gravity and wind, what else did the Twin Towers have to resist?

Drag the plane into the tower and watch the forces.

A sudden, dynamic blow

Weight presses down and wind pushes sideways, steadily. An aircraft impact is a third load that arrives all at once. The towers were meant to take the hit of a Boeing 707, the largest airliner of the 1960s and 70s.
