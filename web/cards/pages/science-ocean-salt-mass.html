SEA SALT

How much sodium chloride is dissolved in the world’s oceans?

≈ 4 × 10¹⁹ kg

That is 40 billion billion kilograms: 40 × 1,000,000,000 × 1,000,000,000. Sodium in nature is not a shiny metal: it sits dissolved in seas and oceans as sodium chloride, salt.
