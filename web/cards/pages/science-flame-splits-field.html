FLAME IONS

Why does a candle flame split in two between charged plates?

Burning makes charged bits in the flame: ions. The field pulls positive ions toward the negative plate and negative ions toward the positive plate, so the flame spreads into two wings, like a butterfly.

Switch on the field, then tap each half of the flame.

WHAT TO KEEPA flame carries electric charges, so an electric field can pull it apart.
