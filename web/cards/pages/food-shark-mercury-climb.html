The shark. The mercury ends up at the top.

A big fish eats a smaller one, which ate a smaller one still, which ate a mollusc. The methylmercury builds up along the chain and the biggest fish ends up holding it. In high doses it can cause problems, above all during development: for children and in pregnancy. It is still fish, with benefits too.
