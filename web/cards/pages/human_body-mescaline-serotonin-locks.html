Which brain receptors does mescaline, the peyote molecule, mainly lock onto?

Serotonin's 5-HT2A and 5-HT2C

Mescaline mostly binds to receptors meant for serotonin, a messenger already in your brain, known as the "happiness neurotransmitter". It slips into those locks, chiefly 5-HT2A and 5-HT2C.
