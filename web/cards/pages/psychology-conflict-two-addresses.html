POINT OF VIEW

One view: saying no hurts less than quietly putting up with it. Agree?

Drag yourself to the side you'd take. The jagged line is the conflict: watch where it goes.

One conflict, two addresses.

For the view: a no is one hard talk with someone who can answer back; a swallowed yes argues with you again every time it comes back.

Against it: a no can cost a job, a friend or your safety, and some of that never comes back. Weigh the shaded areas, not the peaks.

ASK YOURSELF Of the two costs, which could I still undo in a year: the hard talk, or the quiet yes?
