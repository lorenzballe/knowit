How did the aircraft's weight contribute to the towers' initial survival?

Columns cut

Each plane weighed about 130,000 kg. Each tower weighed about 450,000 tonnes, almost ten times the Titanic. The plane disintegrated and the structure absorbed the blow.

The structure was redundant, so the load of the broken columns moved onto the intact ones. That is why each tower stood for about an hour.
