SCIENTIFIC METHOD

How does science decide a theory is worth trusting?

Yeses are light. Surviving a “no” is heavy.

A pile of cases that fit proves little: the next one could still say no. Science sets out to disprove its theories, and only when it can’t do we say it’s getting at something true.

Pile up yeses and watch the scale. Then try to break the theory.

WHAT TO KEEPIf you think something is true, try as hard as you can to disprove it.
