What is the “atomic shadow”?

An outline burned in by the flash

The ground hit about 4,000 °C in an instant, enough to melt granite. People within half a kilometre were vaporized, but for that instant their bodies blocked the heat, so their outline stayed printed on concrete or asphalt.

Drag the person along the wall, then press Flash.

WHAT TO KEEPThe shadow is the person: the only trace the flash left.
