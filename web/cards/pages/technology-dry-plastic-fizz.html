Why is the plastic dried before it's melted into Coke bottles?

Wetter plastic, less fizz

Water in the melt makes the plastic runnier: its viscosity drops. A bottle from runnier plastic holds less CO₂, and a fizzy drink needs its CO₂ kept in. So the plastic is dried first.
