Why did the Great Powers make Switzerland neutral forever in 1815?

Tap Switzerland to make it neutral, and watch where the armies go.

A cushion between rivals

French, Germans and Austrians often fought each other. A neutral Switzerland covered one side of each one's land, so each could mass its forces on a shorter border.
