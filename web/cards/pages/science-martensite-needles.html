What happens inside steel when a weld cools fast?

Drag the thermometer down: cool the hot weld fast.

Martensite. Cooling fast traps the carbon atoms in the iron lattice, and it locks into needles. That steel is really hard, but the lattice can no longer absorb energy, so a blow cracks it.

WHAT TO KEEPFast-cooled steel traps carbon: hard, but brittle.
