Which ocean once split Gondwana from Laurasia, 200 million years ago?

With a finger, draw where an ocean arm cut between the two blocks.

The Tethys Ocean

200 million years ago an arm of ocean wedged in between Gondwana and Laurasia: geologists call it the Tethys. When the two blocks collided, it closed.

WHAT TO KEEPGondwana and Laurasia were split by the Tethys, an ocean arm that closed when they collided.
