ICE & PRESSURE

A weighted guitar string sinks through ice. What is left behind?

One whole block

Under the thin wire the pressure turns ice into water, so the wire sinks. Behind it the pressure is gone, so the water freezes again. This is regelation, and it is why the thinnest string, the high E, was used.
