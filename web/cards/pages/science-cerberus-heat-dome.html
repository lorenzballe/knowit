What is the Cerberus anticyclone, and how does it make a heat wave?

Air sinks.

Cerberus is a subtropical anticyclone from Africa: a dome of high pressure that pushes air down. Sinking air can't build clouds, so the sun heats the ground hard, and the dome stays: a long heat wave. Not El Niño.

Cerberus moves north. Under it, which way does the air go?
