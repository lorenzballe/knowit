BRITTLE STEEL

Why did the Titanic's steel hull crack so quickly?

Too much sulfur.

Tests on a piece of the wreck's hull found steel very rich in sulfur. Sulfur and iron form ferrous sulfide crystals, and under stress they seed micro‑cracks that join up. So under the impact, in cold water, the stiff hull cracked instead of bending.
