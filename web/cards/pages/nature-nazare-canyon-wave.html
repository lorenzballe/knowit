How does the Nazaré canyon make a wave grow so tall?

Water under the crest
Wave height

Slide to push the wave out of the deep canyon toward the coast.

Over the deep canyon, the wave's circling motion fades before it ever touches the floor. Near Nazaré the floor rises fast: the circles hit bottom, the water column is squeezed, and the wave has nowhere to go but up.

WHAT TO KEEPLess water under a wave squeezes it upward: the crest grows taller.
