Why does Sardinia have so few earthquakes, unlike the rest of Italy?

Pick a region to look underground.

The Apennines are an active zone full of active faults: when they slip, the ground shakes. Sardinia is a stable continental block, with no active faults and no plates colliding, so it barely moves.

Flip the switch between the two and compare.

WHAT TO KEEPEarthquakes need active faults: no active faults, almost no quakes.
