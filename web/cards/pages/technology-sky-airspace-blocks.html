AIR TRAFFIC

How is the sky organized for air traffic control?

1 block · 1 controller · all 12 planes to watch

Flip the switch to slice the sky, and watch who looks after each plane.

The sky is split into horizontal and vertical blocks, a 3D grid. Each block is the job of one or more controllers at the area control center, who follow it on radar. Two planes over the same spot can sit in different blocks.

WHAT TO KEEPThe sky is a 3D grid of blocks, each with its own controllers.
