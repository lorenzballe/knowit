TRINITY BOMB

How did a 7‑gram pellet start the Trinity bomb’s chain reaction?

A neutron gun, armed by the blast

The urchin kept polonium and beryllium apart with a layer of nickel and gold. The implosion’s shockwave mixed them, and polonium’s alpha particles made the beryllium release a flood of neutrons. Those neutrons kick‑started the chain reaction.
