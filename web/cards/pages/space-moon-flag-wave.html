MOON LANDING

Why does the flag seem to wave on the airless Moon?

No wind. Just the push of planting.

Driving the pole into the lunar soil shakes it, and the shake passes into the cloth. With no air to brake it, the fabric keeps swinging, slowly and freely, long after the hands let go.

Plant the pole on the Moon. Time how long the flag keeps moving.
