TEMPERED GLASS

Red-hot glass is cooled in an instant. Its skin ends up squeezed or stretched?

Drag the slice sideways: left to squeeze the skin, right to stretch it.

Squeezed, for good.

Cooled in an instant from about 700 °C, the outer surface contracts and locks in compression. That raises its resistance to mechanical stress and thermal shock.

WHAT TO KEEP Tempered glass is tougher because its skin is locked in a squeeze.
