How does thermal tempering make glass stronger?

A pane of glass under a press. Press and hold the picture to load it, ordinary glass first.

About 6–7× stronger

Heated to about 700 °C, then cooled in an instant, the surface locks in a squeeze. Any knock or sudden heat must undo that squeeze before the glass gives way.

WHAT TO KEEPTempered glass is tougher because its surface is born squeezed.
