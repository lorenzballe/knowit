What convention did Einstein adopt for clock synchronization?

Flip the switch. Watch what changes at B, and what A sees.

Same 10 ticks at A either way. Timing the trip to B alone needs B's clock already in sync, the very thing being set. So Einstein simply stipulated equal halves: a definition, not a measurement.

WHAT TO KEEPLight's speed is the same both ways by Einstein's choice; only the round trip is measured.
