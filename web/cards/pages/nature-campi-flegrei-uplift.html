How much did the ground rise in two years after the 1538 eruption?

Almost 20 m in 2 years

When Monte Nuovo erupted in 1538, the Campi Flegrei ground rose almost 20 metres in two years. Since 2016 it has risen about 80 cm: real, but some 25 times smaller.

Tap both buttons to lift the ground. The little figure is a person, for scale.
