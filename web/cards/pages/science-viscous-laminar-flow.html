Why does honey pour in smooth layers while water turns turbulent?

slowfast

Honey wins: it stays smooth.

The Reynolds number weighs speed, density and pipe size against viscosity. Keep those three the same and raise the viscosity: the number drops, so the flow stays laminar instead of turning turbulent.

Push the speed up. Same pipe, same speed, two fluids.

WHAT TO KEEPMore viscous means a lower Reynolds number: smooth, parallel layers.
