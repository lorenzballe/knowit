Why are stratovolcanoes more explosive than shield volcanoes?

Sticky magma traps the gas.

A shield volcano's runny magma lets gas slip away, so lava flows calmly. A stratovolcano's thick magma keeps it in, and trapped gas means pressure, released all at once.

WHAT TO KEEPThick magma traps gas; trapped gas becomes pressure; pressure explodes.
