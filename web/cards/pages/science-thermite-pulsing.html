THERMITE

How does a thermite flame front cross a bed of powder?

In bursts: boom, pause, boom.

Likely the air trapped between grains heats, expands and blasts up, pushing unburnt powder away; or the two kinds of grain must meet in the right ratio. People made thermite for 100 years before anyone noticed.

Pick how the front moves. Then light it.

WHAT TO KEEPThermite doesn't flow: it builds up, bursts ahead, and pauses again.
