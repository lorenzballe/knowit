TORQUE

How does pushing a globe make it spin like Earth?

Your push lands away from the axis, so it makes a torque: a twist. Torque gives angular acceleration, so every push adds spin. Earth turns the same way, from a torque of its own.

Tap the globe to push it. Watch the spin build up.
