BRIDGES

Why couldn’t a gentler ramp be built on Eshima Island?

Height is fixed. Land is not there.

The deck must stay high so ships pass underneath without stopping. Eshima Island is too small for a longer, gentler ramp, so the bridge has to climb that height in a short run: steeply.

Make the ramp gentler: drag its foot outward, or the deck down.

WHAT TO KEEPFixed height + little land = a steep slope.
