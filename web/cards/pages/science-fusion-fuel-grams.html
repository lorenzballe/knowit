FUSION FUEL

How much fuel would fusion need to power one person for ten years?

Drag the slider from water to energy.

Every 50 litres of ordinary water hide 1.6 grams of deuterium. With 5 grams of lithium-6 it could give about 380,000 kWh: ten years of one person's energy. And there is no chain reaction: run out of fuel and it just stops, like a petrol engine.

WHAT TO KEEPFusion fuel is counted in grams: a few bottles of water could power a decade.
