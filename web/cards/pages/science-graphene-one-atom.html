GRAPHENE

What is the thickness of a graphene sheet?

One atom thick

Pencil graphite is carbon sheets piled up, so it is three-dimensional. Keep just one sheet and you have graphene: a two-dimensional layer of carbon only one atom thick. It was discovered in 2004.

Tap to peel the carbon sheets off, one at a time.
