What is the main early warning sign of a tsunami?

CalmSea retreatsWave

Drag the slider: watch the sea in the minutes before the wave.

Before the first wave arrives, the sea suddenly pulls back. The seabed is exposed and boats are left stranded. It is practically the only real advance warning, and it comes about five minutes before the wave.

WHAT TO KEEPIf the sea suddenly retreats, leave the beach: the wave is about five minutes away.
