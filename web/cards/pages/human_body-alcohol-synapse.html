MORE OR LESS?

Alcohol hits four brain messengers. Which does it turn up, which down?

Alcohol slows your thoughts. Tap each: after a drink, more of it or less?

Two up, two down: the brain's messages slow.

Together, the four changes slow the signals between neurons: you sense less, tension drops, you feel euphoric and your memory slips.

WHAT TO KEEP Feeling looser is not a faster brain: alcohol frees you by slowing it down.
