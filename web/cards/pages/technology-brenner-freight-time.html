BRENNER TUNNEL

How much faster will freight trains cross the Brenner through the new tunnel?

0105 min

105 → 35 minutes

The Brenner Base Tunnel will link Fortezza in Italy with Innsbruck in Austria. Freight trains save 70 minutes, two-thirds of the trip, which cuts costs. Passengers drop from 80 to 25 minutes.
