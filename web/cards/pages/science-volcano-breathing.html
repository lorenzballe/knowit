VOLCANOES

What slowly lifts and sinks the ground at Campi Flegrei?

Fluids and magma below

This is bradisismo: magma and fluids push the ground up, then it sinks back as they ease. Slow and cyclic, like the volcano breathing. In the 1970s and 80s it was so intense that quakes shook Pozzuoli and whole areas were evacuated.

Watch the coast rise and sink. Pick the cause, then say how sure.
