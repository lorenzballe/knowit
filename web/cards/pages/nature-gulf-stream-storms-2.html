BERMUDA TRIANGLE

Why does the Gulf Stream contribute to accidents in the Bermuda Triangle?

Warm water makes wild weather.

The Gulf Stream carries warm surface water, about 26 °C, right through the triangle. That heat fuels strong storms that change fast, shaking ships and aircraft with turbulence.
