Why is plastic cheaper than glass to produce?

Step 1 · Make one bottle of each

MAKEWEIGHSHIP

Drag the slider: follow one bottle from factory to shop.

Making a plastic bottle saves only about one cent. The real saving is on the road: plastic is far lighter, so moving it costs less, and pollutes a bit less too.

WHAT TO KEEPPlastic's price edge is its light weight, not its making.
