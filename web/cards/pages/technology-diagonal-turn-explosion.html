How did diagonals change the maze's complexity?

Drag the mouse right to give it more moves, up to 6.

On square hallways a mouse has few choices at each step. Diagonals add new headings, so every move offers more turns, and each extra move multiplies them: the total opens up exponentially. More paths to weigh, but runs become one fluid, snaking motion.
