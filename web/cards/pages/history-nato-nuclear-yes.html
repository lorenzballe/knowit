Who decides if the nuclear bombs stored in Italy can be used?

The NATO council

NATO is a defensive alliance, so the bombs could only be used to defend a member under attack. To use one from Aviano, say for Poland, NATO would not ask Rome’s permission but the Alliance’s council, where Italy is one seat.
