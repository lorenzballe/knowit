POINT OF VIEW

One view: does feeling lucky set you up for a harder fall?

Drag the walker to where you stand on the rope. Then say how sure you are.

Same luck. The net decides the fall.

For the view: when luck seems to do the work, you stop checking, so the first real blow lands from full height. Against it: people who expect good things often try more and get up faster. The risk is not hope. It is hope that replaces the net.

ASK YOURSELF What am I not checking, because I expect it to go well?
