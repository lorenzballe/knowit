CRIMEA 1954

Why did Russia call Crimea “theirs” after giving it away?

In 1954 the gift was symbolic: Ukraine and Russia were in the same Union. When the Union dissolved, it became a real loss of the peninsula that controls the Black Sea. Russia always meant to get it back, and took it in 2014.
