How does thermite's heat make a hard drive's data unrecoverable?

Pick one. Then say how sure you are.

Past the Curie temperature, the magnetism goes.

Past a certain temperature, the Curie temperature, magnets lose their magnetism. So information stored on a magnetic hard drive heated that far becomes unrecoverable.

WHAT TO KEEP Data on a magnetic drive lasts only as long as its magnetism.
