Why can we still detect radiation at Chernobyl today?

19862020

Drag the years from 1986 to 2020. Watch the Geiger counter.

The short-lived atoms burned out fast. Cesium-137 and strontium-90 last for decades, and they are still in the soil, so the counter still clicks. Take them out and it goes quiet.

WHAT TO KEEPContamination lasts as long as its longest-lived atoms.
