LAB GEMS

Why can a magnet catch some fake diamonds but not real ones?

Metal inside

Many lab diamonds are made by HPHT: high pressure and high temperature, with metals added to make it work. That metal makes the stone magnetic, so it jumps to the magnet. A real diamond stays put.
