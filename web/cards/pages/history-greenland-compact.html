Instead of buying Greenland, what deal might Trump offer it?

A compact, like those of the Marshall Islands or Palau, gives formal independence. The US runs defense and pays economic aid, and in exchange gets exclusive rights over the territory.

Try both deals and watch who ends up holding what.
