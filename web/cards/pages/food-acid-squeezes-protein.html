How does an acid like fluoroantimonic acid “cook” meat?

Denatured.

Heat and acid do the same thing to a protein: they squeeze it. Chemists call it denaturing. A squeezed protein loses its juices, which is why overcooked meat, like meat in acid, turns dry and stringy.

Tap heat or acid to squeeze the protein, one step at a time.

WHAT TO KEEPAn acid “cooks” meat as heat does: it wrings the proteins dry.
