TAP TO CLAP

Why can a huge room with no echoes sound as small as a box?

Clap in this big stone hall, then line its walls. Tap a lining and watch, or hear, how long each clap rings.

WHAT TO KEEPYour ears size a room by its echoes. Take them away and the room shrinks.
Source · Kolarik et al., Perception (2021), a test of 15 blindfolded listeners in virtual rooms
