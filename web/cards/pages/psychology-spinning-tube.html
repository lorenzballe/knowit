Spin a labelled tube: why do you only see the side you press?

The spinning tube is a series of quick images, and your brain stitches them into one continuous picture. The side under your finger is the one you keep looking at, so that is the image your brain shows you.

Press one side and watch the tube spin.

WHAT TO KEEPWhat you see is a stitch of quick images, not the object itself.
