SLINKY DROP

Drop a hanging slinky. Why does its bottom end wait?

Tension still balances gravity

Gravity pulls the bottom down, tension pulls it up: equal and opposite, so it doesn’t move. It falls only when the news that the tension changed reaches it, and that news takes time to travel down the coils.
