Can a single 8.6% beer get you arrested for driving in Italy?

1.09 g/L: more than double the limit

One 44 cl beer at 8.6%, and 15 minutes later the professional breathalyzer read 1.09 g/L. The limit is 0.5, so getting behind the wheel then could mean arrest.
