How did engineers find the right rock under the Channel without drilling?

Your line: close.

Sound pulses fired from the surface bounce back from every boundary between rock formations. Timing the echoes maps structures kilometres deep, so engineers traced the continuous chalk formation for the tunnel without drilling.
