COIN PARADOX

Roll a coin around an identical coin. How many times does it turn?

Drag the pin along the scale to your guess. Then say how sure you are.

Two turns, not one.

The rolling coin's centre travels a circle twice as wide as the coin, so it covers two of its own circumferences. Halfway round it is already upright. Drag the coin to roll it yourself.

WHAT TO KEEPRolling around a circle, not along a line, adds one extra turn.
