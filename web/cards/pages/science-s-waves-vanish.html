EARTH'S CORE

Why do S-waves vanish 2,900 km below our feet?

Below 2,900 km, it's liquid.

S-waves can't travel through liquids. Since they vanish at 2,900 km, a liquid layer must begin there: the outer core. That liquid is also why P-waves suddenly slow down.

Send each wave down from a quake and watch what happens at 2,900 km.
