RECYCLING

Why is burning plastic for energy a mistake?

Burning plastic gives back some energy, but less than it costs to make new plastic, and its carbon goes up as CO₂.

Recycling keeps the plastic as plastic, and remaking it takes far less fossil fuel than making it new.
