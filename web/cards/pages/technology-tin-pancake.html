HIGH-SPEED CAMERA

To make the light that prints chips, a laser hits each tin drop twice, microseconds apart. Why not once?

A drop of molten tin, 25 µm across, flies at 70 m/s. Two pulses of an infrared laser wait for it. Drag the clock.

WHAT TO KEEPA weak pulse shapes the target, a strong one fires: 50,000 times a second.
