What must underground water be like to grow crystals in rock cavities?

It must be supersaturated.

Water underground is often full of salts. When it carries more than it can hold and reaches a cavity, the extra comes out as crystals.

Drag up or down to set the salt in the water, then let it in.

WHAT TO KEEPCrystals grow from the salt the water can't hold.
