TWIN LANDSLIDES

How did two landslides make one giant tsunami?

Not two waves: one, made stronger.

The first slide plunged into the bay and raised a wave. The second, farther away, struck the glacier and made no new wave: it added its force to the first. Two slides summed their destructive power.
