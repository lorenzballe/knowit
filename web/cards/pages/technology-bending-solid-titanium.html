How can a solid piece of titanium bend 180 degrees?

Drag the knob: bend the titanium all the way left, then all the way right.

No hinge. No alloy. Just shape.

It is one piece of 3D-printed titanium. It is designed with thin, flexible beams, so the solid metal bends ±90°: 180° of motion, then springs back.

WHAT TO KEEPThe right shape can make even solid metal bend.
