KOLA WELL

Why did drilling the Kola hole get much slower after 7 km?

It switched to coring

The drill stopped smashing the rock to go down. Instead it cut out intact rock cylinders, 6 cm across, to bring them up whole. That care is slow: the next 2 km took three more years.

Pick one, then watch both drills at work.

WHAT TO KEEPTaking rock out whole costs far more time than smashing through it.
