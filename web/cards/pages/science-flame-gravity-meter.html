How can a candle flame tell you how strong gravity is?

0 g · float1 g · Earth2 g

A flame heats the air around it, and hot air is lighter. Gravity pulls the cooler, heavier air down underneath and shoves the hot gas up: stronger gravity, a taller, thinner, flickering flame. With no gravity there is no up, so the flame stays a dim blue ball.

Slide your gravity until your flame looks like the mystery one.
