What kind of place was the Adria microplate 220 million years ago?

A sea floor.

About 220 million years ago Adria, a microplate wedged between Africa and Europe, lay under the Tethys sea. Thick carbonate sediment piled up on it and hardened into Triassic limestone and dolomite, the rock now found high in the Alps and Apennines.
