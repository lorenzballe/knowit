Why can the XM42 flamethrower be bought in U.S. gun stores?

The law files it as a hobby tool.

The XM42 is classed as a non-lethal incendiary device and sold under hobbyist rules, unlike fully military-grade weapons.

WHAT TO KEEP What you may buy depends on how the law files it, not on how fierce it looks.
