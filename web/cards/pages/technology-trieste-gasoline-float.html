Why did the Trieste carry 85,000 litres of gasoline just to float?

0 L gasolineSINKS

Gasoline is lighter than seawater, litre for litre. Swap 85,000 L of water for it, plus the two air tanks, and the float pushes up harder than the steel pulls down.

Drag to swap seawater for gasoline in the float, and watch.

WHAT TO KEEPAnything lighter than water, held in a tank, lifts what it’s tied to.
