FORCED LABOUR

Prisoners made shoes and bags. Who got the money from their work?

The director

Prisoners were forced to work in the fields and to make shoes and bags. Almost all the money went neither to the state nor to the prisoners, but into the director's own pockets.

Pick the bin the money fell into. Then say how sure you are.
