Why can't Saudi and Emirati pipelines replace the oil flowing through Hormuz?

Saudi Arabia's pipeline to the Red Sea and the Emirates' pipeline to the Indian Ocean are real. But together they carry only 2–3 million barrels a day, against the 20 million that normally pass through Hormuz.
