STEREO VINYL

How can one single groove carry both left and right stereo sound?

The groove, cut open: a V. The needle's tip touches both walls.

The groove's two walls slope at about 45°, at right angles to each other: one wall carries the left channel, the other the right.

Each wall pushes the needle along its own diagonal, so the pickup can pull the two apart. Same sound in both swings it side to side; differences make it rise and fall.

Play each channel and watch which way the needle moves.

WHAT TO KEEPOne V-groove, two walls at 45°: one wall per stereo channel.
