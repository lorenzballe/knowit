VOLCANOES

Why can a flat caldera out-blast the tallest explosive volcano?

A super caldera barely shows on the surface, so it hides its power. Beneath it sits a huge magma chamber.

When it erupts, the ground above collapses into the emptying chamber and it all blows out at once: energy orders of magnitude beyond an ultra-plinian eruption.

Erupt the tall volcano first, then the flat one.

WHAT TO KEEPViolence is set by the chamber below, not the mountain above.
