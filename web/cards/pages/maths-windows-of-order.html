LOGISTIC MAP

Past chaos, does the logistic map ever find order again?

Tap the diagram to pick an R, then race it against R = 3.83.

Past about 3.57 the map is chaotic, but not everywhere. At R = 3.83 it locks into a stable 3-year cycle, which then splits into 6, 12, 24… before chaos returns. Islands like this hide cycles of every length.
