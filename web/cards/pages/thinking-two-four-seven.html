CONFIRMATION BIAS

Why does guessing 2, 4, 7 crack the rule faster than 3, 6, 12?

The hidden rule is just “numbers in ascending order”. Every doubling triple also goes up, so its “yes” can never tell ×2 apart from the real rule.

2, 4, 7 is the test where ×2 says no — and the rule still says yes. Your guess breaks.

2, 4, 8 follows the rule. You guess “×2”. Tap a triple to test it.

WHAT TO KEEPTest what could prove you wrong, not what agrees with you.
