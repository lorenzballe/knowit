VAJONT 1963

How high did the Vajont lake rise before Monte Toc gave way?

Up to 710 m

The lake behind the dam was raised to 710 m while the slope of Monte Toc above it was creeping 2 cm a day. It was brought back to a safe level on 26 September, but too late: on 9 October, 270 million m³ of rock broke away.
