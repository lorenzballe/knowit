SIZE MATTERS

Teflon pans are safe. The chemicals used to make them aren't. Why?

Size decides.

Teflon is tens of thousands of atoms long: too big to absorb, so it's flushed out. PFOA is 5 to 10 carbons: it slips into the blood, and its acid end grabs proteins that carry it anywhere.

Bet: which one can get into your blood? Then say how sure you are.

WHAT TO KEEPNot the material but the size: giant chains pass through, small ones get in.
