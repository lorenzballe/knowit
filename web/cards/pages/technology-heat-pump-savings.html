HEAT PUMPS

How much can a home heat pump cut energy use?

About 60% less

Both homes got the same 10 units of heat. The heater makes every unit from electricity. The pump only spends energy to move heat already in the ground, so it buys about 4.

Guess first: how much less energy does the heat pump use?

WHAT TO KEEPMoving heat costs far less than making it: up to 60% less energy.
