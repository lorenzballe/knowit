DILUTE IT

Diluted 500 times, how radioactive is Fukushima's tank water?

The tank water holds 700,000 Bq per litre, 70 times the WHO drinking limit. Tap to pour in clean water, and count the pours it takes to get under the limit.

The tritium doesn't vanish: each pour spreads it through more litres. At ×500, 700,000 Bq per litre becomes 1,500, six times under the WHO's 10,000 for drinking water. About 50 of your pours.
