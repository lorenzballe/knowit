DRAG YOUR GUESS

700,000 Bq/L, diluted 500 times. Where does it land?

Drag the drop to your guess, then say how sure.

Six times under the limit.

 

“Seventy times the limit” sounds alarming. But ÷500 is a longer step than ×70, so 700,000 Bq/L lands at about 1,500, under the WHO’s 10,000 for drinking water.

WHAT TO KEEP Hear “X times too much”? Ask how many times it will be divided.
