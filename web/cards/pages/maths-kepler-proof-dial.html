SWING THE DIAL

Kepler said in 1611 how to stack spheres best. How long until proof?

Swing the needle from 1611 to the year you think it was finally proven.

About 400 years. In 1611 Kepler stated that stacking spheres like supermarket oranges fills about 74% of space, the best possible, but he never proved it. The formal proof was only published in 2017.

WHAT TO KEEPAn answer can look obvious and still take centuries to prove.
