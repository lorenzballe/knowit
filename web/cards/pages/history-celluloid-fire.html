FIRST PLASTIC

Why was celluloid, the first plastic, so dangerous to make?

Extremely flammable

In 1863 the American inventor John Wesley Hyatt created celluloid, the first ancestor of plastic. It was revolutionary, but it caught fire so easily that making it was very risky.
