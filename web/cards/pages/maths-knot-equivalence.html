SAME KNOT?

What is the central, unsolved problem in knot theory?

?

Same knot.

Knot B was Knot A, only moved around. But no systematic method was known to tell two knots apart, and Alan Turing wrote that it might well be unsolvable.

Is Knot B the same knot as Knot A? Bet, then say how sure.

WHAT TO KEEPTelling if two knots are the same has driven knot theory for over 150 years.
