INERTIA

Why is a parked car so hard to push on a level road?

Same push, but the car barely creeps. A car has a lot of inertia: it resists any change in its motion, so getting it rolling takes a big force. On a level road you are not lifting it. The road holds its weight.

Hold PUSH to shove the parked car.

WHAT TO KEEPWeight makes a thing hard to lift; inertia makes it hard to push.
