Why do periodic crystals never have five-fold symmetry?

One point on a floor. 360° to fill around it.

108° doesn't go into 360°.

Triangles, squares and hexagons close around a point exactly. Pentagons always leave a gap or overlap, so no repeating crystal can be built from five-fold symmetry. Only quasicrystals, whose pattern never repeats, have it.
