NEGATIVE EVIDENCE

Why does a “no” teach you more than a “yes”?

Eight guesses all fit 2 · 4 · 6. Which survive?

Tests that fit your guess come back “yes”, but almost every other rule says yes too, so you learn little. One “no” wipes out a whole family of rules at once. That is the scientific method: try to disprove your idea.
