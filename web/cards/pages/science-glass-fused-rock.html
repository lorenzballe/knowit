VOLCANIC GLASS

What is glass, scientifically?

Here is molten rock. Cool it slowly, or plunge it into water.

Cooled slowly, the atoms have time to line up into crystals: minerals. Quenched in water, the melt freezes before they can, stuck in a jumble called an amorphous structure. That is glass, like volcanic obsidian.

WHAT TO KEEPGlass is rock melted and cooled too fast to grow any crystals.
