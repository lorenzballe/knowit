RECORD WHEEL

Ashgabat has a record-breaking Ferris wheel. What record does it hold?

dark cabin height2.9 m

47.6 m tall, all under a roof

This wheel doesn't turn in the open air: the whole thing sits inside a building, beneath a glass dome. At 47.6 m, it is the tallest covered Ferris wheel in the world.

Hold the button and ride the dark cabin to the top.
