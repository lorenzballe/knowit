Why do closed-loop geothermal systems not exchange fluids with groundwater?

Water swapped with the aquifer: 0 L · laps: 0

It never opens.

The pipe is one sealed loop: the same fluid goes down cold, picks up the rock's heat and comes back up to the heat pump. No water is drawn from the aquifer and none is put back, so there is nothing to contaminate it with.
