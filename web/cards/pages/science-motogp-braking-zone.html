HOLD TO BRAKE

How many metres does a MotoGP bike need from 350 to 100 km/h?

Real bikes need about 300 m: at Mugello's first corner Brembo measures 288 m from over 350 to 90 km/h, five seconds on the brakes. Rossi likens it to lifting your maximum in the gym.

Tap Start, then hold Brake to reach the corner at 100 km/h.
