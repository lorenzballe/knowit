How many metres does a MotoGP bike need from 350 to 100 km/h?

SPEED 350 km/hTO CORNER 420 m

Red dashes: Rossi's way. At 350 km/h he grabs the brakes about 200 m from the corner and falls to 100. Losing 250 km/h in that little road means the squeeze must be exact.

Tap Start, then hold Brake to reach the corner at 100 km/h.

WHAT TO KEEPFrom 350 to 100 km/h takes only about 200 m of road.
