POINT OF VIEW

One view: does raging at someone reveal weakness, not strength?

Drag the dot to where you stand. Nothing here is right or wrong.

Rage can hide a void, or carry a cause.

For it: people who can win with skill or reasons rarely need to explode. Rage fills the gap, like a balloon of steel: hard outside, empty inside.

Against: anger at a real wrong can be fair, and it works. Plenty of calm people are insecure. The tell isn't the volume, it's having no other lever.

ASK YOURSELF The last time I raised my voice, was it a choice, or the only lever I had left?
