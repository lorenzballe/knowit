THE ℮ MARK

A pack says 500 g ℮. Is it likelier to weigh less or more?

500 g or a bit more

℮ means “estimated”: one pack may weigh up to 15 g less than the label. But on average the content must never be below the label. So the packs pile up on the label or just above it.

Pick one, say how sure you are, then watch 60 packs get weighed.

WHAT TO KEEP℮ lets a pack be up to 15 g short, but on average you get at least what’s printed.
Source · EU Directive 76/211/EEC on prepackaged products.
