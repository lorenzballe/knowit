THE ℮ MARK

A pack says 500 g ℮. Is it likelier to weigh less or more?

500 g or a bit more

℮ means “estimated”: one pack may be off by up to 15 g either way. But on average the content must never be below the label. So the packs pile up on the label or just above it.

Pick one, say how sure you are, then watch 60 packs get weighed.

WHAT TO KEEP℮ allows ±15 g, but on average you get at least what’s printed.
