PYRAMIDS

Why couldn't the Egyptians just use one long, straight ramp?

More stone than the pyramid itself.

A straight ramp has to climb all the way to the top, so it becomes a giant wedge of its own. It ends up taking more material than the pyramid. The best guess is a ramp that wraps around the pyramid instead.
