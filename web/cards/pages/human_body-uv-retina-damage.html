SUN & RETINA

How do the sun's UV rays damage the cells in your retina?

Chemistry, not fire.

Photoreceptors are the retina's cells that turn light into images. UV rays trigger photochemical reactions inside them that alter their structure and damage the cells, sometimes for good.

WHAT TO KEEPThe sun doesn't literally burn the retina: its UV light chemically breaks the cells that see.
