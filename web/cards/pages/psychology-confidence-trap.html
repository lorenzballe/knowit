CONFIDENCE

What happens to students' confidence after a clear, standard explanation video?

It goes up.

Students called the video clear, concise and easy to understand, and felt surer of their answers than on the pretest. That feeling rises even when the correct idea never got in.

Guess, then press play on the student's video.

WHAT TO KEEPFeeling sure after a clear explanation is not proof you learned it.
Source · Veritasium, “Khan Academy and the Effectiveness of Science Videos” (YouTube, 2011)
