FREEDIVING

Why is the phase around 35–40 m the most dangerous?

Press and hold KICK to swim the diver up from 100 m.

On the way up you must still beat the pull that dragged you to the bottom, so every stroke and kick burns oxygen. Around 35–40 m that effort meets a tank already running low: hypoxia sets in, air hunger hits and the diaphragm starts to contract.

WHAT TO KEEPThe danger is not the bottom but the hard climb back, when oxygen runs out.
