UNDERGROUND CRYSTALS

What natural process fills cavities in rocks with crystals?

Salt in the waternone → too much

Drag to load the underground water with salt.

Underground, water seeps through the rock and is often full of salt. Once it carries more than it can keep dissolved and finds a cavity, the extra comes out as crystals on the walls.

WHAT TO KEEPCrystal-filled cavities are where salty underground water dropped its load.
