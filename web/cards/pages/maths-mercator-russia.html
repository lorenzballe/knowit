DRAG RUSSIA

Why does Russia look bigger than Africa on most world maps?

LOOKS BIGGER Russia>Africa Greenland>Australia

On this Mercator map Russia looks bigger than Africa. Drag Russia down to the equator.

Mercator draws places bigger and bigger the farther they are from the equator. Far-north Russia is blown up several times; Africa, on the equator, is near its true size.

WHAT TO KEEPOn a Mercator map, the farther from the equator, the bigger a place looks.
