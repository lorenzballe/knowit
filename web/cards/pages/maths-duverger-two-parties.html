DRAG TO GUESS

Winner takes all. Imagine a third party on 23%. What happens to it?

A made-up race. Green voters prefer Left, but while Green runs, Right wins on 45%. Drag Green to where it is after 10 elections.

It all but vanishes.

Made-up numbers, not from the video: after each loss, a quarter of Green’s voters switch to Left.

The real case: in Florida in 2000, Nader, the Green candidate, got almost 100,000 votes and Bush won by fewer than 600. Each loss pushes voters to the bigger party, until two are left.

WHAT TO KEEP Under winner-takes-all, a vote for your favourite can elect your least favourite.
