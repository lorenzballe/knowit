Why are perfect numbers also triangles and stacks of odd cubes?

Each perfect number lands on a triangle with 2p−1 rows, so it holds (2p−1)·2p/2 = 2p−1(2p−1) dots: the perfect-number formula itself. The first m odd cubes add to m²(2m²−1), the same formula when m² = 2p−1: 2, 4, then 8 cubes.

WHAT TO KEEP2p−1(2p−1) is one shape seen two ways: a triangle and a stack of odd cubes.
