STOPPED EARTH

Why would oceans run to the poles if Earth stopped spinning?

The poles are 21 km closer to the centre

Earth is slightly squashed, so at the poles you stand about 21 km nearer its centre, where gravity is a little stronger. Spin's centrifugal push holds water out at the equator. Stop the spin and the water slides toward the poles.

Drag the brake to the right to stop Earth spinning.

WHAT TO KEEPWithout spin, water flows to where gravity is strongest: the poles.
