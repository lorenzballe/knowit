ST PETERSBURG, 1738

The pot starts at $2 and doubles at every heads; at the first tails you take it. How much would you pay to play?

Each extra heads doubles the pot and halves the odds. Pick the most you’d pay for one game.

WHAT TO KEEPWhen each doubled prize is half as likely, the average never settles. It grows the longer you play.
Source · D. Bernoulli, 1738 · W. Feller, Annals of Mathematical Statistics, 1945
