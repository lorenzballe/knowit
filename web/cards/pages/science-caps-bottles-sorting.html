Why are caps and bottles sorted apart if they're the same plastic?

Drag the cap flake into the bottle bin.

A cap and a bottle can be the same polymer, PET, yet the cap may hide additives such as aluminium or glass fillers. Mixed in, they would contaminate the recycled bottle plastic, so plants sort by product: caps with caps, bottles with bottles.
