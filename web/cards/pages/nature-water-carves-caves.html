How does plain water carve caves out of solid rock?

fresh rain water full of dissolved rock rock laid back down

Tap above the mountain to rain on the limestone.

Limestone is calcium carbonate, and water dissolves it, grain by grain. Rain sinks through cracks and widens them into passages. Once the water is loaded with dissolved rock, it drops some of it again: that is how stalactites and stalagmites grow.
