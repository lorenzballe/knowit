How does air resistance change a falling elevator's crash?

VacuumReal air

Air pushes up on the falling cabin, and that push grows as it speeds up. So it falls slower than in a vacuum: from the 50th floor it takes 6.1 seconds and hits at about 148 km/h.

Slide from vacuum to real air, let go, and watch it fall.
