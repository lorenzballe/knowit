MANY WORLDS

Does Many-Worlds make the universe deterministic again?

Pull the die back and let go. Which face will you see?

Nothing picked a face. All six landed, each in its own world, on every roll: 100% of the time. It only looks random because you live in one tiny sliver and see one face.

WHAT TO KEEPIn Many-Worlds the whole is certain. Only your slice looks like chance.
