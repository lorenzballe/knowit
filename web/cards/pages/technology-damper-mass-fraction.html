What share of a skyscraper's weight must its tuned mass damper be?

At least 1% to 5%

The damper is a pendulum near the top, tuned to swing at the building's own rhythm, with friction to soak up energy. A light one can't push back hard enough: it must weigh at least 1–5% of the whole building.

The tower is 100 blocks. Load its damper one 1% block at a time, then lock in.

WHAT TO KEEPA sway damper only works if it's heavy: 1–5% of the building.
