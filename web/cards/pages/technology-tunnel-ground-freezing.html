How do you dig a tunnel under a river without it flooding?

Under the river Isarco, the soil is soaked with water.

The water in the soil would pour into the dig. Liquid nitrogen at −196 °C freezes it into a solid shell, then brine keeps it below −10 °C. Both stay inside the pipes: soil and groundwater are untouched.
