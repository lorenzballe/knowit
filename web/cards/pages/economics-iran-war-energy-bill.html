What extra cost could the Iran war impose on Italian companies in 2026?

Drag the knob round the ring: how much bigger is firms' 2026 energy bill?

CGIA of Mestre estimates nearly €10 billion more for Italian firms in 2026: €7.2 billion on electricity, €2.6 billion on gas. That is about 13.5% on top of 2025, and it reaches us through the prices of what we buy.
