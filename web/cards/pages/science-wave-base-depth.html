WAVES

How deep under a wave does the water stop moving?

Drag the ball down into the water and watch its circle.

At half a wavelength

Under a wave, each bit of water rides in a circle, and the circles shrink the deeper you go. In an ideal wave they reach zero at half a wavelength down: the wave base. Below it, the water is still.

WHAT TO KEEPA wave stirs the water only down to half its wavelength.
