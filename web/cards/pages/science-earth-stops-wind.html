If Earth suddenly stopped spinning, how strong would the wind be?

Up to 1700 km/h

At the equator the ground and the air both race east at about 1700 km/h, so we feel nothing. Stop the ground and the air keeps going: winds 3 to 4 times stronger than the most intense storms ever recorded.

Drag the slider to zero to stop Earth. Watch the air.
