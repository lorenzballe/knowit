Why does a flame let a spark jump a wider gap?

The flame is full of ions

Air is a good insulator: about 10,000 V breaks down just 1 cm, so a 20,000 V plate sparks only a little. A flame holds ions, charged particles that conduct, so the same voltage breaks down a much longer stretch.
