How did Fat Man achieve a supercritical mass?

Plutonium-239 is so reactive that a gun design would have set it off before the pieces met. So Fat Man fired 32 charges in perfect unison, within a microsecond, crushing one sphere evenly until it was dense enough for a chain reaction: about 21 kilotons.
