Why does 1.8g make you dizzy and hard to stand?

1.0 g

Drag the plane up to 1.8g and watch the blood.

The plane accelerates upward, so the floor presses you with 1.8 times your weight. That same push drains blood from your head into your feet. Less blood reaches your brain, so you get dizzy.

WHAT TO KEEPMore g pulls blood down, away from your brain.
