SELF-SABOTAGE

What is a “bamboccia disillusa”, and why do valuable men pass?

Two halves: doing nothing, and an excuse for it.

She doesn’t study, work or look for either. She just waits, sure that it’s all pointless anyway. To a man seeking a partner she looks like a couch cover: decorative, with no growth to build on. The coach says it goes for men too.

Tap a bin: is this line “bambocciona” or “disillusa”?

WHAT TO KEEPWaiting plus “it’s pointless” reads as no future to share.
