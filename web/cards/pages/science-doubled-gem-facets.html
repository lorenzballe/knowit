BIREFRINGENCE

What happens when you look at a gemstone through a magnifying glass?

Every main edge doubles.

Many gems split each ray of light into two rays that take slightly different paths. So under a simple lens, the stone's fundamental faces show up twice. This is birefringence.

Drag the magnifying glass over the gem.

WHAT TO KEEPUnder a lens, birefringence makes a gem's facet edges look doubled.
