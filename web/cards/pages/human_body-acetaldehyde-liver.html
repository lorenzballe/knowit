What does your liver turn alcohol into, and why is it worse?

Hold the button to drink, and follow one molecule.

Your liver's ADH turns ethanol into acetaldehyde: more poisonous, and a Group 1 carcinogen. It breaks DNA and blocks its repair.

WHAT TO KEEPAlcohol hurts twice: as ethanol, then as the worse acetaldehyde your liver makes from it.
