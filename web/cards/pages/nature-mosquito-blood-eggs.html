What does a female mosquito actually live on?

Three things around her: the blood, the flower and her belly.

Nectar is her food. Blood is for her eggs.

Like many insects, she drinks plant nectar for energy. She takes blood only for its proteins, which complete her eggs: 100 to 500 from one meal. That's why only females bite.

Tap the blood, the flower and her belly.

WHAT TO KEEPMosquitoes bite to make eggs, not to feed themselves.
