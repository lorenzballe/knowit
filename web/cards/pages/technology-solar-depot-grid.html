SOLAR DEPOT

What two jobs does the depot's 30 kW solar roof do?

Drag the sun across the sky and watch where the energy goes.

The 30 kW roof and its storage batteries recharge the whole convoy quickly. Once the train is full, the surplus is sold to the local grid, so the depot works as a small power plant.

WHAT TO KEEPThe train fills first. Whatever sunshine is left over goes to the grid.
