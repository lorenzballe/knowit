FREE FALL

A lift's cable snaps. What's your best chance of surviving the fall?

Lie flat on the floor

To cancel the fall by jumping, you would have to leap up at the lift's own speed, about 130 km/h: a job for Superman. Lying flat spreads the same blow over your whole body instead of two small feet.
