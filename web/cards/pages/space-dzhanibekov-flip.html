ZERO-G

Spin a T-handle in zero-G. Which spin keeps flipping over?

Pick a spin, then watch it.

Pick the spin you think flips. Then say how sure you are.

Around the bar or flat, the spin holds steady. Around the stem, the middle axis (neither the least nor the greatest moment of inertia), any tiny wobble grows until the handle flips, then flips back, again and again. Astronauts have seen it on the space station with a T-bar handle.

WHAT TO KEEPSpin about the middle axis is unstable: it keeps flipping.
