RADIATION

Can microwaves from mobile phones damage molecules?

No, they shouldn't.

Phones transmit microwaves: waves very similar to light, not fast particles. The radiation that can damage molecules like DNA is UV, X-rays, gamma rays, alpha and beta. Microwaves are not in that group.

Pick one, then watch the wave reach the DNA.

WHAT TO KEEPPhone microwaves are not the kind of radiation that damages DNA.
