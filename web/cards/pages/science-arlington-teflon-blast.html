ARLINGTON 1944

Why did the 1944 Teflon production attempt cause a massive explosion?

Seen from above: one solid cylinder of TFE turning into Teflon, making heat. Tap where that heat ends up.

The heat had no way out.

Polymerizing the solid cylinder made heat that could not dissipate. It stayed in, expanded and built up pressure until it failed: a massive explosion at Arlington in 1944 that killed two workers.

WHAT TO KEEP A reaction that makes heat needs a way to take that heat away. Without one, the heat stays in, expands and creates the force.
