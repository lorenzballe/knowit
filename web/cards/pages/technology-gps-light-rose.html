How does GPS depend on light having the same speed in all directions?

Drag the pulse. Then pick.

It syncs its clocks on it.

GPS satellites send time-stamped signals. If light's speed changed with direction, the signals would arrive at different rates and the clocks wouldn't be properly synced, and positioning needs that sync.

WHAT TO KEEP GPS doesn't check that light is the same every way. It's built on it.
