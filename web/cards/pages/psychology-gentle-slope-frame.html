READ THE ROAD

Walking this road, what does your brain judge the middle stretch against?

Drag the orange dot to your answer. Then say how sure.

Move the dot first to unlock.

Against the two steep stretches beside it.

Three things add up. Nothing beyond the road to compare with. A gentle stretch between two very steep ones. And no view past the horizon. So the brain struggles to tell which way the middle goes.

WHAT TO KEEP With no outside reference, the brain uses the slopes next to a slope as its reference.
