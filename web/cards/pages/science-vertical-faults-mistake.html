GEOLOGY

Why should a geological section never show a vertical fault?

It hides the movement.

A fault's lean tells which block lies on top, and so whether it slid down (normal) or was pushed up (reverse). Draw it vertical and you say neither: to a geologist it reads as "I didn't understand this place".

Drag the fault sideways to lean it, even a little.

Pick a reason, then say how sure you are.

WHAT TO KEEPAlways lean a fault: its tilt is the kinematics.
