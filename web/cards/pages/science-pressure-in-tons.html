DEEP PRESSURE

How much weight presses on one square metre at 3,500 metres deep?

Drag the table down to Titan's depth.

Every 10 m of sea adds about one atmosphere: at 3,500 m, 350 of them. That is about 3,500 tonnes on each square metre.

WHAT TO KEEPAt Titan's depth, each square metre carries about 2,000 cars.
