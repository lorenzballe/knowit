EARTH'S CORE

How do we know Earth's inner core is solid?

A P-wave from an earthquake waits at the surface.

In the liquid outer core, P-waves slow down. At about 5,100 km deep they suddenly speed up again, which means they have entered something denser and solid: the inner core.
