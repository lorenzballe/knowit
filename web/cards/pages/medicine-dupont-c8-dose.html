DOSE AND HARM

C8 kills a rat at 570 mg/kg. How little made its liver grow abnormally?

DuPont's in-house scientists, 1961.

As little as 1.5 mg/kg.

DuPont's own tests in 1961 found livers growing abnormally at that dose. The company kept making C8 for decades.

WHAT TO KEEP Ask how little harms, not how much kills.
