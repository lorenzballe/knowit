DRAG YOUR GUESS

A tube flies forward. The X on it spins backwards. How fast does the X move?

Drag the X's arrow: how fast does it go, next to the tube?

Slower than the tube itself.

The X travels with the tube, but its own spin points backwards, so it takes away from the tube's speed: the X's velocity is reduced. The O spins the way the tube moves, so its spin adds and it crosses your view much faster.

WHAT TO KEEP A mark on a spinning tube moves at the tube's speed plus its spin: backward spin subtracts, forward spin adds.
