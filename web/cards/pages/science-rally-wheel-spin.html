RALLY

Why must drivers consider wheel spin rate when landing from a jump?

Tyres grip when they turn at road speed.

At touchdown, wheels spinning faster than the ground slip before they bite, and at full throttle the engine over-revs. So mid-air drivers ease off the throttle, land on all four wheels, then accelerate.

Set the throttle in the air, then tap Land.

WHAT TO KEEPLand with wheels turning at road speed and grip comes back at once.
