Why does a golf club feel light after you hit the ball?

impacttime →later

Drag time forward from the hit. Watch the shaft and your hand.

The hit is a huge jolt at the club head. It has to travel up the shaft as a wave, so your hand feels it only when it arrives, with the ball already well on its way. And what reaches you is much smaller than what the ball got.

WHAT TO KEEPA force isn't felt everywhere at once: it travels, like the slinky's message.
