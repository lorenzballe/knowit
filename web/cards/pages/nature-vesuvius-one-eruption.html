Why is Vesuvius's 1944 eruption seen as a continuation of 1906?

Drag the drawing apart: lift the surface off what lies beneath.

One process, two outbursts

At the surface, 1906 and 1944 look like two separate eruptions. Scientists agree 1944 was in every sense a continuation of the strong 1906 eruption: the same magmatic process, still going.

WHAT TO KEEP1944 wasn't a new start for Vesuvius: it was 1906, carried on.
