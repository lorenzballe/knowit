How fast was the Titan's implosion?

Press play and watch the hull, then your brain, against one clock.

At those pressures the hull likely gave way in under 1 millisecond. Your brain needs about 13–15 ms just to process an image, and around 100 ms to register pain. It was over before anyone could notice.
