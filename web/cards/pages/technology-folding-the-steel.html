What are the two main benefits of folding a sword's steel?

Spread out, and lined up

Each fold stretches and stacks the steel, so clumps of silicon, sulfur and phosphorus thin out until there is no weak point. The layers give it a grain, strongest where it will be hit. A little oxidation darkens each layer, which makes the patterns.
