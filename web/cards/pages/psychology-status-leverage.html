POINT OF VIEW

Does fame or wealth, by itself, give someone power over you?

Drag yourself to where you stand.

One view: no use to you, no hold on you.

For: leverage is what someone can give or withhold that you need. Need nothing, and the gold card is blank. Against: status pulls with nothing given, along those three strings.

ASK YOURSELF When someone important makes me shrink, what exactly could they give me, or take away?
