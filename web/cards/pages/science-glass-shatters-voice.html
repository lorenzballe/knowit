SLOW MOTION

When a voice breaks a glass, what happens at the very end?

It shatters.

Every note pushes the glass and makes it swing a bit wider. In slow motion you can see it oscillate, until it bends further than it can take and breaks.

Tap fast to sing at the glass. Watch it wobble in slow motion.

WHAT TO KEEPSmall pushes add up into a swing big enough to break the glass.
