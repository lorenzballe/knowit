What does radiation actually do to the cells in your body?

Press and hold: send radiation into a strand of DNA.

Radiation strikes DNA, snapping its strands or changing its letters. A changed letter is a mutation: the cell now follows altered instructions. Enough damage ruins tissue and can lead to cancer.
