CAPACI, 1992

Why did the first car explode before Falcone's?

The trigger was rehearsed with a car at a steady 160–170 km/h, until the flash fired exactly on the spot. Falcone was driving at 100–120 km/h, so at the blast his car hadn't reached the charge yet. The lead car had.
