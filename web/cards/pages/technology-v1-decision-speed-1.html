GO OR STOP

What is V1, and why can't a takeoff stop after it?

Hit Full power, then Abort whenever you like. Where will the plane stop?

The captain calls out V1 on the takeoff roll: the speed beyond which the takeoff can no longer be aborted. Below V1 the brakes still stop the plane on the runway. Above it there is no runway left to stop on, so the plane must fly.

WHAT TO KEEPBefore V1 you may stop; after V1 you go, because the runway is spent.
