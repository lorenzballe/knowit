BONE IN ORBIT

Floating in orbit, how much bone mass can an astronaut lose in one month?

Drag down on the orbit bone to hollow it to your guess.

1–2% of bone mass,every month

Over 10 times faster than osteoporosis on Earth.

With no weight pushing on them, astronauts' bones thin, mostly in the legs.

WHAT TO KEEP Bone stays only where load is felt.
