NIGHT REPAIR

How many joints were welded overnight, and which came first?

Over 200 joints, ranked by importance.

Every night, welders put two steel plates, 5 cm thick and 2 m long, on each joint, like Band‑Aids on both sides. LeMessurier started with the 30th floor, so the most critical joints were fixed first.
