FIRE PHYSICS

In a room fire, why does the temperature suddenly rise out of control?

Heat soaked up by wallsAir temperature

The walls and furniture soak up the heat. The air warms slowly.

Walls and furniture soak up heat only until they are full. Then surfaces shed it as hot, flammable gas under the ceiling, whose radiation makes more gas: the temperature runs away.
