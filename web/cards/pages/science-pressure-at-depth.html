DEEP PRESSURE

On the dive to the Titanic, 3,800 m down, at 3,000 m: what presses on 1 m²?

Drag up the tower: how much of its weight presses on that square metre?

301 atmospheres

Every 10 m of water adds one atmosphere, plus one from the air. At 3,000 m, on the way to the Titanic at 3,800 m, that makes 301: about 3,000 t on 1 m², a third of the Eiffel Tower.

WHAT TO KEEP Deep pressure is plain counting: every 10 m of water, one more atmosphere.
