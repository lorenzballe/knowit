BRIDGE TOWERS

How tall and how heavy is one section of a bridge tower leg?

Hold the button to lift a section onto the tower legs.

20 m · 1,200 t

The tower legs of the Messina Strait bridge are stacked from 44 prefabricated sections. Each one is 20 meters tall and weighs 1,200 tons, so the crane lifts a whole block at a time.

WHAT TO KEEPOne tower section: 20 m tall, 1,200 tons; 44 of them make the legs.
