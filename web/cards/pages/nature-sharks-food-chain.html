PULL THE SHARK

Why would losing sharks throw the whole sea off balance?

Sharks sit at the top of the food chain and keep their prey in check. Take them away and the prey multiply, and every level below tips out of balance. Yet about 100 million sharks and rays die every year.

Sharks keep the fish below them in check. Drag the shark up to the boat.
