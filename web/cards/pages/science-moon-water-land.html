Why does the Moon affect water more than land?

Bring the Moon overhead and watch both sides.

The Moon pulls on the whole Earth, but liquid particles can flow, so the sea piles up into a bulge. Solid ground is locked together and barely moves. That is why tides show up in the sea.

WHAT TO KEEPWater can flow, so it piles up under the Moon; rock can't.
