How deep must an undersea volcano's crater be for its tsunami risk to be very low?

Drag the volcano's top up or down to where you think the tsunami risk turns very low.

Deeper than 500–600 m, the tsunami risk is very low

An eruption shoves the water column, but the energy disperses in the water. From the Marsili, just over 500 m down, only some pumice would come up.

WHAT TO KEEP Judge an undersea volcano by the water above its crater.
