A weighted wire is pulled down through a block of ice. What's left?

One solid block

Under the thin wire the pressure is huge, so the ice right below it melts. The water slips above the wire, the pressure is gone, and it freezes again. This is called regelation.

Bet on what's left. Then say how sure you are.
