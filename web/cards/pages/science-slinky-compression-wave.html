SLINKY DROP

Drop a hanging slinky. What type of wave tells the bottom to fall?

A compressional wave

Letting go is news, and it has to travel down the slinky as a squeeze, coil bumping into coil. The bottom hangs still until that squeeze reaches it. Only then does it start to fall.

Pick an answer, then watch the slinky drop.

WHAT TO KEEPThe bottom can't know you let go until the compression wave arrives.
