ROAD OR RAIL

What physiological phenomenon discourages building a road tunnel under the Channel?

Drag across the tunnel, left to right, to drive on into the night.

That's highway hypnosis: after many hours at the wheel, above all at night, concentration and reflexes drop and the driver just stares at the lane lines. In a long road tunnel that means accidents. On rails, nobody has to keep the car in its lane.

WHAT TO KEEPA long, monotonous drive dulls the driver; on a train, the track does the steering.
