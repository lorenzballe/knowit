What is the primary tactical purpose of the 'Pistolero Walk'?

Watch them walk. Tap DRAW! at any moment: who gets the pistol out first?

It comes from the KGB training manual; Putin learned it in Cold War Dresden, East Germany. The stiff right arm never leaves the holster's side, so the pistol comes out as fast as possible.

WHAT TO KEEPThe stiff right arm is a gun hand kept parked by the holster.
