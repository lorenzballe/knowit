TUNNELS

If you just dug this tunnel, how high would the water rise?

The volcanic rock here is soaked with water. Open a hole and the water pours in: it would reach 14.33 m above sea level, so the tunnel would be flooded.

Flip the switch to “Just dig” and watch the tunnel.
