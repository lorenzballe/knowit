VINE ROBOT

A vine robot grows from its tip. How does its camera stay on?

Two frames, locked through the skin

The camera sits on an outer frame whose wheels interlock with an inner frame inside the pressurized body, like a roller coaster's wheels around its track. So it can't fall off as the robot grows.
