THERMITE

Why won't a lighter, or even a propane torch, set off thermite?

Every aluminum grain wears a skin of aluminum oxide, which is very stable. Nothing reacts until heat breaks that seal in a great many grains at once.

That barrier, a very high activation energy, is out of reach for a lighter or a propane torch. A barium hydroxide igniter, the stuff in sparklers, gets there.

Pick a heat source and hold it to the thermite.

WHAT TO KEEPThermite's fuel comes sealed: no broken oxide skin, no reaction.
