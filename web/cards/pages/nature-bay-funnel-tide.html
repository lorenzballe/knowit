TIDES

How does the shape of Mont Saint‑Michel’s bay raise water levels up to 15 m?

Low tide: the bay is open sand, seen from above.

The bay is a funnel. The tide comes in wide, the shores close in, and the water has nowhere to go but up: 10–12 m at the Mont, peaks near 15 m.

Tap the bay to bring the tide in, one step at a time.

WHAT TO KEEPSqueeze a tide into a funnel and it climbs.
