ROAD POTHOLES

A heavy truck rolls over a cracked, wet road. What does the water do?

It forces the cracks wider

The truck’s weight squeezes the soil. Water can’t be squeezed, so it pushes on the cracks already there and opens them further.

Pick what the water does. Then say how sure you are.

WHAT TO KEEPSoil gives under weight; trapped water can’t, so it pries cracks apart.
