NUCLEAR WATER

Why is tritium the only radioactive thing left in Fukushima's treated water?

Tritium is part of the water itself.

A first filter takes out cesium-137, then ALPS removes essentially every other radionuclide. Tritium is an isotope of hydrogen, chemically the same, so it sits inside the water molecule and can't be separated chemically.
