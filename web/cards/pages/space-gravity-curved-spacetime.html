BLACK HOLES

What is a black hole's gravity, really?

Little mass, little density: spacetime stays almost flat, and light goes straight.

small mass · looseenormous mass · dense

Drag right to pack more mass into less space. Watch the light.

Nothing pulls the light. Enormous mass at enormous density curves spacetime itself, and light just follows the curve. Bend it enough and no path leads back out: that is why the hole is black.
