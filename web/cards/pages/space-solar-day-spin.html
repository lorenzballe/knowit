SOLAR DAY

How far does Earth turn between one noon and the next?

Spin Earth with your finger, one day: from noon, Sun overhead, to the next noon.

While Earth spins, it also moves along its orbit, so the Sun ends up at a slightly different angle. Earth must turn about 1° more, some 4 minutes, to bring the Sun overhead again.

WHAT TO KEEPA 24-hour solar day is a bit more than one full spin of Earth.
