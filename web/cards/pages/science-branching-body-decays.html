How often do the radioactive atoms in your body split the universe?

About 5,000 times a second

Each radioactive nucleus in you is in a superposition: decayed and not decayed. When one decays, it hits what is around it, becomes entangled, and the universe branches. Your body has about 5,000 such decays every second.
