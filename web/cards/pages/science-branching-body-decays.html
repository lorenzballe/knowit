How often do the radioactive atoms in your body split the universe?

Each radioactive nucleus in you is in a superposition: decayed and not decayed. When one decays, it hits what is around it, becomes entangled, and the universe branches.

Drag along the scale to guess how many splits per second. Then say how sure you are.

WHAT TO KEEPBranching is constant: your own body does it thousands of times a second.
