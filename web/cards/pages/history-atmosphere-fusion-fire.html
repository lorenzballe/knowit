A runaway fusion fire

The bomb's heat might make the air's hydrogen fuse. Each fusion's energy would fuse more, and split water vapor to feed the fire, until the whole atmosphere became one giant fusion bomb. Oppenheimer feared the nitrogen in the air could join in too.
