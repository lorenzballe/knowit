48 HOURS

After a nuclear blast, why wait 48 hours before leaving shelter?

You are sheltered. Drag the gold bead round the clock to let the hours pass. Tap the door to try leaving.

For the first 48 hours the fallout outside is at its most intense. Staying in through that window spares you the most lethal dose. Even after, wait for the authorities' all-clear if you can.

WHAT TO KEEPAfter a blast, stay sheltered at least 48 hours: that is when fallout peaks.
