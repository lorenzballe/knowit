PICK THE HOUR

You survived the blast in a shelter. When should you step outside?

Drag to the hour you would open the door.

Wait at least 48 hours.

Fallout radiation is most intense in the first 48 hours. Wait at least that long before you go out and move away, or ideally until the authorities give the all-clear.

WHAT TO KEEP When a danger fades with time, waiting is not doing nothing: it is the move.
