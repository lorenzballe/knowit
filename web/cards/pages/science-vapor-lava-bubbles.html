LAVA & WATER

What happens to the vapor the water makes inside lava?

It swells into bubbles

Water touching molten rock turns to vapor at once. The vapor expands inside the lava, making bubbles that push the rock up, so the lava seems to breathe. Enough of it and the lava bursts open.

Tap the lava to drop water on it: 3 drops.

WHAT TO KEEPWater in lava becomes vapor that expands, and expanding vapor can blow lava apart.
