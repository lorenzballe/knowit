Why is toothpaste chemically unable to dissolve an eggshell?

Base meets base: nothing happens

The shell is calcium carbonate, a base. Toothpaste is basic too. Dissolving the shell would need an acid-base reaction, and two bases can't have one, so the viral video is fake.

Pick the reason, then watch the paste meet the shell.

WHAT TO KEEPNo acid, no acid-base reaction: a base can't eat a base.
