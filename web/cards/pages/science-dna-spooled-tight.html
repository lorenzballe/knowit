PUT IN ORDER

DNA is 2 nm wide. A chromosome is cm long. How does it fit without tangling?

Tap the two steps from thread to chromosome, in order, then say how sure you are. One of the three never happens.

It never tangles. It wraps, then folds, again and again.

DNA wraps around histone proteins, forming nucleosomes, and the nucleosomes fold again and again. Massive compaction that stays organized: no headphones-in-a-bag knot.

WHAT TO KEEP To pack something long without knots, wrap it first, then fold what you wrapped.
