OCEAN PLASTIC

Why is the Pacific plastic island a problem on your own plate?

Tap the open ocean 3 times to drop plastic. Follow where it goes.

Currents sweep floating plastic into the gyre, where it breaks into tiny pieces. Those pieces drift back to fishing coasts, get eaten by small fish, then big fish, and end up in our food and water.

WHAT TO KEEPNo ocean is far away: the plastic we throw in comes back to the table.
