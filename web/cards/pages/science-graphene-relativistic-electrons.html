Why does Einstein's relativity apply to electrons in graphene?

Nothing slows them down

In copper, defects in the lattice keep scattering electrons, so they crawl. Graphene's strong, flexible carbon bonds make a perfect lattice, so electrons go so fast they act like massless particles, and only Einstein's relativity describes their motion.
