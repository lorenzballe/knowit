RECYCLING

How much CO₂ is avoided by recycling six tons of glass?

6 t of glass ≈ 1 t of CO₂ saved

Melting old glass replaces making new glass from raw sand, and that is far more carbon‑intensive. So every six tons recycled keep about one ton of CO₂ out of the air.

Tap to recycle glass, one ton at a time, and watch the CO₂ you avoid.
