CAVE RESCUE

What is the shortest possible rescue time from deep in a cave?

A caver is hurt at the bottom. Tap spot 1 to start the clock.

At least 9–10 hours

No rescue gets you out in an hour. Two people must climb out to call help, rescuers rig every wall with ropes, and the injured caver crosses the rock on a stretcher.

Tap the glowing spots in order to run the rescue.
