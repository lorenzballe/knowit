How do volcanic rocks prove Sardinia and Iberia were once joined?

LONG AGOTODAY

One landmass: Iberia and the Sardinia–Corsica block are joined.

The same rock, on both sides.

A volcano sat right on the split, so its rock fell on both pieces at once. If they had already been apart, the same rock could not have landed on both.
