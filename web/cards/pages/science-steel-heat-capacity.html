How much load can a steel column still carry at 1000 °C?

20 °C

Drag the slider to heat the column, from room temperature to 1000 °C.

Only about 5% is left

As steel heats up it loses stiffness and strength. At 1000 °C, as NIST notes, it has lost 95% of its ability to carry loads, so the floors it holds become too heavy and it gives way.

WHAT TO KEEPAt 1000 °C steel keeps just 5% of its load-bearing capacity.
