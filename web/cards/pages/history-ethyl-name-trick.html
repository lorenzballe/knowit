Why was the lead additive named ‘Ethyl’?

One bottle holds the lead additive. Tap it.

Its full name was tetraethyl lead. Midgley and Kettering sold it as “Ethyl”, perhaps so it might be confused with ethyl alcohol, a common additive. They made no mention of lead.

WHAT TO KEEPA friendly name can hide what’s inside: ask for the full one.
