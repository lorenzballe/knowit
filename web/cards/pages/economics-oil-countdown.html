OIL CLOCK

At today's pace, how many years of oil are left?

Each dot is a billion barrels still underground. Drag along the timeline: when does it run out?

About 45 years

Known reserves are about 1,650 billion barrels. The world uses about 100 million barrels a day, which is 36.5 billion a year. 1,650 ÷ 36.5 ≈ 45, if both stay as they are.
