GAME THEORY

Why does not knowing when a game ends keep players cooperating?

Each domino is a round, green while both cooperate. Push over the known last round.

With a known end, betraying in the last round is safe, and that logic slides back to round 1. With a random end, typically about 200 rounds, any round might be followed by another: players keep cooperating, because they might still need each other.

WHAT TO KEEPWhen the end is uncertain, the future keeps people cooperating.
