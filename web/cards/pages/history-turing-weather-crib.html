How did weather reports help Alan Turing break Enigma?

Weather reports gave Turing a guessed piece of text. An Enigma key never lights its own lamp, so a letter can't encrypt to itself, which rules out every spot but one. There the letter pairs form a loop, R→E→T→R, and only correct settings bring R back to R.

Slide the guess along the message until no key sits under its own letter.
