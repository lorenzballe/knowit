Pick one: your arrows appear in the rock. Then say how sure.

Farther out, the rock kept an older field

Cooling magnetite takes the direction of Earth's field. Rock farther from the ridge is older: it set when the field had a different orientation. Dark bands carry today's positive polarity, light bands a different one. This record confirms the seafloor is spreading.

WHAT TO KEEP When a record is laid down over time and carried off, distance becomes a clock.
