DRAG THE MOUNTAIN

Stand Everest on the deepest seabed. Where does its summit end up?

Drag Everest down until its foot rests on the deepest seabed, wherever you think that is.

 

Over 2 km under the waves.

The Challenger Deep, in the Pacific, goes down over 10,900 m. Everest is about 8,848 m tall, so even standing on the bottom its summit stays over 2 km under water.

WHAT TO KEEP We size things from the surface we can see; measure the hidden half before you compare.
