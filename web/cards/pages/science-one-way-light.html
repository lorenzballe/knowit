ONE-WAY LIGHT

What if light travelled faster one way than the other?

Drag to make light slower one way and faster back. Then send a signal to Mark on Mars.

WHAT TO KEEPWe can only time light's round trip. Timing one leg needs synced clocks, and syncing them assumes light is equally fast both ways.
