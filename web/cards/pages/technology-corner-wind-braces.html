Why did corner wind strain Citicorp's braces more than head-on wind?

Drag the wind arrow around the tower. Watch the braces.

√2 ≈ 1.41: 40% more

Corner wind splits into two pushes of F/√2, one on each face. In half the diagonal braces they cancel; in the other half they add: 2 × F/√2 ≈ 1.41 F. That is 40% above the head-on load LeMessurier had calculated.

WHAT TO KEEPWind on the corner, not on a face, loaded Citicorp's braces hardest: 40% more.
