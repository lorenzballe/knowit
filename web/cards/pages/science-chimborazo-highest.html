Which mountain becomes the highest if Earth stops rotating?

Spinning todayStopped

Chimborazo, 13,600 m

A spinning Earth bulges at the equator, and its oceans bulge with it. Chimborazo sits on the equator, so its summit is the farthest point from Earth's centre. Stop the spin and the seas flatten and drain away from the equator, leaving Chimborazo towering above Everest.
