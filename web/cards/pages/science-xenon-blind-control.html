Why did the automatic control fail during Chernobyl's low-power test?

The automatic control ignored xenon, a gas building up in the core that upsets the fine balance of the reaction. So power dropped fast, down to 1%, and the poisoned core became truly unstable. A basic safety rule says: never restart a reactor poisoned with xenon.
