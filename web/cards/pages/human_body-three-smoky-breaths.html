FIRE SMOKE

How many breaths of thick black smoke before you pass out?

Just 3 or 4 breaths

Hot smoke rises, spreads across the ceiling, then drops fast. In a few moments the air can't be breathed, and three or four breaths of dense black smoke are enough to make you lose consciousness.

Guess how many breaths. Then say how sure you are.

WHAT TO KEEPIn thick smoke you don't get minutes, you get a few breaths.
