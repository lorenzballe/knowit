DEEP FREEZE

How is meat sold at Yakutsk’s outdoor markets prepared for sale?

Drag a tool onto the frozen meat and try to cut a slice.

With a chainsaw.

At Yakutsk’s outdoor market, meat and fish are inevitably frozen solid. A knife can’t get in, so to sell them, vendors cut them with a chainsaw.
