FERTILISATION

Sperm climb the uterus. How do they find the egg?

The egg calls them

They meet in a Fallopian tube, the little pipe between ovary and uterus. The egg pops out of the ovary and sends chemical signals; the surviving sperm are drawn to it, like iron to a magnet.

Pick one, say how sure you are, then watch the egg.
