Little Boy carried 64 kg of uranium-235. How much of it actually split?

Each square is 1 kg of uranium-235. Drag across them to fill how many kilograms you think split.

Under 1 kg. Just 1.5%.

Gun-type bombs like Little Boy had low efficiency: 98.5% of the 64 kg of U-235 took no part in the reaction and fell as radioactive debris.

What to keep A bomb's mass is not its yield; ask how much of it reacts.
