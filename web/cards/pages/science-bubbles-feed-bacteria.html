Air for the bacteria

The bubbles are air, blown in from the bottom of the tank. Some of the bacteria living there are aerobic: they need oxygen to stay alive. With that oxygen they oxidize the organic and nitrogen-rich matter in the sewage.

Tap to pump air into the tank and watch the bacteria.

WHAT TO KEEPThe bubbles are oxygen delivery for bacteria that clean the water.
