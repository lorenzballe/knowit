Who actually invented the Condorcet voting method first?

Ramon Llull, a monk, 450 years earlier

Llull found it while studying how church leaders were chosen. His book, Ars eleccionis, was lost, so his idea made no impact and the method got Condorcet's name. The book was only found again in 2001.
