How does the Archimedes bridge let the sea carry its weight?

Drag the sea up until the tunnel is fully underwater.

The sea pushes back

Underwater, the tunnel pushes water aside. That water pushes up with a force equal to its own weight, so the pillars only carry what is left.

WHAT TO KEEPAnything underwater is pushed up by the weight of the water it moves aside.
