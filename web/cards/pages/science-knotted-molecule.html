When was the first synthetic knotted molecule created?

1989

Two copper ions (Cu) hold two threads of atoms crossed. Join the ends.

Chemist Jean-Pierre Sauvage tied molecules around copper ions into a trefoil knot, the first synthetic knotted molecule. The knot stops the atoms from unfurling, trapping them in higher energy states that give the molecule new properties.

WHAT TO KEEPTie a molecule in a different knot and you get a different material.
