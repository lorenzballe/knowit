RALLY PHYSICS

How can a thousandth of a second decide a rally championship?

Tap the wheel, the brake and the throttle: give each a tiny error.

0.001 s

A tiny late or extra input pushes the car off its best line, and off the line it loses grip and time. Those losses add up stage after stage, and over a season they can swing a world championship.

WHAT TO KEEPIn rally, a title can hang on half a degree or a thousandth of a second.
