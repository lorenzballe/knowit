TOTAL ECLIPSE

Why do solar prominences and the corona become visible during an eclipse?

Always there, just outshone.

The Sun’s blazing disc drowns out its faint outer glow. Once the Moon covers the disc, the corona and pink prominences show.

Drag the Moon over the Sun.

WHAT TO KEEPAn eclipse doesn’t switch the corona on; it switches the glare off.
