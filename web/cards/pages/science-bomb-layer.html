Why do some geologists mark the Anthropocene’s beginning around 1950‑1960?

Pick one, then say how sure.

Bomb-test fallout: one layer, laid worldwide at once

In the 1950s and 60s, hydrogen-bomb tests sent massive fallout into the atmosphere. It came down across the whole globe in the same years and left one radioactive layer. Some geologists use that layer as a clear marker for the start of the Anthropocene.

WHAT TO KEEP A start date in the Earth’s layers needs a mark laid down everywhere at the same time.
