TUNNEL SAFETY

How does the Turin–Lyon tunnel deal with asbestos in the rock?

0lining segments
0asbestos loads kept on site
0asbestos loads that left

These mountains are likely to hold asbestos. TELT, the company building the line, guarantees that rock with asbestos never leaves the worksite: it stays inside, handled under a strict safety protocol. Clean rock gets reused, even as aggregate for the tunnel's lining segments.
