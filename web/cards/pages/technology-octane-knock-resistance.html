What does a fuel's octane rating actually measure?

Resistance to knocking

Squeeze a fuel and air mix hard enough and it can ignite on its own, before the spark: that's knocking. Iso-octane takes far more squeezing than n-heptane, so it was simply set at 100 and n-heptane at 0. The number says how much squeeze a fuel can take, not how good it is.

Pick a fuel, then hold the button to squeeze the mix.

WHAT TO KEEPOctane measures resistance to knocking, not the quality of the fuel.
