ALPINE TUNNEL

At what depth and gradient will the Turin–Lyon tunnel run, and why?

1,000–2,200 m deep · 2–12 % slope

So deep below the peaks, the track never has to climb up the mountains. Its slope stays gentle, so it runs more like a railway on a plain than a mountain line.

Flip between climbing over the Alps and boring deep under them.
