MOTOGP CRASHES

What makes a low-side crash different from a high-side in MotoGP?

Flip between the two crashes and watch the rear tyre.

Slide out vs. thrown off

Low-side: the rear tyre loses grip and the bike drops to the inside, so you slide. High-side: the tyre slides, then suddenly grips again, and the bike flicks you up and over. Rossi says the one with the gas closed throws you higher and harder; he was hurt that way at Mugello, at 180 km/h on a cold tyre.

WHAT TO KEEPLow-side: grip goes and you slide. High-side: grip comes back and you fly.
