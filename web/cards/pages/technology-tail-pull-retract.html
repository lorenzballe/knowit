PULL THE TAIL

How does this growing robot pull itself back in?

Drag along the bar to pull the tail. Watch the blue band.

Pulling the tail drags the material back through the core of the body. At the tip, the wall turns inside out and goes back inside itself: the robot ungrows. In a tight space, that is all it takes.
