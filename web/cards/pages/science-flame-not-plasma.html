PLASMA?

A candle flame holds ions. Does that make it a true plasma?

Tap the zone where the candle flame belongs.

Ions, but not plasma

A flame does contain ions: strong electric fields pull its charges apart. But it isn't hot enough and its ions aren't dense enough to count as a true plasma.

WHAT TO KEEPHaving ions isn't enough: plasma needs great heat and dense ions.
