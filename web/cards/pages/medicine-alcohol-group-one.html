How does the international cancer agency classify alcohol?

Group 1: carcinogenic to humans

The International Agency for Research on Cancer puts alcohol in Group 1: there is sufficient evidence that it causes cancer. Cigarette smoke is in the same group. So drinking raises the risk of some kinds of cancer.
