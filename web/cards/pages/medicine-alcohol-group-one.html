How sure is the cancer agency that alcohol causes cancer?

Drag the glass to where you think the agency puts alcohol.

Group 1: it causes cancer.

The agency puts alcohol in Group 1, carcinogenic to humans, alongside cigarette smoke: the evidence that it causes cancer is sufficient. So drinking raises the risk of some cancers.

WHAT TO KEEP A ranking of evidence tells you how sure we are, not how much it hurts.
