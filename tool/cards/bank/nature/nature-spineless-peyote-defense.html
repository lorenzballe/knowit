PLANT DEFENSE

Peyote has no spines. Why does that matter for its psychoactive power?

Chemistry instead of thorns

With no spines, peyote has to protect itself another way: it makes mescaline. That substance gives the plant its very bitter taste and its psychoactive effects, and both are weapons against animals that would eat it.

Look at the two cactuses, then pick the reason.

WHAT TO KEEPPeyote's mind-altering power is its defense: mescaline does the job of the missing thorns.
