The Siberian anticyclone

Freezing ground makes the air above it cold, dry and heavy: a high-pressure zone where the weather changes very slowly. Yakutsk sits on a valley floor ringed by mountains, so that dense air sinks in and stays trapped.

WHAT TO KEEPCold air is heavy: under high pressure in a valley, it settles and stays.
