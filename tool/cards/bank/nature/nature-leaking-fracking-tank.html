FRACKING

How can a badly built tank poison the water we drink?

Switch the tank to “badly built” and watch the ground below.

Fracking fluid is corrosive. If the tank holding it is not built with care, it leaks, and the fluid seeps down through the ground into the aquifer, the underground water pumped up for people to drink.
