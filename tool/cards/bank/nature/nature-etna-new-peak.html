Which crater is now Etna's highest point?

The South-East crater · 3,357 m

Since 16 February 2021, about 50 explosive paroxysms each piled pyroclastic material and lava onto its cone. The youngest of the four active craters overtook the North-East crater's 2018 record of 3,326 m: Etna grew 31 m in a few months.
