What does a male Pisaura mirabilis do if it has no prey?

A male Pisaura mirabilis meets a female. He carries something.

He wraps a pebble in silk.

Males court with a gift: prey caught and wrapped in silk. If he finds nothing edible, he balls silk around a small bit of rock or earth and offers that instead.

Tap the male, his gift and the female.
