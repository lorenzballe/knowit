TREE PLUMBING

How low can the pressure become in a tree’s xylem?

no evaporationfull sun

Water evaporating from the pores of the leaf’s cell walls tugs on the water behind it. The column holds together and is stretched: in an average tree it reaches about −15 atmospheres, while the air outside pushes in with just 1.
