Which way did a slab of Europe swing, 25 million years ago?

3Crust tears 5Sea floods in 1Breaks off 4Floor sinks 2Swings ↺

It hinges on the Gulf of Genoa. Aim it: which way does it swing?

It tore off Europe and swung anticlockwise on the Gulf of Genoa, like a compass. Behind it the crust sank and the sea poured in. That swing later helped shape the Apennines.

WHAT TO KEEP25–20 million years ago a slab of Europe swung anticlockwise and opened the Ligurian Sea.
