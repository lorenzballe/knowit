Why can water stay liquid at −10 °C, then freeze the moment you tap it?

Try it at home: purified water left about 2–3 hours in a freezer can stay liquid below 0 °C. This bottle is at −10 °C. Tap it.

WHAT TO KEEPFreezing gives off heat, so supercooled water turns only part to ice, about 1/80 per degree, then sits at 0 °C.
