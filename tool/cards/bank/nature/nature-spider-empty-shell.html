What is left after a spider eats its prey?

The empty shell

A spider doesn't chew its prey: it drinks it. Its juices turn the soft insides to liquid, and it sucks them up. The hard exoskeleton stays in the web, hollow.

Pick one, then watch the spider at dinner.
