SPIDER WEBS

Why does this spider string one thread from the web's center to its hideout?

It's an alarm line

The spider hides in a corner. When prey hits the web, this thread vibrates like a fishing line, telling it something is trapped.

Pick one, then say how sure you are.

WHAT TO KEEPThe spider doesn't watch its web: it feels it, through one thread.
