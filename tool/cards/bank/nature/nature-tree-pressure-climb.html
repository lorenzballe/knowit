What pressure difference must a 100 m tree make to lift water?

Drag the treetop up until it stands 100 m tall.

About 10 atmospheres

Each 10 m of water in the trunk adds about one atmosphere to overcome. At 100 m that stacks up to a 10‑atmosphere difference between roots and leaves. Evaporating leaves give suction, but that alone doesn’t explain it.

WHAT TO KEEPEvery 10 m of water height costs about 1 atmosphere: 100 m needs 10.
