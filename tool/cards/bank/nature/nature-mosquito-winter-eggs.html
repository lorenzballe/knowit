WINTER EGGS

Tiger mosquitoes can't stand the cold. So how are they back every spring?

Autumn. The cold is coming. Who makes it to spring?

Their eggs

The tiger mosquito (Aedes albopictus) dies in the cold. Before winter it lays eggs that resist freezing and drying out; they stay dormant and hatch when warmth returns.

Pick one, say how sure you are, then watch the winter.

WHAT TO KEEPThe adults die in winter; the eggs carry the species to spring.
