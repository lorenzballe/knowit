AIR BASIN

How does the shape of the Po Valley affect its air?

It stays trapped.

The Po Valley is a basin between the Alps and the Apennines, so its air is hardly exchanged. No strong wind carries the pollution away, and the stable air the Alps bring favours an inversion that holds it down.

Smoke rises from the plain. Pick where it ends up.
