LANDSLIDES

Soak a clay slope with water: does the landslide speed up?

Water fills the clay: friction drops, as if lubricated, and cohesion weakens, so the mass slides and flows. Drier ground moves slower. Steepness, the material and the volume matter too.

The dry block creeps. Pick what lots of water does.

WHAT TO KEEPSpeed depends on saturation, cohesion, slope and volume.
