DRAG TO GUESS

What happens to a tsunami's speed as it nears the coast?

One wave, drawn at equal time steps. Drag: near the shore, is it slower or faster?

It slows drastically, and grows taller.

As the water gets shallower, friction with the seabed and other factors slow it. Its energy can't vanish, so it goes into height.

WHAT TO KEEP Slow a flow but leave its energy nowhere to go, and it doesn't fade. It piles up.
