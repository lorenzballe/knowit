Drag the cube up or down until it holds everything the volcano threw out in ten days.

More than 245 million m³

626 × 626 × 626 is about 245 million, so the material would fill at least a cube 626 m a side. Falling ash and lapilli damaged towns such as Pompei, Nocera, Scafati and Poggiomarino.
