What are the six needles inside a mosquito's proboscis doing?

6 needles, 4 jobs

Two dark-green divaricators pry the skin open, two red saw-edged lancets cut like knives. A light-green stylet injects chemicals, and the blue syringe drinks the blood.

Tap each tool and watch that needle go to work.
