OYSTER REEFS

How much water can a single oyster filter in one day?

Hold the button: each square is one litre of murky sea.

110 to 190 litres a day

To feed, an oyster pulls sea water in and keeps the particles and algae, letting cleaner water out. Day after day that adds up, so oyster reefs work as natural biofilters for the whole ecosystem.

WHAT TO KEEPOne small oyster cleans 110–190 litres of water every day.
