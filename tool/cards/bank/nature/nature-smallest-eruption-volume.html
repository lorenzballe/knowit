The smallest step on the eruption scale: how much material comes out?

0.0001 km³ ≈ a 46 m cube

Step 0 is 0.0001 cubic kilometres: 100,000 m³, a cube about 46 m wide. Next to a kilometre cube it's a speck. Each step up is ten times more: Step 1 is 0.001 km³.
