THERMAL INVERSION

Why does Po Valley smog get stuck near the ground in winter?

Ground air warmervs air abovecolder

Under high pressure, mostly in winter, the air at the ground ends up colder, so heavier, than the air above it. Heavy air doesn't rise to mix: it stays there, stale, with the smog in it. In the Po Valley, a basin between Alps and Apennines with few strong winds, nothing sweeps it away.
