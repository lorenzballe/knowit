VESUVIUS

Why is the collapse of the eruptive column the most dangerous moment?

The fall unleashes pyroclastic flows

Once the column turns dark and heavy, it collapses. That sets off pyroclastic flows: rivers of incandescent gas and volcanic material that race down the slopes and destroy what they reach.

Tap the column to load it with heavy ash and rock.

WHAT TO KEEPWhen the column falls, the deadliest part starts: flows racing downhill.
