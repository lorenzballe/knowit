AIR & MOUNTAINS

Why does Milan's spot beside the Alps make its air so dirty?

The Alps stop the wind.

Strong winds would carry the smog away, but the Alps block them. The Po Valley is a basin between Alps and Apennines, so its air is rarely changed.

Send the north wind toward Milan and watch the smog.
