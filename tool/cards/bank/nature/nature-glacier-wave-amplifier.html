What effect does a landslide hitting a glacier have on tsunami size?

Flip between the two cases and watch the wave.

The rockslide alone was only 55 million m³. Striking the glacier tore loose ice and water too: the missing 245 million m³. Far more mass shoved into the bay makes a far taller wave.

WHAT TO KEEPA slide that hits ice drags the ice in with it: more volume, bigger wave.
