DEEP OCEAN

Flip Everest into the Mariana Trench: how much water is left above its peak?

Drag the line to where Everest's peak will stop. Then say how sure you are.

The trench's deepest point is 10,994 m below sea level. Everest is 8,848 m tall. Turned upside down and pushed in, its peak still stops about 2,000 m short of the bottom.

WHAT TO KEEPThe ocean's deepest point is deeper than Everest is tall, by about 2 km.
