TSUNAMIS

How does an asteroid's tsunami compare to a landslide's?

Same mechanism: a rocky body falls into the water and shoves it aside. The difference is violence. Space hurls the asteroid at incredible speed, like a stone thrown hard into a pond, so it sets off a train of far bigger, faster waves.

Flip the switch: let a cliff slide in, then hurl a rock from space.

WHAT TO KEEPSame rock-into-water push; the speed of the impact makes the monster.
