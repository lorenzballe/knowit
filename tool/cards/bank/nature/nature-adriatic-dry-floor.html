ICE AGE SEAS

Why was there no Adriatic Sea as we know it in the last ice age?

Ice age: the sea sits lower. Drag the sea down and watch where the coast ends up.

In the last glacial period the sea sat about 120 m lower, not 10 or 15, so much of today's Adriatic floor was dry land.

WHAT TO KEEPDrop the sea 120 m and the Adriatic coast moves about 200 km south.
