DRAG THE SEA

In the ice age, the northern Adriatic was land. How much lower was the sea?

Drag the sea down to your guess. Depths are drawn stretched.

About 120 m lower

A 120 m drop spread over 200 km is, on average, only about 0.6 m per km. So when the sea fell, the coast moved about 200 km south, and the Adriatic as we know it was gone.

WHAT TO KEEP On flat ground, a small change in height is a huge change in distance.
