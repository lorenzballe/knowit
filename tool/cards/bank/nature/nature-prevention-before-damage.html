PREVENTION

What's the main difference between how we handle landslides and how we should?

Flip the switch: spend the money after the slide, or before it.

Italy already knows its ground well: the maps exist. What's missing is the habit of putting big funds into works that cut the risk, further up the slope, before anything moves. Today institutions mostly step in once the damage is done.

WHAT TO KEEPPrevention money spent upstream beats repair money spent after.
