TREE WATER

Why must a tree's water tubes be completely free of air bubbles?

One bubble is enough.

Pulled this hard, the water should boil, yet it stays liquid while nothing gives the boiling a place to start. A bubble is that place: it grows and the column snaps. Xylem is water-filled from the start, so no air ever gets in.
