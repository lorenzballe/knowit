Sub-Plinian, 1631

The video takes the Sub-Plinian eruption of 1631 as its model. Today's cone has a crater about 450 m across and a summit of 1 281 m, but its base spreads about 20 km: more than 15 times wider than tall. Drawn true to scale, it is a broad swell, not a spike.
