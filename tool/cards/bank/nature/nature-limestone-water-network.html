Why do the Apennines act as natural water collectors?

Drag the rain cloud along the mountain. Let it rain on every crack.

The Apennines are mostly limestone, a carbonate rock that is heavily fractured. Water dissolves it, widening cracks into caves, tunnels and conduits. That is karst: under the mountains runs a dense network that gathers the water.

WHAT TO KEEPLimestone dissolves in water, so the mountain hollows itself into a water network.
