How would an explosive eruption of Marsili lose its energy?

Marsili sleeps on the seabed. Its top is about 500 m below the surface.

Into the water, not the sky.

Marsili sits about 500 m under the sea. Studies cited by INGV say an explosive eruption would spend all its energy in that 500 m water column: shock waves and heat in the sea, little left for the air.
