DROP EVEREST

Drop Everest into the Challenger Deep. How much water covers its peak?

Stand Everest on the bottom. Drag the flag to where its summit ends up.

Over 2 km of water on top

The Challenger Deep is over 10,900 m deep; Everest is about 8,848 m tall. Stand it on the bottom and its summit stays more than 2 km under the sea.10,900 m of trench, 8,848 m of Everest: over 2 km of sea still sits on the summit.

WHAT TO KEEPThe sea's deepest hole is deeper than the highest mountain is tall.
