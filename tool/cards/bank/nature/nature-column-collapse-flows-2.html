Why are pyroclastic flows deadlier than the eruption column itself?

Flip the switch: let the column cool and turn heavy.

While it is hot and light, the column rises. When its top cools and grows heavier, it falls back and races along the ground as a glowing cloud, at hundreds of km/h, scorching whatever it reaches.

WHAT TO KEEPThe worst danger is not the column going up, but the column coming down.
