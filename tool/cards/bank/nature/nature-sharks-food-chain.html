TAKE ONE OUT

No more sharks. What happens to the fish they eat?

About 100 million sharks and rays die every year. Take them off the top of this chain:

More. With no one hunting them, they explode.

Sharks sit at the top of the food chain and keep the fish below them in check. Take them away and those fish multiply unchecked, and the whole sea, and with it the planet’s ecosystem, tips out of balance.

WHAT TO KEEPThe predator at the top is the regulator: remove it and its prey explodes.
