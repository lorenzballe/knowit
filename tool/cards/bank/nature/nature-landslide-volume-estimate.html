TSUNAMI

How much rock did the first 1958 Alaska landslide drop into the bay?

5.5 × 10⁷ m³: 55 million m³

That is what the professor estimated for the rock that fell: a block about 380 m on each side. With the depth and shape of the bay's floor, it was a key number for simulating the wave.

Pick a volume: the block on the cliff shows how big it is.
