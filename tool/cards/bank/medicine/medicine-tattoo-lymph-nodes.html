Does a tattoo cause long-term inflammation in nearby lymph nodes?

The skin heals, but the story goes deeper. A study by the Institute for Research in Biomedicine in Bellinzona found that tattooed people generally have long-term inflammation in the lymph nodes near the tattoo. Good data on tattoos are still scarce, so take it as an early signal, not the last word.
