How much stronger is an MRI's magnetic field than Earth's?

Inside a common 1.5-tesla MRI you sit in a field about 30,000 times stronger than Earth's; a 3-tesla one doubles it. That is why it can pull metal objects with dangerous force. The scan itself is safe and non-invasive.
