What is a major consequence of radiation exposure?

Tap the cell to hit its DNA with radiation.

Radiation can cause cancer.

Radiation damages the DNA inside cells: those changes are mutations. They can ruin tissues and turn a healthy cell into a cancer cell.

WHAT TO KEEPRadiation mutates cells, and mutations can become cancer.
