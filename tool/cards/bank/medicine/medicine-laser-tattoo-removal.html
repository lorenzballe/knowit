TATTOO LASER

How does the laser hit only the ink and spare the skin?

◀ long pulsepicosecond pulse ▶

The dark ink soaks up the laser's light, while the skin around it lets that light pass. Each pulse lasts only picoseconds, so the pigment heats until it shatters before its heat has time to reach the skin.

Drag the slider to make the laser pulse shorter.

WHAT TO KEEPHit only what absorbs the light, and stop before the heat can spread.
