How did DuPont pick its “safe” C8 limit for drinking water?

DuPont scientists calculated about 0.6 parts per billion, then rounded it up to 1. At the time, 1 ppb was about the lowest level you could detect in water: one drop in 50,000 litres.

DuPont's scientists got 0.6 ppb. Tap to round it, as they did.
