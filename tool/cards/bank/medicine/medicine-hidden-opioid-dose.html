HIDDEN DOSE

Why is pink cocaine likelier to cause overdose than a pure drug?

Three pinches of the same pink mix. Tap the one hiding the least opioid.

You couldn't tell, and neither can anyone using it: in a mix you don't know what's inside, or how much. One pinch can carry far more of a potent opioid like fentanyl than the next, so it's easy to overdo it without knowing, and an overdose can kill.

WHAT TO KEEPIn an unknown mix, you can't know your dose of anything.
