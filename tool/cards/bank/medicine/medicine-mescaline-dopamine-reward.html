Why is mescaline considered to have low addiction potential?

No dopamine surge, little pull

Addictive drugs fire up the brain's dopamine reward system, the signal that says “do that again”. Mescaline barely switches it on, so its dependence potential is low. Low is not zero risk. (Curves are illustrative.)
