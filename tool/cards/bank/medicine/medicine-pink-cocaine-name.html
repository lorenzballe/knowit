Why is “pink cocaine” a misleading name for what's actually in it?

Just a brand name. Lab analyses almost never find cocaine or 2C-B in it, and when they do it's a tiny amount. It's mostly ketamine and MDMA, often with caffeine, opioids or other drugs added, and the mix changes from one clandestine lab to the next.

Run each test on the sample. Start with the two its name promises.
