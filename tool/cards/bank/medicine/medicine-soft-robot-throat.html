SOFT ROBOTS

How does a growing robot find the right tube inside your throat?

The robot lengthens from its tip.

Tap Grow, one step at a time, and watch the side branch.

The side branch is soft: when it hits the throat wall, it bends and slides along it into the opening. The body’s own shape does the steering, so no exact map is needed. Engineers call this passive, or mechanical, intelligence.
