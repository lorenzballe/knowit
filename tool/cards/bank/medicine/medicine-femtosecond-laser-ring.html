EYE SURGERY

What does the femtosecond laser do in the first phase of cataract surgery?

A perfect ring, then pieces

The lens sits in a thin bag called the capsule. The laser first cuts a neat circle in it, then cuts the cloudy lens into fragments so it can be taken out.

Pick one, then watch the laser work.

WHAT TO KEEPPhase one: the laser opens the capsule in a circle and breaks up the lens.
