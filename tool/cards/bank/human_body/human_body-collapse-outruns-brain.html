How fast does a submersible collapse, and could the brain notice?

The walls rushed inward at about 2,400 km/h, so the whole collapse took about one millisecond. The brain needs about 0.25 seconds to process a stimulus: 250 times longer. It was over before anyone inside could perceive it.
