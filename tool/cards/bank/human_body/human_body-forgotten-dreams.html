DREAM MEMORY

Why do we usually forget our dreams?

Low noradrenaline, no saving.

Noradrenaline is a brain messenger that helps fix memories for the long term. During sleep its level drops a lot, so the brain struggles to save what you dream.

Hold the button to sleep. Watch what gets saved.

WHAT TO KEEPDreams fade because noradrenaline, the brain's “save” signal, drops during sleep.
