X INACTIVATION

Why do some tissues in women show a stripy mosaic?

Tap each embryo cell: it shuts down Mum's or Dad's X at random.

Early on, each cell of the embryo randomly picks which X stays active: Mum's or Dad's. Every cell it makes keeps that choice, so each one grows into its own patch. Side by side, the patches make stripes.

WHAT TO KEEPA woman is a mosaic: some patches run on Mum's X, others on Dad's.
Source · Veritasium, “Why Women Are Stripey” (YouTube, 2014)
