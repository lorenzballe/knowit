Why does sweat cool you only when it evaporates?

Evaporation

To turn into vapour, sweat needs energy. It takes that energy, latent heat, from your skin, so the vapour carries your heat away and your temperature falls.

Drag the bead of sweat from the pore onto the warm skin.

WHAT TO KEEPWet skin alone doesn't cool you: sweat cools you as it evaporates and carries your heat off.
