SALT OVERLOAD

What can happen to you if you keep drinking sea water?

Salt in your blood: normal.

Too much salt: hypernatremia

Sea water pushes the salt in your blood too high and dries you out. One glass may do nothing at first. Keep drinking and it climbs: kidneys, brain, stomach, then coma or death.
