Which part of the brain switches on every time you dream?

Drag the moon through one night. Watch the brain each time a dream starts.

A region at the back of the brain

Light, deep or REM: whenever a dream starts, the same region at the back of the brain switches on. That is why researchers call it a real dream switch.

WHAT TO KEEPDreams switch on at the back of the brain, in any phase of sleep.
