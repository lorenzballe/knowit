X CHROMOSOMES

How is one X chromosome silenced in early female embryos?

In every cell, Mom’s X and Dad’s X fight a tiny molecular battle, and the winner is random. The loser is packed tight, marked on its histone tails, bound by proteins and tagged with methyl groups, so the cell shuts it down.

This embryo is 4 days old: about 100 cells. Start the battle.

WHAT TO KEEPAround day 4, each cell randomly packs away one X and keeps the other on.
