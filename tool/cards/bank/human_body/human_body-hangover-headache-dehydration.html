How does less vasopressin give you the morning-after headache?

Vasopressin 100%water kept in

Alcohol cuts vasopressin, the hormone that holds water in, so the body dries out. Vital organs pull fluid from everywhere, even the brain: its tissue shrinks and the fluid between brain and skull thins. The membranes holding it get pulled, and that hurts.

Slide vasopressin down, as a night of drinking does.
