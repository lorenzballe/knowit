Why might a light sweater use a salt-free deodorant?

Drag the slider down to a light sweater.

Aluminum salts work by plugging sweat pores. If you sweat little, there is almost nothing to plug, so their action is wasted. Not because the salts are harmful: they are harmless.

WHAT TO KEEPSalt-free is fine when there is little sweat to stop.
