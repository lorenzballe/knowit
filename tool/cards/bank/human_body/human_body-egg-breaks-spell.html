FERTILISATION

What happens to the egg's chemical signals once one sperm gets in?

Tap a sperm to send it through the egg's wall.

The egg breaks the spell.

The moment one sperm is inside, the egg switches off all the chemical signals that drew sperm in. The wall closes, and the rest literally bump into it and can't get in.

WHAT TO KEEPOne sperm in, signals off, wall shut: only one gets through.
