TURN THE HEAD

Why are passengers told to keep their heads still in hyper-G?

Level flight, normal gravity. Drag sideways to turn the passenger's head and watch the ear.

In hyper-G your vestibular system, the balance sense in your inner ear, becomes hypersensitive to movement. The same small head turn hits it far harder and brings on motion sickness. That's why the plane is nicknamed the vomit comet.

WHAT TO KEEPIn hyper-G, keep your head still and look straight ahead.
