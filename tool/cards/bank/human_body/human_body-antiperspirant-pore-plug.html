How do antiperspirants work?

A plug in the pore

Antiperspirants carry aluminium salts, such as aluminium chlorohydrate. With sweat they form a gel plug that blocks the duct, so the sweat stays in. Deodorants without these salts act on bacteria instead: fewer bacteria, less smell.
