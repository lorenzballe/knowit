LOVE CHEMISTRY

Love begins with four molecules shifting. Which rise, and which fall?

Three rise, one falls

As love begins, dopamine, noradrenaline and phenylethylamine go up while serotonin goes down. That swing of brain messengers cuts appetite and can cause insomnia.
