WARM ILLUSION

Alcohol makes you feel warm. So why does your body actually get colder?

Sober: warm core, normal skin.

Warm skin, colder core

Alcohol widens your blood vessels, so more blood flows to the skin. That feels cosy, but the skin is giving heat away, so your core temperature drops. In extreme cases: hypothermia.
