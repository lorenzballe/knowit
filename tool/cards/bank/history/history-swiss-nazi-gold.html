SWISS GOLD

Why did Switzerland keep trading with Nazi Germany?

Both pans weigh.

Surrounded, and dependent on imports from Germany and Italy, Switzerland traded to survive. But its banks also bought Reich gold it knew was partly taken from occupied countries and Holocaust victims, and kept Nazi leaders' money: that was interest.

Weigh each fact: survival or self-interest?
