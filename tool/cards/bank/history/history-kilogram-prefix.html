GRAVE TO KILO

Your scale says kilograms, your pills say milligrams. Why is the kilogram the only SI base unit with a prefix?

In 1793 France named its new unit of mass the grave: the mass of a litre of water.

WHAT TO KEEPThe kilogram is the 1793 grave under a gram’s name. That’s why a pill box says 500 mg, never 500 µkg.
