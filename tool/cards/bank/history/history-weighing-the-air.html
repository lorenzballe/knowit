SEPTEMBER 1648

How did climbing a mountain show that air has weight?

Pascal’s bet: if air has weight, a summit has less of it above, so the mercury should sink. Drag it to your guess for the top.

WHAT TO KEEPAir pressure is the weight of the air overhead, so crisp bags puff up high.
Source · F. Périer, letter to B. Pascal, 22 Sept 1648; Linda Hall Library, “Florin Périer”
