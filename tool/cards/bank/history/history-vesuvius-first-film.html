VESUVIUS 1944

What made the 1944 eruption unique in Vesuvius's historical record?

The first eruption ever caught on camera

It began at 16:30 on 18 March 1944. American and British troops had just reached Naples, freed from Nazi occupation, and their cameras made it the first Vesuvius eruption ever photographed and filmed.
