NAPOLEON'S OFFER

Why did San Marino turn down Napoleon's offer of more land?

Small is safe

In the late 1700s Napoleon offered San Marino more land. It said no: a bigger state gets noticed, and noticed states get attacked. Bothering no one and staying almost invisible kept it free.

Try both answers and watch who comes for it.
