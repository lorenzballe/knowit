IMOLA 1994

What did Senna do at the Tamburello corner?

Drag up or down: how much throttle did Senna use through the corner?

Tamburello was taken flat out. Nobody lifted there, least of all Senna. Yet the telemetry shows his throttle suddenly at half and the car slowing. After 1994 the track was changed, with two corners added to cut the speed there.
