What is the 'Atlantis of Italy'?

Drag the coast down, the way the volcanic ground of Baia did.

Baia: a Roman city 5–8 m under the sea

Baia sits in the Campi Flegrei, a volcanic area where the ground itself moves. The land slowly sank (bradyseism), and the sea closed over its mosaics, colonnades and houses.

WHAT TO KEEPBaia didn't fall into the sea: the volcanic ground carried it down.
