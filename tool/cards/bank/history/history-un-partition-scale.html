PARTITION 1947

Why did the 1947 UN partition plan seem unfair to Arabs?

Weight, not headcount

Arabs were 60% of the people, yet the scale tipped the other way.

Western interest and a united, influential community added weight. Arab division, and leaders who had backed Germany in WWII, took it away.

Arabs were 60% of the people, so the scale starts on their side. Tap each star to put it on the scale.

WHAT TO KEEPThe 1947 plan was weighed by allies and unity, not by who was the majority.
