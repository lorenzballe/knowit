Before Hong Kong's cage homes, how many crammed into Kowloon Walled City?

Drag along the scale to guess how many people lived inside. Each dot is 100 people.

Until its demolition in 1994, Kowloon Walled City crammed in 50,000 people, one of the most densely populated places on the planet. Today's cage homes, where 220,000 people live, carry on that crowding.
