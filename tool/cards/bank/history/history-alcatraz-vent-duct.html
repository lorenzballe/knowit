Escaping Alcatraz: where in the cell did the prisoners dig their hole?

Around the vent grille

A hole in the bare wall would be hard to hide. So the four chipped away around the grille of the ventilation duct. It opened onto an unguarded service corridor, and from there they could reach the roof.
