NORTH KOREA

How does North Korea classify its 25 million citizens?

A state ranking sorts every citizen into one of these layers. Tap each one.

Three classes, one ranking

The state runs a “sociopolitical classification” that puts all 25 million citizens in three classes. One in five, about 5 million, is elite. The other four in five are split between two lower tiers.

Tap each layer of the pyramid to see who is in it.
