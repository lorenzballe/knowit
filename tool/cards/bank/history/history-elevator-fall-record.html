RECORD FALL

What is the longest elevator fall anyone has ever survived?

75 floors: over 300 metres.

In 1945 a plane hit the Empire State Building, and one engine damaged an elevator's cables. Rescuers put the injured Betty Lou Oliver, on a stretcher, in that very elevator, and it fell. She survived.

Drag the elevator down the shaft, from the 75th floor to the ground.
