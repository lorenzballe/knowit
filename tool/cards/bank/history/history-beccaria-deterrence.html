What was Cesare Beccaria's main argument against the death penalty?

Cruel, and it doesn't work

In 1764 Cesare Beccaria argued the death penalty is not only inhumane but useless: the terror of execution does not reduce crime. Data has backed his idea over time.

Flip the switch: bring in the death penalty and watch crime.
