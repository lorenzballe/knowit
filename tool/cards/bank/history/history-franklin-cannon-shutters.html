FRANKLIN 1784

What bizarre measures did Benjamin Franklin propose in 1784 to save candle oil?

Cannons at dawn, a tax on shutters

Franklin saw Parisians sleep through sunny mornings and burn candles at night. His joke in the Journal de Paris: wake them with cannons, tax their shutters. He never moved a clock.
