STEP BY STEP

How did the US and USSR manage nuclear disarmament?

Both sides want fewer nukes. Pick how to disarm.

One all-or-nothing treaty is a single Prisoner’s Dilemma, and the safe move is to cheat. Cutting a few nukes a year, then checking, turns it into a repeated game. Every check builds trust for the next step.

WHAT TO KEEPTrust between rivals is built in small steps you can check.
