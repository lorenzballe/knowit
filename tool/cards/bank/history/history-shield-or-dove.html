SHIELD OR DOVE

Switzerland is neutral. Does that make it pacifist?

Tap the neon sign that hangs over Switzerland: the shield or the dove.

Neutrality is a political status: take no side in other countries' wars. Pacifism is a moral stance against all violence, and Switzerland never adopted it. Invaded, it fights, and it is very hard to conquer.

WHAT TO KEEPNeutral means taking no side, not refusing to defend yourself.
