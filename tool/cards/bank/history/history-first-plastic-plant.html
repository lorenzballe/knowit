The Plastic Waste Recycling Mill

Aluminium and glass were already reused in wartime, because they were scarce. Plastic needed another reason: we realised it can badly damage the environment. So it got a plant of its own, and this mill was the world’s first.

WHAT TO KEEPPlastic recycling began to protect nature, not to make up for scarcity.
