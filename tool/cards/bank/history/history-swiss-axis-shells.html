WWII TRADE

Neutral Switzerland's WWII factories made shells. Who mostly bought them?

Mostly Germany and Italy.

Swiss industry exported munitions and arms above all to the Axis, and the central bank bought Reich gold without asking where it came from, though part was taken from occupied countries and Holocaust victims. A useful neutral was worth more to Hitler than an invaded one.
