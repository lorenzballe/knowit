How high did the Tsar Bomba's mushroom cloud rise?

Drag the cloud's top to your guess. Then say how sure you are.

64 km high, 40 km wide

Dropped from about 10 km, it went off at 4 km. Its cloud rose 64 km high: roughly seven Everests stacked.
