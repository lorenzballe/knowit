How could a simple circuit test a guess about Enigma's plugboard?

The three rotors loop one of these letters back into itself. Which one?

One live wire: R is plugged to Z

The rotors alone turn Z back into Z, so the current closes into a single loop. A wrong guess sets many wires live, as if R were plugged to many letters at once. That's impossible, so every one of them is ruled out together.

Guess what R is plugged to: tap a letter's wire.

WHAT TO KEEPRight guess: one loop. Wrong guess: many live wires, a contradiction.
