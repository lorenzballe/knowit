PLASTIC

How did Bakelite change what plastic is made from?

Before: plastic was made from cellulose, a material from plants. It caught fire easily.

From plants to oil and gas

Early plastic came from cellulose, a plant material. Bakelite (1907) used oil and methane gas instead: far more abundant and far less flammable, so plastic could be made in huge amounts.
