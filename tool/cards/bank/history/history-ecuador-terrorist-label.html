How does Ecuador's emergency decree classify its 22 criminal groups?

Terrorists, to be neutralized

The decree lists 22 organized-crime groups and officially defines them as terrorists. That label lets the government treat them under the laws of armed conflict: an enemy to neutralize, not just suspects to arrest.
