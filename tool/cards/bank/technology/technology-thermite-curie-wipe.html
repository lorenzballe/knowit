How can thermite be used to destroy data?

This drive stores data as a map of tiny magnets. Press and hold to light the thermite. Let go to let it cool.

Past the Curie temperature, magnets lose their magnetism.

The data was only a pattern of magnets. Once the heat erases it, cooling does not bring it back, so the data is unrecoverable. That is why thermite's heat is used to destroy information.
