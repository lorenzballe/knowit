SWORD STEEL

Why can a Japanese sword slice a bullet in half?

Hard steel, layered grain

The sword is high-carbon steel with a layered grain. That makes the edge very hard, and it stays sharp, so it parts the bullet’s core instead of giving way.

Blades seen from above, edge toward the bullet. Fire and watch in slow motion.
