Reuse beats rebuild

Australia didn't build a new vehicle: in 2017 it converted a two-car convoy from 1949. Reusing it skipped the cost and time of a new train, and proved old trains can go solar too.

WHAT TO KEEPConverting what already runs is cheaper and faster than building new.
