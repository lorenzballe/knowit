RALLY JUMPS

Steep jump or gentle hump: when does a rally driver press the throttle?

Tooth
Hump

Opposite pedals.

Steep tooth: slow down going in, then a sharp kick of throttle at the top sets the flight straight. Soft hump: go in fast and never lift on the ramp, so the car flies parallel to the road and all four wheels land together.

Pick a pedal plan for each jump, then hit Jump.
