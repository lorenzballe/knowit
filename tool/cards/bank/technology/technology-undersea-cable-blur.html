UNDERSEA CABLE

Why did early undersea telegraph cables blur dots and dashes together?

Tap to lay more cable under the sea and send · — · again.

A long cable soaks up charge: its capacitance grows with its length. Each pulse must fill it before the far end rises, then drain it before it falls, so pulses stretch and blur into each other.

WHAT TO KEEPLonger cable, more capacitance: pulses stretch until dots and dashes merge.
