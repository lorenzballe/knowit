DRAW IT

A crisp dot-dash-dot enters a long undersea cable. What comes out?

Lengthened and distorted

Long cables increased capacitance, so the pulses were stretched and blurred, and dots were hard to tell from dashes. Scientists debated why. William Thomson pictured signals flowing like water through a rubber tube. Heaviside and Fitzgerald argued that the fields around the wire carried the energy, and their view proved correct.

WHAT TO KEEP Over a long enough line, a message can smear until a dot looks like a dash.
