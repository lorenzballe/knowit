CLUTCH

How can one bending part act as a clutch that engages only at speed?

Speed makes it reach out.

Spinning flings the heavy outer parts outward, and because the arms bend, they swing out further the faster it goes. Fast enough, they press on the drum and drag it round: a centrifugal clutch, the way a chainsaw engages its chain.

Drag the speed slider right and watch the gap to the drum.
