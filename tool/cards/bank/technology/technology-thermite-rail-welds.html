How much railway is built each year using thermite welds?

Drag to add welds. Each one pours 2.5 cm of new rail.

Every year about 2 million rail welds are made with thermite. Each one fills a gap of about 2.5 cm with liquid steel. Add them all up: about 50 km of rail a year.

WHAT TO KEEP2 million welds × 2.5 cm ≈ 50 km of rail born liquid each year.
