PICK TWO

Smiths fold the steel again and again. Which two things does that buy?

Pick the two real reasons. Then say how sure you are.

 

It spreads the impurities, and it gives the steel a grain.

Each fold spreads the flecks thinner.

The impurities end up spread throughout, so no spot is weak, and the grain reinforces the blade in the direction it will be hit.

WHAT TO KEEP Spread a flaw thin enough, everywhere, and it stops being a weak point.
