FLIP SHIP

On a ship that flips upright, how does the toilet still work?

Drag to tip the ship upright. Then lock the pivots and try again.

On FLIP, fridges, stoves, pantries and even toilets were mounted on pivots. As the ship swung from horizontal to vertical, each one turned on its pin and stayed upright.

WHAT TO KEEPWhen the whole room turns 90°, the furniture turns back.
