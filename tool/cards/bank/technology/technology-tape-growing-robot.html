DRAG THE TAPE

Lay sticky tape across the floor. Which one makes it across?

Drag the tape left into the middle of the floor. Then pick.

Only the robot gets across.

Powered by compressed air, the robot grows from its tip, so it grows over the tape instead of getting stuck to it. Something like a car just gets stuck.

WHAT TO KEEP A thing that grows from its tip goes over glue rather than getting stuck in it. Nothing holds it back.
Source · Veritasium, “This Unstoppable Robot Could Save Your Life” (2021)
