What was the first innovation that changed Micromouse?

Corner by corner
On the diagonal

Running on the diagonal

Mitee Mouse 3 was the first to cut straight through a zigzag instead of turning every corner: a shorter road with no stops. To fit, mice had to shrink below 11 cm wide, and maze designers now add diagonals on purpose.
