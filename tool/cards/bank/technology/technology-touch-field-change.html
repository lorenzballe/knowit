TOUCH SCREENS

How does the phone know exactly where you touched?

The screen keeps an electric field across it and checks it all the time. Your body conducts electricity, so your fingertip changes the field right where it lands. The phone spots that one change and reads it as a touch at that exact point.

Tap anywhere on the phone's screen. Then try the plastic stylus.
