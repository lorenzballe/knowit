What happens if a growing inflatable robot gets punctured?

50

It keeps growing.

A needle makes a hole, not a pop. Air hisses out, but if you push air in hard enough, the pressure stays high and the tip keeps moving.

Set the air pressure, then hit Grow. Three needles wait on the way.
