What is the Micromouse equivalent of the Fosbury flop?

Old mice turned every corner square. Drag from the mouse to aim one straight line, then let go.

Mice used to drive cell by cell, turning square at every corner. Mitee Mouse 3 was the first to drive diagonals, cutting straight through the zigzag between the posts. Not new technology: a new way of seeing the maze.
