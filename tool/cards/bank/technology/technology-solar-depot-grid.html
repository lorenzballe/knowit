What two jobs does the depot's 30 kW solar system do?

Drag the sun across the day and watch the train charge.

Two jobs: it charges the train, then sells the rest.

With its storage batteries, the 30 kW system quickly recharges the whole convoy. What the train can't take is sold to the local grid, so the depot works as a small power plant.

WHAT TO KEEP Panels with one user waste what that user can't take. A second buyer, the grid, puts the rest to work.
