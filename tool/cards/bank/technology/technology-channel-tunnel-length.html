How long is the Eurotunnel's stretch under the sea, and why does it matter?

Drag the drill from England to France and watch the kilometres pile up.

39 km under the sea

No rail tunnel in the world has a longer stretch beneath the sea. Digging that far under the water took seven years and about 4,000 workers following one plan. Their secret weapon was the geology below the seabed.

WHAT TO KEEPThe Eurotunnel runs 39 km under the sea: the longest undersea rail stretch on Earth.
