BASE TUNNEL

What's inside the Turin–Lyon base tunnel, and how often are its tubes linked?

A train stops in trouble. Drag it along its tunnel and find the way out.

The tunnel is really two single-track tubes side by side, joined every 333 m by cross-passages. Wherever a train stops, the other tube is never more than about 170 m away: a safe exit, and a link for ventilation.
