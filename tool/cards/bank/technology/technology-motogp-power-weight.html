What power would a 1,300 kg car need to accelerate like a MotoGP bike?

Drag up to pile power on the car, 100 hp a brick, until it would pull like the bike.

About 2,000 hp, by Rossi's reckoning.

Same pull, rough figure: Rossi says 2,000 hp, then “more or less 1,800”. His 168 kg is a dry weight; with fluids he counts about 175 kg, which lowers the car's number.

WHAT TO KEEP Halving the weight does what doubling the engine does: compare power per kilo, never power alone.
