UNDERSEA CABLES

Iron armor made undersea telegraph cables stronger. Why did it make signals worse?

Iron raised the capacitance.

Iron is a good conductor. Wrapped around the insulated core, it turned the cable into a capacitor, and that hindered the electromagnetic fields that carry the signal. It's why most power lines today hang high in the air.
