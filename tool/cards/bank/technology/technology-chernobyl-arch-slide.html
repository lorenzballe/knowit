How do you put a new lid over Chernobyl's old sarcophagus?

Build it beside, then slide it over.

The new containment is a giant steel arch built next to the old sarcophagus, not on top of it. Once finished, it slides sideways into place and covers the old structure like a lid.

Drag the steel arch along its rails onto the old sarcophagus.
