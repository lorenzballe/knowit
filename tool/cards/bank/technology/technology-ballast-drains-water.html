How does the ballast layer protect the track from water damage?

Make it rain on the stones, then on bare soil.

The gaps between the stones let rain flow straight through and away from the track. Without them the water stays put, the soil turns to mud and the sleepers sink.

WHAT TO KEEPBallast is a drain: no standing water, no mud under the rails.
