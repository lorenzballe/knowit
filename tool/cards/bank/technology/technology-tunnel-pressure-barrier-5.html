TUNNEL SAFETY

Why doesn't tunnel smoke flow into the emergency escape passage?

Higher pressure inside.

Fans keep the escape passage at a slightly higher pressure than the road tunnel. Its air keeps pushing out through the door, a moving wall smoke can't cross, so the air inside stays clean to breathe.

Hold the button and watch the smoke at the doorway.
