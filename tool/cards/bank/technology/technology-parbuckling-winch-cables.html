What is the parbuckling technique used to right the ship?

WINCH0%

Cables + winches = a slow, steady turn

Massive cables are fixed to the hull, and winches reel them in, rotating the ship upright. The ship turns only as far as the cable is reeled, so every degree is under control. That precision is why the method was chosen for the Costa Concordia.

Drag the winch to reel in the cables. Stop anytime: the ship stops too.

WHAT TO KEEPParbuckling: pull a ship upright with cables and winches, slowly and precisely.
