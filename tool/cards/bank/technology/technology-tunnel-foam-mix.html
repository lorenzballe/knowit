TUNNEL SAFETY

How does a tunnel's fire system turn plain water into foam?

Pipe, then mix, then spray.

Water reaches the room of the hit sector through special pipes. There it is mixed with 3% fire foam, then rises into the tunnel and is sprayed. The tunnel has 61 such rooms, about one every 127 m.

Drag the 3% foam into the sector room under the fire.

WHAT TO KEEPWater comes by pipe, the room adds 3% foam, the tunnel gets the spray.
