ROBOT LOGISTICS

How are heavy components moved around the shipyard at night?

Robot rovers, alone

At night, automatic rovers carry the parts. Each one calculates the shortest route and steers around obstacles in total autonomy: no driver needed.

Tap the floor to drop a crate in the rover's way (0/3).

WHAT TO KEEPNight shift: the rover finds the shortest path and dodges what's in it.
