DARK WEB

Why is the Dark Web almost completely untraceable?

Drag your wrapped message onto relay 1, then try to trace it back.

The trail breaks at every hop.

Darknet traffic hops through several relays, wrapped in layers of encryption. Each relay peels one layer and knows only its neighbours. The site sees just the last relay, so ordinary IP tracing never reaches you.

WHAT TO KEEPMany encrypted hops, each knowing only its neighbours: nobody sees the whole path.
