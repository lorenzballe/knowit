BUILT ON ICE

How do Yakutsk's buildings keep from melting the frozen ground?

Drag the house up, off the frozen ground.

About 1.5 m up

A heated house touching the ground would warm and thaw its top layer, and the building could sink. Lifted about 1.5 m, its heat stays in the air, while piles driven about 10 m down hold it up.

WHAT TO KEEPOn permafrost, keep the heat off the ground: build on stilts.
