HYDRO POWER

How much of Italy's August power could one very rainy summer supply?

Almost 90%

An unexpectedly rainy summer filled Italy's rivers and reservoirs. Dams turned the extra water into power, so in August hydro nearly covered 90% of demand and other sources were briefly barely needed.
