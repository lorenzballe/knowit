INDUCTION

How does an induction hob heat a pot with no flame?

The coil carries alternating current, so its magnetic field keeps changing. That changing field drives an electric current inside the metal of the pot's bottom. The current heats the metal itself: the pot is the heater.

Drag the pot down onto the hob.

WHAT TO KEEPCoil current → changing field → current in the pot → heat in the metal.
