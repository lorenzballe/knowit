HEAT IT UP

Did the fires need to melt the steel to bring the towers down?

No. The steel only had to soften.

Steel doesn't have to turn liquid to fail. As it heats it weakens until it can no longer hold the floors above. NIST's simulations point to that weakening, not melting at about 1500 °C, as the trigger of the collapse.

Drag along the heat bar to warm the beam.
