MICRO MACHINES

How are bending machines made at microscopic size?

This silicon wears a film that reacts to light. Make a machine.

Like computer chips: photolithography.

Light through a mask draws the whole shape at once, then etching carves it out. A machine that moves by bending is just one shape, so it can be made this way, far smaller than one with parts.

WHAT TO KEEPMachines that bend can be printed like chips, so they can be microscopic.
