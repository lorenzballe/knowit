Carbon fibre is super strong. So why is it risky deep underwater?

Carbon fibre is light and very strong when you pull it. When it is squeezed, its thin fibres can bend sideways and buckle, and when that starts is hard to predict. A deep-sea hull like Titan's is squeezed at up to 300 times atmospheric pressure.

Drag the pressure up to 300×. Then try Pull, or a new panel.
