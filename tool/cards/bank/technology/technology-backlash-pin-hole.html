HOLD, THEN GUESS

When the handle turns back, what does the pointer do first?

Hold, then let go. The choices unlock after that.

It waits, then follows.

A hinge is a pin in a hole, with some give. When the motion reverses, the pointer waits before it follows, so it no longer matches the handle. That delay is backlash, a classic source of error in traditional mechanisms. Parts that bend instead of pivoting don’t suffer from it.

Source · Howell, Compliant Mechanisms (Wiley, 2001).
