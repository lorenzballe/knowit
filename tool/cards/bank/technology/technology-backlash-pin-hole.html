HOLD, THEN GUESS

When the handle turns back, what does the pointer do first?

Hold, then let go. The choices unlock after that.

It waits, then follows.

A hinge is a pin in a hole, with some give. When the motion reverses, the pointer waits before it follows, so it no longer matches the handle. That delay is backlash, the main cause of inaccuracy in traditional mechanisms. Parts that bend instead of pivoting don’t suffer from it.

Source · Veritasium, “Why Machines That Bend Are Better” (YouTube, 2019)
