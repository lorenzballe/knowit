SOFT ROBOTS

What happens when you retract a vine robot in a big open area?

The tube grew by turning inside out. Pull its tail through the core to bring it home.

The robot should ungrow, slipping back inside itself. In open space nothing holds the tube straight, so it buckles and coils into an ugly tangle instead. Internal rollers guide it back in cleanly.
