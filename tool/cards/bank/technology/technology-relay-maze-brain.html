RELAY MAZE

How was the first intelligent mouse's brain built?

The maze was the brain.

Under the floor sat a computer made of telephone relay switches. The mouse was just a magnet on wheels, following an electromagnet that the relays moved.

Watch it find its way. Where does the thinking happen?

WHAT TO KEEPThe clever part was in the maze, not in the mouse.
