What electronic aid stops the rear wheel spinning when you accelerate?

GAS

Traction control

Sensors see when the rear wheel turns faster than the bike moves: it is slipping. The control unit then cuts engine torque, so leaned over at full gas the tyre keeps its grip. That's why, as Rossi says, rear slides on acceleration are now much rarer.

Open the gas with TC off, leaned over. Then switch TC on and try again.

WHAT TO KEEPTraction control trims power the moment the rear wheel starts to slip.
