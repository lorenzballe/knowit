DAM BUILDING

Why was the Hoover Dam poured as many separate blocks?

Setting concrete makes its own heat. In one giant pour the outside cools and shrinks while the core stays hot, and that thermal stress cracks it. Blocks of limited length and width let the inside cool too.

Pick how to pour the concrete and watch its heat escape. Then try another way.

WHAT TO KEEPBig concrete cools from the outside in: cut it small so the inside cools too.
