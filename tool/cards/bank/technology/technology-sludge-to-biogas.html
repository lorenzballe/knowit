How does a sewage plant turn its sludge into its own energy?

Sludge → biogas → power

At the DAC plant in Genoa every tank is sealed, to keep the smells in. Inside, the sludge is treated so it also gives off biogas, and the plant reuses that gas itself: up to 50% of the energy it needs.
