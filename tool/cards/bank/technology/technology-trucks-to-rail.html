FREIGHT SHIFT

What changes when Alpine freight moves from trucks to the Turin–Lyon rail line?

Each load that rides the new line is a heavy truck that no longer climbs the Alpine roads: about 1 million of them. Fewer trucks burning diesel means roughly 3 million tonnes less CO₂ every year.

Drag the switch from ROAD to RAIL to move the freight.
