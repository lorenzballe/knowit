MADE HOW

How is the coil cassette of an induction hob given its shape?

1000 tons on a sheet under 1 mm

The cassette starts as a thin flat steel sheet. A 1000-ton press molds it until it has the shape it needs. Then it is filled by hand with the electronics and the copper coil.

Drag the press down and squeeze the steel sheet.

WHAT TO KEEPThe coil's box is pressed, not built: thin steel plus enormous force.
