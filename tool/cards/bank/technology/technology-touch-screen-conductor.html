TOUCH SCREENS

Why does a touch screen work when you touch it?

Sensor: field steady

Under the glass, a layer of indium tin oxide carries current. Your skin conducts, so your finger gives that current a path to the ground and changes the electric field at that spot. The phone reads the change as a touch right there.
