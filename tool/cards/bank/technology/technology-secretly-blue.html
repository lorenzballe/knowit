Scratch the yellow coat off a white LED. What colour is the light underneath?

Lit · rub the yellow

A white LED, switched on and seen from above. Rub its yellow coat away with your finger.

WHAT TO KEEPMost white LEDs are blue LEDs in yellow coats: no bright blue, no white light.
