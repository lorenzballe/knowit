RAILWAYS

Why are small gaps left between rail sections?

Room to grow in the heat

Steel shrinks in the cold and grows in the heat: 40 °C warmer, a 12 m rail is 6 mm longer. With no gaps, people expected kilometres of joined rail to buckle sideways and derail trains.
