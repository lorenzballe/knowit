EARTHQUAKES

Japan gets big earthquakes all the time. Why is damage so low?

MAGNITUDEM 6.0

Prevention pays.

Japan spends a lot of money on prevention, and spends it efficiently. Its buildings are designed to flex, not break. So a magnitude 6, 7 or 8 still does damage, but far less than elsewhere.

Drag the magnitude up to 8 and watch both cities.

WHAT TO KEEPMoney spent before a quake buys far smaller losses after it.
