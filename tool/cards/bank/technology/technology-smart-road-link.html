Why will the Salerno–Reggio Calabria be Italy's first hands-free motorway?

The road talks to the car

A last-generation wireless network will link road, user and vehicle, making it Italy's first smart road. The irony: a motorway famous for delays, slowed by money and politics but also by cliffs over the sea and ever-changing rock.
