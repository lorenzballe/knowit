How do new heated-tobacco sticks get hot from the inside?

magnetic field

Flip the switch to run current through the coil, then watch the middle of the stick.

Heat from the inside.

The coil’s changing magnetic field heats the hidden metal foil, up to 350 °C at most, so the tobacco warms from its centre and gives off smoke.
