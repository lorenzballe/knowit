MASS BALANCE

Sand and charcoal go into the furnace. How heavy is the block that comes out?

Drag the orange block to the size you expect. Area is weight.

100 kg. Under 8% of what went in.

And that block is steel, iron and slag together. Only about a third of it is good enough for a sword.

WHAT TO KEEP Judge a furnace by what comes out: 1,284 kg in, one 100 kg block out.
