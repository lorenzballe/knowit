How much pressure is needed to lift a human?

Just 0.1 atmosphere.

Pressure is a push on each patch of surface. A tenth of an atmosphere is gentle, but spread over a whole square meter the pushes add up to about 1000 kilograms, and the pad stays soft.

The air inside pushes at a fixed 0.1 atm. Drag to make the pad wider.

WHAT TO KEEPLifting force is pressure times area: go wide, not hard.
