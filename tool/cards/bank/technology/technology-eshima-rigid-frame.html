How long and tall is Eshima Ohashi, and why is it special?

1.7 km long, about 45 m high

Spread over 1.7 km, a 45 m rise is a gentle slope: the steep look is an illusion. Deck and piers are locked into one rigid frame, the largest in Japan, so the load spreads evenly with no moving parts.
