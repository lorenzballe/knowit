ENERGY FLOW

What is the simple expression for electromagnetic energy flux?

S = (1/μ₀) E × B

S is the cross product of the electric and magnetic fields, times one constant: 1 over μ₀, the permeability of free space. It always points at right angles to both, so turning B turns where the energy flows, and flipping E flips it.
