MANY WORLDS

In Many-Worlds, before you open the box, is the cat alive or dead?

Pick the Many-Worlds answer, then say how sure you are.

Already one or the other. In Many-Worlds, you split first.

In Many-Worlds, the detector tangles with the air and light in the box almost at once, and that splits the world, and you, into two identical copies. Each copy's cat is already alive or already dead. Opening the box only tells you which copy you are.

WHAT TO KEEP Not knowing an outcome is not the same as it being undecided.
