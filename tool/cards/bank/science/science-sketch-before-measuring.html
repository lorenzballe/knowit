What is the first step in constructing a geological section?

The map shows a fault and two fold axes, but the section below is still flat. Tap the three marks on the map.

First, a hand sketch with no measures at all: this fault must reach the surface here, it's normal, here an anticline, there a syncline. That keeps the whole picture coherent. Only then do you project faults and fold axes precisely.

WHAT TO KEEPSketch the big picture by hand first; measure after.
