LAVA & WATER

What happens to water the instant it touches lava?

Tap the picture to drop the water on the lava.

Lava is so hot that water turns to steam the moment it touches it. The steam expands inside the molten rock and blows bubbles, so the lava seems to breathe: a violent, explosive reaction.

WHAT TO KEEPWater on lava doesn't cool it quietly: it flashes to steam and bursts.
