Why did discovering the neutron in 1932 change nuclear research?

Protons in: 0 of 0Neutrons in: 0 of 0

Pick a particle, then hold the button to fire it at the nucleus.

The nucleus is positive, so a proton (+) is pushed away before it arrives. The neutron, found in 1932, is about 0.1% heavier than a proton but has no charge: nothing pushes it back, so it slips straight in.

WHAT TO KEEPNo charge, no push: the neutron could reach the nucleus and split it.
