HYDROGEN BLAST

What actually blew up the Fukushima reactor buildings?

Hydrogen met air.

With no cooling, the water and fuel overheated and made lots of steam and hydrogen. The pressure rose, so technicians vented steam, and the hydrogen went out with it. In the building it mixed with air and exploded.

Hold the button to keep heating the fuel with no cooling.
