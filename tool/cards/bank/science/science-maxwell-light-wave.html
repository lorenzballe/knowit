What did Maxwell discover light is actually made of?

Here is light's electric field. Draw its magnetic field in the lower strip.

In the 1860s and 70s, Maxwell realised light is an electric field and a magnetic field oscillating together. They stand at right angles to each other and travel forward as one electromagnetic wave.

WHAT TO KEEPLight is electric and magnetic fields waving together, at right angles, moving forward.
