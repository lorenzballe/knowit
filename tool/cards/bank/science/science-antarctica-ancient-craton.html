DEEP TIME

How do we know rocks under Antarctica are over 3 billion years old?

Ice lifted away. Drill into East Antarctica.

Radiometric dating of East Antarctica's igneous and metamorphic rocks gives ages above 3 billion years. This ancient shield, a craton, sits under younger layers; West Antarctica is built very differently.

Drag the drill bit down through East Antarctica.
