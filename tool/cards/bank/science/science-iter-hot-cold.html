How can one machine be the hottest and coldest place in the universe?

The plasma is 150 million °C. Drag the marker: how cold must its magnets be?

−269 °C

At 150 million degrees no material can hold the plasma, so magnets hold it floating inside the hollow ring. Those superconducting magnets keep their magnetic properties only when chilled to −269 °C.

WHAT TO KEEPIn ITER, the hottest thing is held in place by the coldest thing.
