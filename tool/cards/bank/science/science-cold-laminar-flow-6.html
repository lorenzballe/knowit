Which water is more likely to pour in one smooth stream, cold or hot?

Drag to open the tap, tap its knob for cold or hot. Then pick.

Probably cold.

A stream stays smooth while its Reynolds number stays low: speed weighed against viscosity. Cold changes the water's viscosity and can also slow the flow. Both lower that number.

WHAT TO KEEP Smooth or rough is a ratio, not just a speed: cool the water and the stream tends to calm.
