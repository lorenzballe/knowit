FAULT LINES

Why is a tunnel under the Strait riskier than a bridge?

A tunnel runs through the rock, so it inevitably crosses several faults. If one slips and makes a step of 2–4 metres, as in 1908, the tube is cut.

A bridge only touches the ground at its towers, and it can be designed to absorb the step far more easily.

Slip the fault under the tunnel, then switch to the bridge.
