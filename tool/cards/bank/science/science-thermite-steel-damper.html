RACE TWO POTS

How is the temperature of thermite controlled?

Tap the right pot to drop in pieces of pure steel. Then ignite both.

The steel doesn't react. It absorbs heat as it melts, so the mix around it stays cooler, burns slower and throws far fewer sparks. That's how thermite can be lit indoors.

WHAT TO KEEPSteel that melts but doesn't burn soaks up the heat: it sets thermite's rate and temperature.
