This thermite has pieces of pure steel mixed in. What does the steel do?

Tap the picture to drop the steel in. Then pick what it does.

It never reacts. It just melts.

The steel stays out of the reaction, but it soaks up heat as it melts. That helps control how fast the thermite burns and how hot it gets. It is one part of a recipe so dialed in that thermite can be lit indoors.

WHAT TO KEEP You can help tame a fierce reaction without joining it: give its heat something to melt.
