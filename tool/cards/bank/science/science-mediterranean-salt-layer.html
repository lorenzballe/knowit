SALT GIANT

How thick is the salt left under the Mediterranean when it dried out?

2 to 3 km of salt

Not a thin crust: a giant salt bank. Drilling in the 1970s (the Deep Sea Drilling Program) hit it under the seafloor and dated it to about 5.6 million years ago. Only a sea that dried out could leave that much.

Tap “Dry the sea”: each tap lays down half a kilometre of salt.
