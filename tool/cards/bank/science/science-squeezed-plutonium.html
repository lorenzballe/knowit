CRITICAL MASS

Why does compressing plutonium lower its critical mass?

Closer nuclei, more hits.

Squeezing packs the same nuclei closer together, so a stray neutron hits one before it can escape, and each split sets off more. At normal density 6 kg of plutonium-239 won't explode; crushed by explosives, it can.
