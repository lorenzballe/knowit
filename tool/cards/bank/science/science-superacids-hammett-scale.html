SUPERACIDS

Why can't the pH scale measure the strongest acids?

Superacids are so strong they protonate even weak bases, so pH stops being accurate. Chemists switch to the Hammett acidity function, H₀: the lower, the stronger. Pure sulfuric acid sits at −12, fluoroantimonic acid at about −28.
