TAP TO HEAT

How hot does the Tsar Bomba's core get, next to the Sun?

Tap the dial. Each tap makes it ten times hotter.

Huge pressure sets off fission in a small plutonium sphere, releasing an enormous amount of heat: up to 100 million °C. The Sun's surface is about 5,500 °C, so that is roughly 18,000 times hotter.

WHAT TO KEEPFor an instant, the bomb's core is far hotter than the surface of the Sun.
