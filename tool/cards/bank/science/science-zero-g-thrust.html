ZERO-G

How does a plane fall exactly like a pen in a vacuum?

Thrust cancels drag. Only gravity is left.

Air drag would slow the plane, so the pen would drift ahead of it. Pilots add just enough thrust to cancel drag, so plane and pen both fall at 9.8 m/s², and nothing presses on you.

Slide the thrust, then fly. Keep the pen floating.
