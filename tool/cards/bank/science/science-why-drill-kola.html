DEEPEST HOLE

Why drill the world's deepest hole on Russia's Kola Peninsula?

The ancient crust is right there

In most places, rock over 2 billion years old lies buried deep under younger layers. Kola is a geological oddity: the young cover is very thin, so the drill hits Proterozoic crust almost at once, and it only gets older on the way down.
