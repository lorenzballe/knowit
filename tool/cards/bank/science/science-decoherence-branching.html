How does the air turn one quantum wave into separate worlds?

Hits: 0 / 30Interference: 100%

It gets entangled.

Every air molecule or photon that bumps the atom carries off a record of it: one version for “decayed”, one for “not decayed”. Once the environment holds both records, the two versions can no longer interfere. The wave function of the universe has branched into two slightly different copies.
