IRON SAND

How rich in iron oxide can Japanese iron sand get?

About 80 % iron oxides

Running water carries the light sand downstream; the heavy iron grains stay behind. Done in engineered river channels, this leaves sand that is about 80 % iron oxides by weight: richer than high-quality iron ore, and with fewer impurities.
