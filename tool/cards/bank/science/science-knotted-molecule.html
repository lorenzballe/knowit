MOLECULAR KNOTS

When was the first synthetic knotted molecule created?

1989

Chemist Jean-Pierre Sauvage tied molecules around copper ions into a trefoil knot. The knot stops the atoms from unfurling, trapping them in higher energy states that give the molecule new properties.

Drag the copper ion into the ring and watch the strand.

WHAT TO KEEPTie a molecule in a different knot and you get a different material.
