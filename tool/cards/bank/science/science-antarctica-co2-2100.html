CO₂ IN 2100

What CO₂ levels are projected for 2100, and why does Antarctica care?

By 2100, scenarios put CO₂ between 730 and 1,020 parts per million. Levels like these could dramatically speed up Antarctic melting, pushing the continent toward a far warmer, even tropical, climate.
