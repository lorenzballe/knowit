Why did Chernobyl's reactor number four blow its top off?

Heat → steam → bang

Runaway fission made heat far faster than the water could carry it away. The water flashed into steam, and the pressure ripped the reactor open, throwing out radioactive isotopes.

Hold the button to let fission heat the core. Watch the water.
