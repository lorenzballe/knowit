VOLCANO DRILLS

Why can't a calm evacuation drill test a real Campi Flegrei escape?

A real eruption means chaos.

Everyone leaves at once, on their own. A small, voluntary, assisted drill never feels that stress, so it can't expose the problems a drill is meant to find. Like F1 tests: run a few laps at race speed.

Flip each switch from drill to real, and watch the road.
