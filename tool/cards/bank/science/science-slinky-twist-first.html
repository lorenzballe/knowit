FALLING SLINKY

What signal reaches the slinky bottom first?

A twist, but the bottom stays put

When the top is let go, a twisting signal rushes down ahead of the collapse, so the bottom coil starts to rotate. A twist releases no tension, so the bottom keeps sitting there until the collapse arrives.

Pick what you think, then watch the slinky let go.

WHAT TO KEEPThe first signal to arrive isn't always the one that lets go.
