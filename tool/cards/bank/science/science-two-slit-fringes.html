LIGHT WAVES

What pattern appears on the screen when two narrow slits are used?

Drag along the screen to see where the light lands.

One bright middle, not two lines. There both trips are equal, so the waves arrive in step and add up. Half a wave longer, they cancel; a whole wave, they add up again.

WHAT TO KEEPTwo slits, one bright middle: light waves that arrive in step add up.
