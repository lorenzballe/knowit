DEEP SEA

What did Piccard see through his porthole on the trench floor in 1960?

White mud, clear water

At the bottom of the Mariana Trench, Piccard said the floor was white mud. With the lights on, the water was so limpid he could see out through the plexiglass porthole. Outside it was 3.5 °C.
