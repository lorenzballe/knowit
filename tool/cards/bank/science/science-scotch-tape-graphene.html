How did two scientists get a sheet of carbon one atom thick?

16 layersGraphite: carbon sheets stacked up

Just graphite and scotch tape

Graphite is many sheets of carbon stacked loosely. Tape pulls the top sheets away; peel again and again and some flakes end up one atom thick. In 2004, Geim and Novoselov made graphene this way, with no complex chemistry.

Tap “Peel” until only one layer is left.

WHAT TO KEEPGraphene was first made by peeling graphite with tape down to one layer.
