THERMITE

In a thermite reaction, how does pure iron separate from the slag?

The iron sinks

Liquid iron is more than twice as dense as liquid aluminum oxide, so it settles while the slag floats. When the bottom melts through, pure iron drains out first; the slag comes only after.

Which liquid sinks to the bottom of the crucible? Tap it.

WHAT TO KEEPHeavier liquid sinks: density sorts the metal from the slag for free.
