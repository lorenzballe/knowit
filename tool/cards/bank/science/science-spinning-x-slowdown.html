SPIN + SPEED

How does the X's backward spin change how fast it moves?

Say the tube flies forward at 10 and the X's side spins back at 7. Drag the pin to show how fast the X crosses your view.

10 − 7 = 3: the X slows down

The X spins backwards while the tube moves forwards, so its spin takes speed away. The O spins the same way the tube moves, so it races across your view much faster.

WHAT TO KEEPSpin against the motion subtracts; spin with it adds.
