HALF-LIFE

Why do unstable atoms transform at a constant rate?

8 left: half of what remains, every time

Each unstable atom has the same fixed chance of changing in every moment, set by the forces in its nucleus. So each half-life turns half of whatever is left into another atom: 64, 32, 16, 8. That time is fixed for each kind of atom and we know it, so atoms work as clocks for rocks.
