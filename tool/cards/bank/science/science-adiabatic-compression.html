FIRE SYRINGE

What is adiabatic compression, and why can it set cotton on fire?

Squeezing air heats it. Push slowly and the heat leaks away. Push fast and it’s trapped, hot enough to light the cotton on its own.

Push the plunger slowly. Then slam it.

WHAT TO KEEPAdiabatic compression: so fast the heat has no time to escape.
