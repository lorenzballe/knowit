What collision built the Himalaya, and why is it the tallest chain?

Drag India north, hard into Eurasia, and watch the crust.

A head-on crash

India raced north at a remarkable speed and slammed into Eurasia. The crust at the impact had nowhere to go but up, so it crumpled and piled into the Himalaya, the tallest chain on Earth.

WHAT TO KEEPThe Himalaya is the scar of India smashing violently into Eurasia.
