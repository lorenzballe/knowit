DRAG TO THE DEEP

How hard does the sea press, on the way down to the Titanic?

Drag the sub down toward the Titanic, 3,800 m below.

Every 10 m of water adds one atmosphere, plus one for the air: 301 at 3,000 m, 381 at the Titanic. Each square metre of hull bears some 3,800 tonnes, almost half the 8,000-tonne Eiffel Tower.

WHAT TO KEEPEvery 10 m down adds one atmosphere, and the sea never stops adding.
