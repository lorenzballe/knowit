Why are Greenland's rocks such a big deal for mining?

Greenland holds some of the planet's oldest rocks, part of the great North Atlantic cratons. Complex magmatic systems cross them, and they gather rare, strategic elements from thinly scattered to concentrated pockets worth mining.
