WEIGHT

Why is it impossible to lift a car off the ground?

Weight wins.

The car's weight is gravity pulling it down into the Earth. That pull is far stronger than any pull a person can make, so the car stays put.

Set your pull to your hardest, then press Lift.

WHAT TO KEEPWeight is a force: gravity pulling a thing down.
