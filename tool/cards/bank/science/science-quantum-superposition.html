QUANTUM BITS

What is the state of an electron before it is measured?

Hold the button, let go to measure. Repeat a few times.

Before you measure it, the electron is in a superposition: spin up and spin down at once. Measuring always gives just one. The mix only sets how likely each one is.

WHAT TO KEEPUnmeasured, it is both at once; measured, it is one.
