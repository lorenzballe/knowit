How is plasma held without touching any wall?

A cage made of magnetism

Plasma at millions of degrees would wreck any material it touched. So nothing solid holds it: a complex system of magnetic fields keeps it isolated from the walls and under control.

Release the hot plasma into the ring and watch the wall.
