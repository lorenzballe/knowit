How many times can plastic be recycled, compared with paper?

Recycling cycles0

Drag the slider to send both through recycling, again and again.

Every time paper is pulped, its fibres get weaker. After about 7 rounds they lose their strength and end up as processing waste. Plastic can go round at least 20 times.

WHAT TO KEEPPaper wears out after about 7 recyclings; plastic lasts at least 20.
