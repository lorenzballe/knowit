A POCKET SUNSET

Why is aerogel blue, yet yellowish when you look through it?

Path1×

Aerogel is glass foamed up to 99.8% air, its pores tens of nanometres wide, far smaller than a wave of light. Drag your eye round to look through it.

WHAT TO KEEPSky blue is scattered light; sunset orange is what is left.
Source · Lord Rayleigh, Phil. Mag. 41 (1871); Kasten & Young, Applied Optics 28 (1989)
