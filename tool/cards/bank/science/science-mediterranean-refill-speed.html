How quickly did the Atlantic refill the dried-up Mediterranean?

1 to 2 centuries

When Gibraltar reopened at the start of the Pliocene, the ocean stood far above the dry basin floor. It crashed in as waterfalls bigger than any on Earth today and filled the whole sea in about a hundred years: an instant in geology.

Guess how long the fill took, then watch Gibraltar open.

WHAT TO KEEPThe Mediterranean refilled in a geological instant: one or two centuries.
