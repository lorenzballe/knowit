ELECTRICITY

How fast do electric and magnetic fields reach a bulb 1 m away?

Tap the switch to close the circuit.

A few nanoseconds. What matters is not what happens in the wires but what happens around them. The electric and magnetic fields spread through space at essentially light speed, far faster than the electrons drift.

WHAT TO KEEPThe fields around the wire light the bulb, at light speed, not the slow electrons.
