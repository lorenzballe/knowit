FILL THE LAKE

What did filling the Vajont reservoir wake up inside the mountain?

The dam is built. Tap the glass tap to fill the lake, and keep an eye on the orange clay layer.

Wet clay woke an old landslide.

The lake rose until it reached a layer of clay. Clay is impermeable: instead of letting water through, it swelled and deformed. The ancient landslide resting on it lost its balance and moved again.

WHAT TO KEEPThe reservoir broke the slope's balance by soaking the clay beneath an ancient landslide.
