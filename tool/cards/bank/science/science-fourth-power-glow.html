DRAG THE HEAT

Heat steel a little hotter. How much more heat does it radiate?

Drag right to heat the steel, and watch how much power it radiates.

2× hotter, 16× the heat

Radiated power grows with the fourth power of absolute temperature: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16. So a slight rise in temperature means a huge rise in the heat steel loses.

WHAT TO KEEPRadiated power ∝ T⁴: a little hotter means a lot more heat lost.
