GUESS THE HEAT

Steel made a little hotter radiates how much more heat?

Drag the hot rail's column up to your guess.

Far more heat than the small step.

Radiated power grows with the fourth power of absolute temperature, counted from absolute zero. The hot rail stands only a little further from zero, but that small step is multiplied by itself four times. Slight increases in temperature result in huge increases in power.

WHAT TO KEEP Before trusting that a small step changes little, find the true zero and the exponent.
