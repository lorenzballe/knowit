UNDERGROUND

What does the bank's floor rest on, letting thieves dig silently?

Old rubbish, not rock

The floor slab sits on metres of fill: soil, shards, tiles, blocks and rubble piled up over decades, maybe centuries. It is loose, like sand with chunks in it, so it digs out easily. Only the foundations go deeper, down to hard tuff.
