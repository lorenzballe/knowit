How hot does a burning room get before it all ignites at once?

Drag the thermometer to where the whole room bursts into flame.

Hot gases pile up under the ceiling and the heat climbs out of control. At their combustion limit, typically 500–600°C, all those gases catch fire together: that is flashover, and it is practically unstoppable.

WHAT TO KEEPAt about 500–600°C, a room's gases ignite all at once.
