LIGHT OR HEAT

A blue LED can hold billions of light-killing defects per square centimetre. Why does it still shine?

light 0%heat 0%

Each electron–hole pair could give one photon. In plain gallium nitride, with no indium, most wander into a defect first and die as heat.

WHAT TO KEEPThe white LED bulb at home is a blue LED like this, saved by uneven indium.
