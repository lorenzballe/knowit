COLD SPRAY

Why does a deodorant spray feel cold?

Tap the three glowing spots: the can, the nozzle, the mist.

The can holds propane gas, packed in tight. As it leaves the nozzle it expands, and an expanding gas pulls in heat, just like a fridge's refrigerant. Propane also burns, so never use a spray near a flame.

WHAT TO KEEPThe cold isn't the deodorant: it's the propane expanding.
