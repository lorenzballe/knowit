GRAVEL

On gravel, why does a rally driver let the car slide into corners?

Pick a reason, then say how sure you are.

It rolls forward and slides sideways at once.

The video calls it almost a third kind of friction, halfway between rolling and sliding. Rolling drives the car forward, sliding carries it sideways, and using both lets it slow down less into the bend.

WHAT TO KEEP A slide isn't always lost grip: used on purpose, it's a second force to steer with.
