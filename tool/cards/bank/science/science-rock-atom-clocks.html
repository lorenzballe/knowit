ROCK CLOCKS

How can a rock tell us how old it is?

The mix is the clock.

Rocks hold unstable atoms that slowly turn into stable ones. Compare how many have turned with how many are left, and you know how much time has passed. Some atoms take millions of years, others thousands, minutes or a fraction of a second.

Drag time until the fresh rock’s mix matches the found rock.
