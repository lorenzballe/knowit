BIO ≠ BIODEGRADABLE

Are bioplastics always biodegradable?

No. About half aren't.

“Bio” is the origin: sugars from plants like wheat or sugarcane. “Biodegradable” is the fate: broken down in human time. Two separate questions.

Pick one, then say how sure you are. Then watch the tray sort itself.

WHAT TO KEEP“Bio” is where a plastic comes from (plant sugars, like wheat or sugarcane). “Biodegradable” is how it ends. Check both.
