A deep fault creeps without quakes. How can it still cause them?

The deep fault slides all the time, too slowly to shake anything. That steady creep strains the locked faults above it, and when they give way all at once, that is the earthquake.

Drag the blue arrow along the deep fault and watch the rocks above.
