SEWAGE

What happens in a sewage tank after the air is turned off?

Air: OFF

Air feeds bacteria that eat the organic matter and the nitrogen compounds. Switch it off and anaerobic bacteria, which work without oxygen, finish the remaining pollutants. Nothing is added: just bacteria and a natural process.

Flip the air ON, watch the tank, then flip it OFF.

WHAT TO KEEPSewage is cleaned by two crews of bacteria: one with air, one without.
