DRY ICE

Why does CO₂ solidify on the water surface?

Drag the thermometer down: pour in solid nitrogen and chill the surface.

The surface got colder than –78 °C.

That is CO₂’s sublimation point. Below it, CO₂ gas from the air skips the liquid stage and deposits straight onto the surface as solid dry ice.

WHAT TO KEEPMake a surface cold enough and the air itself turns to frost on it.
