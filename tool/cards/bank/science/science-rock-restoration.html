RESTORE THE ROCK

Why do geologists undo the faults in a broken rock section?

Two faults broke this rock and dropped it. Drag each sunken block up until its glowing layers meet the ones beside it.

Faults break the layers and shift them, so their first shape is lost. Undoing each fault's throw, like a puzzle, brings back the undeformed state, and the clinoforms building out to the left become plain to see.

WHAT TO KEEPUndo the deformation first, and the original layers become easy to read.
