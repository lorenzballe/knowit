Why does a rally driver steer away from a corner before turning in?

the car's weighttyre pressed downtyre losing grip

Steering away first throws the weight to one side. Turning back while lifting off swings it hard the other way, like a pendulum, so the unloaded tyres lose grip and the car rotates decisively.
