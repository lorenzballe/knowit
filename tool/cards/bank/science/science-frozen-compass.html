FROZEN COMPASS

Why do cooled rocks keep the magnetic direction they had while hot?

Both lavas line up with Earth's field. Hold the picture to cool the right lava below its Curie point.

Grains of magnetite and hematite line up with the field while the rock is hot. Below the Curie point they lock in place, so when the field later reverses, the cooled rock keeps pointing the old way.

WHAT TO KEEPA cooled lava is a frozen compass: it keeps the field it set in.
