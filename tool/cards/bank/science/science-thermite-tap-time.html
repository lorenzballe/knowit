What is a thermite 'tap time', and why does it matter so much?

Tap earlyTap late

Tap time is how long after the reaction starts the metal flows out the bottom. Too soon, and the metal hasn't fully separated from the slag. Too late, and it dissolves silica from the crucible walls and comes out colder.

Slide the dial to pick when to tap, then pour.

WHAT TO KEEPWaiting cleans the metal, but waiting too long cools and taints it.
