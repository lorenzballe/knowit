How does the depth of the surface of rupture affect a landslide's speed?

The shallow one

The deeper the surface of rupture, the more rock sits above it: a larger volume on the move. More volume tends to make the landslide move more slowly.

Same hill, two cuts. Pick the slide that moves faster.

WHAT TO KEEPDeep cut, big volume, slow slide. Shallow cut, small volume, fast slide.
