SIGNALS

Hit the top of a steel bar. Does the bottom move at once?

A tiny moment later

The hit squeezes the top, and the squeeze is passed down slice by slice: a signal. In steel it runs about 5 km every second, so the delay is tiny, but never zero. A falling slinky shows it slowly enough to see.

Bet first, then say how sure you are. Then watch it in slow motion.

WHAT TO KEEPNothing is perfectly rigid: a change at one end takes time to reach the other.
