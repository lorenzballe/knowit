RIGID?

Change one end of a steel bar. When does the far end feel it?

Pick when the far end responds. Then say how sure you are.

A very, very short time later.

The change at one end is a signal, and the far end only moves once it arrives. A falling slinky shows it slowly. In steel the time is very, very short, but never zero.

WHAT TO KEEP Even in a rigid steel bar, a signal takes a finite time to get from one end to the other.
