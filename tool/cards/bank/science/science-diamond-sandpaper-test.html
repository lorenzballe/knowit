SANDPAPER TEST

What happens when a real diamond is rubbed on sandpaper?

Drag the stone back and forth across the sandpaper.

A real diamond is harder than the grains of sandpaper, so the grains wear flat and the stone stays clean. A fake is softer than the grit, so the grit scratches it.

WHAT TO KEEPA real diamond dulls the sandpaper; the sandpaper scratches a fake.
