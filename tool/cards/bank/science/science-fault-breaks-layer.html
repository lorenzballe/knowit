What happens to a rock layer where a fault cuts it?

It breaks, drops, and its base dips.

The fault cuts the red unit in two and one side slides down it: the layer stops at the fault and carries on lower down. Beyond the fault its base still dips; it is not flat.

The fault is about to slip. Pick where the red layer ends up.
