Messina 1908: how does the West Fault differ from the fault beneath it?

The deep fault creeps all the time, so slowly that it never jolts: it is aseismic. Its creep loads the West Fault above, which is stuck: it bends, then slips all at once. That sudden slip is the earthquake.
