TUNNEL SMOKE

In a tunnel fire, which way does the smoke tend to drift?

With the traffic →

Moving cars push the air ahead of them, so the tunnel's air flows the way they drive. The smoke rides that flow downstream. That's why you escape the other way, against the traffic.

Watch the traffic, then bet where the smoke will go.

WHAT TO KEEPIn a tunnel, smoke goes where the cars go: escape the other way.
