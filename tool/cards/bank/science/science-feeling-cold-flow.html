HEAT FLOW

Why does aluminium feel colder than a book?

Tap the aluminium, then the book, to lay your hand on each.

Both are at the same temperature. Aluminium conducts heat away from your hand much faster, so your hand loses heat quickly, and that is what feels cold. It's why a bath mat feels nicer than a towel after a winter shower.

WHAT TO KEEPTouch senses how fast heat leaves you, not how hot things are.
