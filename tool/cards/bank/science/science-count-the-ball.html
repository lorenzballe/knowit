COUNT BY MEASURING

Physicists know how many atoms are in this 1 kg silicon ball, without counting one. Roughly how many?

93.7 mm ÷ 0.543 nm172.6 million cells across
cubed: a box round the ball5.14 × 1024 cells
× 0.52: the ball's share2.69 × 1024 cells
× 8 atoms in every cell2.15 × 1025 atoms

Pick a power of ten, then say how sure you are.

WHAT TO KEEPMeasure the whole and one repeating piece, then divide: that counts a book's pages, or a ball's atoms.
