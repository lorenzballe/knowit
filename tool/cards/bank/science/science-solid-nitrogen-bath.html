COLD PHYSICS

Why does pouring solid nitrogen onto a water bath work?

It turns to gas and drinks heat.

Solid nitrogen sublimates fast: it goes straight from solid to gas. That takes huge amounts of heat, which it pulls out of the water bath, so the surface stays extremely cold.

WHAT TO KEEPTurning solid into gas soaks up heat from whatever it touches.
