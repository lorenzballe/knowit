What is the apparent dip, and how is it found?

Drag to turn the section. Watch how steep the layer looks.

A layer dipping 36° shows that full angle only in a section square to its strike. Turn the section toward the strike and the same drop is spread over a longer run, so the layer looks flatter: at 65° it reads about 33°. Geologists read this value off a true-to-apparent dip conversion diagram.

WHAT TO KEEPOnly a section square to the strike shows the true dip; any other shows less.
