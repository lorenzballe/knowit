What is a ‘template horizon’ in drawing a geological cross-section?

Drawn layer by layer, it comes out patchy. Draw the key layer first: one line through the three red dots.

The template horizon is a key layer, like a particular sandstone, found across most of the section. Drawn first, it guides every other horizon, so the layers stay continuous and consistent.

WHAT TO KEEPDraw one key layer first, and let it guide all the rest.
