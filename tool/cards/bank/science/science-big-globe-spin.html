A globe twice as wide, same mass: how many pushes to spin as fast?

4 pushes: 2 × 2

Twice the radius puts the same mass twice as far from the axis, and spin inertia grows with the radius squared: I = m r². So every push speeds the big globe only a quarter as much. It isn't hard to push, just hard to get going fast.

The small globe got one push. Guess first.
