INERTIA

How much inertia does the video's giant spinning globe have?

2 to 12 tons

That much mass resists every change in speed. So you really feel it: you have to push hard just to make the globe spin faster.

Drag the globe along the line to your guess, in tons.

WHAT TO KEEPInertia is resistance to speeding up: this globe's is 2–12 tons.
