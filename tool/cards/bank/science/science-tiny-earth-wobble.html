Earth and Moon pull each other equally hard. Why does Earth barely wobble?

Earth, compared with the Moon
pullsame
masssame
motionsame

16× the mass, 1/16 the motion.

Each feels the same pull, but its effect is the pull divided by the mass. Earth's huge mass gives it huge inertia, so the Moon's pull barely moves it.

Drag to make Earth heavier. Watch its wobble.
