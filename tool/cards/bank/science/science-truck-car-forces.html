THIRD LAW

When a truck hits a car, which one pushes harder?

Exactly equal.

At every instant the truck pushes the car just as hard as the car pushes the truck, whatever their size. The car's speed changes far more only because it has far less mass to move.

Pick one, then say how sure you are.

WHAT TO KEEPForces come in equal, opposite pairs; mass only decides who moves more.
