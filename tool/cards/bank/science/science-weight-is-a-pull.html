MASS VS WEIGHT

What is the fundamental definition of weight?

SCALE READS5.0 kg
MATTER IN BALL5 kg

Take the Earth away, then bring it back. Tap the globe to move the scale.

Weight is a pull, not something the ball has.

It is the Earth pulling the ball toward its centre, so "down" points there from every spot. Take the Earth away and the weight goes; the 5 kg of matter, the mass, stays.

WHAT TO KEEPMass is how much matter; weight is the Earth's pull on it.
