How can a slinky's breathing tell you if your model is right?

Your model breathes slower than the real slinky.

SofterStiffer

This is the breathing mode: every turn simply moves in and out together. Its period depends in a simple way on the slinky's parameters. So if the model's numbers give the right beat, the numbers pass the test.

Drag the stiffness until your model breathes in step with the real slinky.
