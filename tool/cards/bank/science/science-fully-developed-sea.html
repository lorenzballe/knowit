OCEAN WAVES

When do wind waves reach their maximum size?

When breaking loses energy as fast as the wind adds it.

The wind pours energy in at a steady rate. The bigger the waves get, the more they break, turning their energy into swirling eddies and heat. When the losses match the input, the waves stop growing: a fully developed sea.
