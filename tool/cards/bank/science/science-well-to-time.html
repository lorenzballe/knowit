GEOLOGY

Wells are measured in metres, seismic in time. How do they meet?

Convert the well to time.

The seismic section is in time; the well's markers are in metres. Put the markers in time and they sit on their reflectors: hard data to interpret from. Depth conversion comes only at the end, once the interpretation is ready.

Try both ways of putting the well's H1 and H2 on the section.
