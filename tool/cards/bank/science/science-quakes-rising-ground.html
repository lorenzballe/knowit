When do earthquakes strike as the Campi Flegrei ground breathes?

Only while it rises.

The ground at Campi Flegrei breathes: it slowly rises, then sinks. Earthquakes come only in the rising phase, called positive bradyseism. While it sinks, there are none.

Flip the switch: make the ground rise, then make it sink.

WHAT TO KEEPCampi Flegrei shakes on the way up, never on the way down.
