Why is the Adriatic Sea considered a flexural basin?

Tap the picture to pile weight on the Apennines · 0/5

Mountains are heavy. As the Apennines rose, their weight bent the crust beneath them downward, opening a depression in front of the chain: a foreland basin. Water filled it, and that is the Adriatic.

WHAT TO KEEPHeavy mountains bend the crust; the sag in front of them becomes a basin.
