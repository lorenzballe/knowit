HEAT FLOW

A metal hard drive and a book share a room. Which is colder?

Same temperature

Both sit at room temperature, and your warmer hand loses heat to each. Metal conducts it away fast, so it feels cold; the book takes it slowly. Same reason a bath mat feels nicer than the towel beside it.

Touch them in your mind. Pick one, then say how sure.

WHAT TO KEEPYou don't feel temperature; you feel how fast heat flows to or from you.
