INERTIA

Same push on a globe ten times heavier. How fast does it spin up?

Ten times slower

It does move, but its large mass gives it a high rotational inertia. The same push adds spin ten times slower; to keep up, you must push ten times harder.

Pick an answer, then say how sure you are.

WHAT TO KEEPHeavy things aren't hard to move; they're hard to speed up.
