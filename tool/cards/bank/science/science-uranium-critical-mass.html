How much uranium‑235 does a bomb need to go supercritical?

About 52 kg: a ball 17 cm wide

Each split nucleus throws out neutrons. In a smaller lump too many fly out before hitting another nucleus; at about 52 kg enough stay in for the chain to grow. Plutonium‑239 needs only about 10 kg, a 10 cm ball.
