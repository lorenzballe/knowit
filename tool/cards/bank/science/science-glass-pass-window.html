LIGHT & GLASS

Why is glass transparent to light but not to UV rays?

Light comes in many frequencies, and a material lets only some of them through. Glass lets visible light pass, so it looks clear. Ultraviolet is outside its window, so it is stopped.

Drag along the colour bar: try visible light, then go past violet.

WHAT TO KEEPTransparent is never absolute: it always means "transparent to which frequencies".
