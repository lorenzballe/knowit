What physical experiment first confirmed period-doubling in the logistic map?

Heat the mercury box from below, step by step. Watch the spikes.

Albert Libchaber heated a box of mercury from below, and it turned in convection rolls. Raising the temperature gradient made the rolls wobble at half the frequency: spikes of two heights, then four, then eight.
