Why is a well-tuned piano deliberately out of tune with the textbook octave?

Play both octaves and listen for a wobble. No sound? The ribbons show it.

Its strings are stiff, so their overtones run slightly sharp of whole-number multiples, and tuners widen each octave to match. The stretch adds up: the top notes sit tens of cents sharp, a cent being a hundredth of a semitone.

WHAT TO KEEPWhen the parts are imperfect, tune them to each other, not to the ideal.
Source · Railsback, Journal of the Acoustical Society of America (1938)
