POINT OF VIEW

One view: why not refuse bad treatment as fast as a rotten cake?

Diventare Strong says: treat it like the cake. Drag the dot to where you stand.

Source · Diventare Strong (YouTube, 2021) · a point of view

The cake is easy because it is obvious.

For: you never debate the cake. Every excuse you invent for someone is work you do so they don't have to.

Against: people are rarely as plain as a cake. A first, unclear slight can have a reason, and a fast no can be wrong.

ASK YOURSELF Am I still weighing this because it's unclear, or because saying no feels rude?
