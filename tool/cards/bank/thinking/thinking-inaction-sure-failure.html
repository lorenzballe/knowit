POINT OF VIEW

One view: is doing nothing a guaranteed failure?

A coach's claim: if you never try, you fail anyway, so trying can only add a chance. Drag the figure to where you stand.

It hangs on how far you could fall.

For: when the chance passes while you wait, not trying is a sure no, and a small failure costs little more. Against: waiting is not always a no, and when a mistake does lasting harm, preparing first beats leaping.

ASK YOURSELF If I try and fail, what do I lose that waiting isn't already costing me?
