GAME THEORY

Why does not knowing when a game ends keep players cooperating?

No sure last round, no unravelling.

With a known end, betraying in the last round is safe, and that logic slides back to round 1. With a random end (200 rounds on average), any round might be followed by another, so you keep cooperating: you might still need them.
