DIVIDE IT

Why isn't the Bermuda Triangle more dangerous than other seas?

The Triangle counts the most wrecks. Drag the dial right to divide each sea's wrecks by the ships that cross it.

The Triangle is vast and busy, so it piles up wrecks. Per ship crossing it, the risk is the same as in other seas.
