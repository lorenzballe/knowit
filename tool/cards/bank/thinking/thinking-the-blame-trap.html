POINT OF VIEW

One view: does blaming others for your problems hand away your power?

Drag the dot to where you stand. Then say how sure you are.

Two questions, not one.

You leaned: on the fence.

For the view: blame hands the next move to someone else, so you wait. Asking “what was my part?” keeps a lever in your hand.

Against: sometimes others really are at fault. Saying so is honest and fair, and is often the first step to a fix.

The trap is fusing the two questions. Fault looks back and power looks forward, and their answers can differ.

ASK YOURSELF Whoever is at fault, what is the next move only I can make?
