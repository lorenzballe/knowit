CLOSED DOOR

Why did North Korea refuse to open its economy after the Soviet collapse?

“Open the window, and the flies come in.”

The flies were foreign influence. So North Korea shut the door and isolated itself completely. The price: it lost its markets and trade partners.

Pick a reason, then watch the window.
