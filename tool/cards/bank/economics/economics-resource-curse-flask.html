RESOURCE CURSE

Why is the DRC poor despite being rich in gold and cobalt?

Under the soil of the DR Congo: gold, cobalt, diamonds, and about 80% of the world's coltan. Drag along the timeline.

The resource curse.

The riches are taken through exploitation and fought over in conflict. The wealth flows out, past the people, so the DRC stays one of the poorest countries in the world.
