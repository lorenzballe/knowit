UNDER THE MOUNTAIN

Where do the underground apple-storage cells inside the mountain come from?

Mine voidApple store

They are holes left by a mine.

The cells are quarry voids of a mine that is still working, about 600 m up inside a mountain. Some 35,000 m² of empty rock was recovered to keep apples.

Drag the slider: turn the mine's empty void into a store.
