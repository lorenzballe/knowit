PACKAGE WEIGHT

How far from 500 g can a 500 g pack really weigh?

At most 15 g either way

The small ℮ beside the weight means it is estimated. EU rules cap the gap: a 500 g pack can hold 485 to 515 g, and on average packs are not lighter than the label.

Drag across the scale to set how wide the allowed gap is.

WHAT TO KEEP℮ means estimated, within ±15 g on 500 g, and usually a bit more, not less.
