FOOD SAFETY

Why does the plastic reactor run at just 2 mbar?

To suck the bad gases out.

Melting the plastic gives off limonene and benzene, which are harmful. The vacuum pulls them out of the melt, so the new bottle stays within the legal food-safety limits.

Drag the knob to pump the air out, down to 2 mbar.

WHAT TO KEEPThe vacuum is a cleaner: it sucks harmful gases out of melted plastic.
