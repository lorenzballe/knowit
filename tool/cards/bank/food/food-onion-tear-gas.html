ONION TEARS

Why do onion vapors go straight for your eyes when you cut one?

It's a gas that flies.

Cutting starts a reaction that makes propanethial S-oxide, the “tear factor”. It is volatile: it evaporates and drifts up to your eyes, which sting and water. Goggles stop it as a physical wall.

Pick how the tears reach you. Then say how sure you are.

WHAT TO KEEPOnion tears travel by air: block the air, keep dry eyes.
