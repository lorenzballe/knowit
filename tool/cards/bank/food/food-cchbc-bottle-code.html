BOTTLE CODE

What does the code CCHBC 70 on a Coca-Cola bottle mean?

The “70” is a size. Drag the marker to guess how big the bottle is.

70 means a 2-litre bottle

CCHBC is Coca-Cola HBC, the company that bottles the drink. The number after it tells the bottle's size, and 70 is the 2-litre one.

WHAT TO KEEPCCHBC names the bottler; the number names the size: 70 is 2 litres.
