POINT OF VIEW

Does a steady character prove you're the one steering?

That's Alex Onofri's view. Drag your car toward the side you lean to.

A straight track can't tell you who is steering.

For: Decide who you are before the pressure comes, and the moment can't steer you.

Against: A steady hand and a jammed wheel draw the same line until the road bends.

Test steadiness only at bends: if things changed and you didn't, ask whether that was a choice or a habit.

ASK YOURSELF When the road last bent, did I turn, or just stay who I'd said I was?
