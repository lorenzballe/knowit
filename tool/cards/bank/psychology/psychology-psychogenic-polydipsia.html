ENDLESS THIRST

What psychiatric condition makes a person feel constantly thirsty?

Psychogenic polydipsia

A psychiatric disorder keeps the feeling of thirst switched on, so water never quenches it. The person drinks far more than the body needs, which risks overhydration. It is very rare.
