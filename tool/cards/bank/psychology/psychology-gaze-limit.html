How long can you hold a stranger’s gaze before it feels too long?

Picture a stranger facing you on the train. Tap Start and they look up at you. Tap again the moment it feels too long.

WHAT TO KEEPA stranger’s gaze feels right for about 3 s. Past 5 s, nine in ten find it too long.
Source · Binetti et al., “Pupil dilation as an index of preferred mutual gaze duration”, Royal Society Open Science, 2016
