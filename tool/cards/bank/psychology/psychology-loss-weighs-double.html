How much more heavily do people weight losses than gains?

Win: $10

A coin flip: lose $10 or win more. Drag the win up until the bet feels fair.

Losses are felt much more intensely than gains. Most people weight a loss around twice as heavily, so an even 50/50 bet feels bad. It varies: one person in the video wanted about $100 to risk $10.
