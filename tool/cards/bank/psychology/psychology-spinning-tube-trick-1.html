PERSISTENCE OF VISION

Flick a spinning tube labelled X and O. Why do you see only one?

Only the side you pressed.

The spinning tube is a series of images. Your brain stitches them together into one continuous image. The side you press is the one you keep looking at, so that's the only image your brain shows you.

Pick a side to press, then flick the tube.

WHAT TO KEEPWhat you “see” is your brain stitching quick images into one picture.
