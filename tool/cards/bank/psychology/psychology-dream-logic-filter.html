DREAMS

Why do absurd dream events seem perfectly normal?

Logic filter (prefrontal cortex): 100% on
ASLEEPAWAKE

The logic filter is turned down.

The prefrontal cortex handles critical thinking: logic, planning, the sense of time. During sleep it is less active, so the rational filter shrinks. The brain lets go and builds strange stories that we accept without questions.

Slide the brain from awake to asleep. Watch what gets through.

WHAT TO KEEPIn dreams the part of you that asks “really?” is turned down.
