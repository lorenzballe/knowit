SEEING

Spin a tube marked X and O, pressing the O. What do you see?

Only O's

The spinning tube is a series of images, and your brain stitches them into one continuous picture. The side you press is the one you're looking at, so that's the image your brain shows you.

Make your bet, then say how sure you are.
