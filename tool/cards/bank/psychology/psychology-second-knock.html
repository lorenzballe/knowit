THE LAWN SIGN

Does saying yes to a small favour make the big one easier?

1966: a stranger asks you to plant a big, ugly DRIVE CAREFULLY sign on your lawn. Knock on each street.

WHAT TO KEEPOne small yes can become “I’m someone who says yes”. Notice the second knock.
