SIMULATION

Why does giving in “just once in a while” make the begging harder to stop?

A pretend child asks for a sweet every day, and quits once a run of no’s is too long to be bad luck. A model, not data.

Pick a rule for weeks 1–3. From day 22 it’s no, for good. Which child keeps asking longest?

WHAT TO KEEPEach no that turns into a yes teaches one thing: ask longer.
Source · L. G. Humphreys, Journal of Experimental Psychology 25:141 (1939)
