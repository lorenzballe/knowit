POINT OF VIEW

One view: answer a rumor by showing your worth, not chasing it. Agree?

Drag your jug to where you stand between the two views.

You lean toward showing your value.

For: a rumor is judged against what people already hold of you; the more they hold, the less room it has. Against: talking yourself up can look defensive or vain, and the dark layer never drains by itself: a claim nobody answers can harden into “what everyone knows”.

ASK YOURSELF When you hear a rumor about someone you know, what wins: their denial, or what you had already seen of them?
