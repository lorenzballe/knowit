Why did Apollo 13 swing around the Moon to get home?

The Moon did the turning.

Turning straight back needed the rocket's strong third-stage engines, and the explosion may have damaged them. The LEM's weak thrusters could only nudge the aim, so the Moon's gravity swung the ship around and sped it home.
