SOLAR DAY

How far does Earth turn between one noon and the next?

A bit more than 360°

While Earth spins, it also moves along its orbit, so the Sun ends up at a slightly different angle. One full turn isn't enough: Earth must turn a little extra to bring the Sun overhead again.
