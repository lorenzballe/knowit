When can't Artemis II astronauts add water to food, and what do they eat?

Drag the capsule from launch, around the Moon, and home. Watch the water.

Most Artemis II food is freeze-dried: water must be added. During launch and re-entry the water dispenser is not available, so for those hours the crew eats only ready-to-eat food, like tortillas.
