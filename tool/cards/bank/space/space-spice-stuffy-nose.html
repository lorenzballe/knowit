SPACE FOOD

Why is the food on Artemis II often so spicy?

Flip from Earth to orbit and watch the nose. Then add spice.

Without weight, body fluids drift up to the head and stuff the nose, a bit like a cold. With a stuffy nose food tastes flat, so space food is made spicy and hot to taste of something again.

WHAT TO KEEPIn orbit a stuffy head dulls taste; spice gives the flavour back.
