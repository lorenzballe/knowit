What actually made Apollo 13's oxygen tank explode?

Shorted fan cables caught fire.

A misreading sensor got the stirrer fan switched on. Its cables short-circuited and burned inside the sealed tank. The fire's gas drove the pressure up until the tank burst and damaged the second one.
