GRAVITY

If the Sun vanished, when would Earth stop feeling its pull?

8 min 20 s later

Gravity isn't instant: Einstein's relativity says it travels at the speed of light, 300,000 km/s. Across 150 million km that takes 8 min 20 s, so Earth keeps circling an empty spot until the news arrives.
