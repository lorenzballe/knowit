ZERO-G

Why is lifting weights useless for astronauts in zero-G?

In orbit the dumbbell weighs nothing, so lifting it asks nothing of your muscles. Unused, they shrink: up to 20% of muscle mass can go on flights of just 5 to 11 days.

A stretched elastic pulls back the way weight would, so astronauts train against bands instead.

Drag the dumbbell up in all three places. Watch the load.

WHAT TO KEEPMuscles need a pull to work against; in space it comes from elastic, not weight.
