3-ADIC

How can a 3-adic number make x² + x⁴ + x⁸ a perfect square?

Drag each square into the frame and count its tiles.

In 3-adic numbers, …111 equals −1/2, and the formula said that should be a solution. Squaring it gives squares of side 1/2, 1/4 and 1/16: 64, 16 and 1 tiles of 1/256. Together that is 81 tiles, exactly a square with side 9/16.

WHAT TO KEEP1/4 + 1/16 + 1/256 = 81/256 = (9/16)², a rational answer found through a 3-adic number.
