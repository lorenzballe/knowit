HENSEL LIFTING

How can you solve an equation one prime-power digit at a time?

0 / 9 digits

Drag the slider: each step tries 0, 1, 2 for the next power of 3.

Each new digit only has to work for one more power of 3, so there are just three choices to test, and exactly one fits. Keep going forever and the digits build an exact 3-adic solution of x² = 7.
