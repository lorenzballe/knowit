How long did Fermat’s Last Theorem wait for its proof?

Drag the quill to how many years you think the proof took.

358 years

Fermat’s “marvelous proof” never turned up, and the old tools couldn’t do it. The proof needed numbers invented long after Fermat: the p‑adics, first described by Kurt Hensel in 1897. They also give a systematic way to solve other problems in Diophantus’ Arithmetica.
