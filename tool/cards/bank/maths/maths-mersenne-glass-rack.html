MERSENNE PRIMES

What does the Lenstra–Pomerance–Wagstaff conjecture say about Mersenne primes?

Numbers 2ᵖ − 1 · one tube for each doubling of p found 0 predicted 0

Drag along the glass to double p again and again. Gold beads are the Mersenne primes found.

The level never drops.

The conjecture predicts about the same handful of Mersenne primes every time p doubles. That never runs dry, so there should be infinitely many, and so infinitely many even perfect numbers. But a conjecture is not a proof.
