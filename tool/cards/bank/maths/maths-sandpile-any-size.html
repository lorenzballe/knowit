DROP GRAINS

Why can one grain of sand set off an avalanche of any size?

This pile was fed grain by grain until it could take no more. A square with 4 grains topples, one to each neighbour. Gold beads hold 3. Tap any square to drop a grain.

WHAT TO KEEPOn the edge, a huge avalanche needs no huge cause: any grain can start one.
Source · Bak, Tang & Wiesenfeld, Physical Review Letters 59, 381 (1987); Frette et al., Nature 379, 49 (1996)
