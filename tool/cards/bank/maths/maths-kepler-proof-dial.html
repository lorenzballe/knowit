DRAG THE YEAR

How long did it take to formally prove Kepler's sphere packing conjecture?

In 1611 Kepler said two stackings tie as the tightest possible: hexagonal close packing and face-centred cubic. Drag the pin to the year the formal proof was published.

The formal proof was published in 2017.

Kepler stated it as fact but never proved it. Seeing his stackings are tight is easy; showing that no other stack is tighter is the hard part.

WHAT TO KEEP Showing one arrangement is good is easy; showing it is the best means ruling out every other one, and there are infinitely many.
