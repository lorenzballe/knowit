KNOT THEORY

What simple test can prove two knots are different?

The trefoil is tricolorable.

Twists, pokes and slides never change whether a knot can be coloured this way. A plain loop is one strand, so it can only ever be one colour. So no amount of moving turns the trefoil into a plain loop.

Tap a strand to change its colour. Make every crossing work.

WHAT TO KEEPIf one knot is tricolorable and another isn't, they're different knots.
