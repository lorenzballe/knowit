BORDA COUNT

Add a hopeless third candidate to this made-up Borda count. Who wins now?

Carl can't win. Each voter puts him in the blue slot. Count, then tap the winner.

Ben wins, 7 to 6.

Nobody changed their mind about Ann versus Ben. But on Ben's ballots Carl sits between them, so Ben's lead there doubles, from 1 to 2. Borda's points depend on how many names are on the ballot, so a candidate nobody picks changed the winner. Condorcet: it “relies on irrelevant factors.” These ballots are invented; the rule is Borda's. Tap the ballots to undo.

WHAT TO KEEP A fair choice between two things can't hinge on a third nobody picks.
