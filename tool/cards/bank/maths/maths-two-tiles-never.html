Can just two tiles be forced never to repeat?

Yes, with just 2 tiles

Bumps and notches (or matching colours) say which edges may touch. Copy-and-shift always breaks a rule, so the only way to keep going is a pattern that never repeats, all the way to infinity.

Look at the two tiles, pick an answer, then say how sure you are.
