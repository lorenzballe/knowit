VOTING MATHS

How can a candidate win by doing worse in the first round?

Bohr wins, with fewer votes.

Say 6 of Bohr's 45 points jump to Einstein. Now Curie finishes last and is knocked out. As the centrist, her voters split half to Einstein, half to Bohr, and that pushes Bohr past 50%.

Bohr's speech flops; some voters defect to Einstein. Who wins now?

WHAT TO KEEPIn instant runoff, losing votes can change who's knocked out first, and hand you the win.
