Why does Greenland look so huge on the usual world map?

×14its real size, as drawn up here

Mercator’s map stretches everything more the farther it sits from the equator, wider and taller at once. Up by the Arctic Circle, Greenland gets blown up many times over. Slid to the equator, its real 2.16 million km² shows.
