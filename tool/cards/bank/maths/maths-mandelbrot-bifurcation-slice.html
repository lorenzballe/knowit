How is the logistic map's bifurcation diagram related to the Mandelbrot set?

z: 0 → 1 → 2 → 5 → 26 → …
Blows up → c is NOT in the set

Drag the dot left along the set's middle line. Watch the orbit below.

Start at z = 0 and repeat z² + c. Every c whose orbit stays finite lies in the Mandelbrot set, and along its middle line those orbits draw the bifurcation diagram: 1 value, then 2, then 4, then chaos. Each split lines up with a new bulb of the set.

WHAT TO KEEPThe bifurcation diagram is the Mandelbrot set seen along its real axis.
