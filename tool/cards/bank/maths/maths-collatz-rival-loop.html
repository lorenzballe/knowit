COLLATZ LOOPS

If Collatz had a second loop, how long would it have to be?

Every number tested ends in the loop 4 → 2 → 1. Drag to grow a rival loop until nobody has ruled it out.

At least 186 billion numbers.

Every number tested ends in 4, 2, 1. From that, mathematicians calculate that any other loop must hold at least 186 billion numbers. Likely, but not proved.
