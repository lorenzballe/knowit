GUESS THE PEAK

How high does the Collatz sequence for 27 climb before descending?

if odd ×3 + 1  ·  if even ÷ 2

9,232 m

An odd number is tripled plus one, then halved: it grows by about half. Land on odd numbers again and again and 27 climbs past Everest, until the halvings win and it falls to 1.
