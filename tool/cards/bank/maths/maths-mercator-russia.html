MAP SIZES

Russia looks bigger than Africa. Laid on Africa at true size, how big?

Drag up or down to size the white Russia on Africa.

Russia is smaller than Africa.

Mercator draws land bigger the farther it lies from the equator. Russia lies far north, so it swells; Africa straddles the equator and stays close to true.

WHAT TO KEEP On a Mercator map, distance from the equator buys size: compare countries only at the same latitude.
