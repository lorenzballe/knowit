10-ADIC NUMBERS

How can the endless number …857142857143 be 1/7?

Multiply it by 7, digit by digit, from the right.

7 × …857142857143 = …0001

Every step writes a 0 and launches a carry to the left, and the carries never run out. So every digit is 0 except the last 1: seven times this number is exactly 1, which is what 1/7 means.

Tap to multiply one digit and launch its carry left.

WHAT TO KEEPIn the 10-adics, a number is 1/7 if 7 times it equals 1.
