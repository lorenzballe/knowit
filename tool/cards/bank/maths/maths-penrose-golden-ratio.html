GOLDEN RATIO

Count Penrose kites and darts. What number does kites ÷ darts approach?

KITES5
—kites ÷ darts
DARTS0
the ratio after each split

That's the golden ratio, φ. Kites and darts are cut from pentagons, whose diagonal ÷ edge is φ, so it is built into the pieces. A repeating pattern would give a fraction of whole numbers; φ is irrational, so this one can never repeat.

Tap Split: every tile breaks into smaller kites and darts. Watch the ratio.
