10-ADIC NUMBERS

Why does …6667, with 6s going on forever, equal 1/3?

3 × …6667 = …0001 = 1

3 × 7 = 21: write 1, carry 2. Every 6 then gives 3 × 6 + 2 = 20: write 0, carry 2 again, forever. Three of this number make exactly 1, so it is 1/3.

Tap to multiply by 3, one column at a time, right to left.
