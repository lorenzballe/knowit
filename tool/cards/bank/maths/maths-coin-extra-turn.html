COIN PARADOX

A coin rolls once around a circle with 3× its circumference. How many turns?

Flat road, 3 coins long0.0
Around a circle 3 coins round0.0

4 turns: N + 1

Rolling over the 3-coin rim gives 3 turns; circling the path once adds one more.
