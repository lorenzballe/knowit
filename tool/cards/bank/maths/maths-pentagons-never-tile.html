Why do periodic crystals never have five-fold symmetry?

One point on a floor. 360° to fill around it.

108° doesn't go into 360°.

Triangles, squares and hexagons close around a point exactly; pentagons never do. So no repeating crystal can have five-fold symmetry: only quasicrystals, whose pattern never repeats.
