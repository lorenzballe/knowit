A 1-in-16 chance, any given year, of a storm that topples Citicorp. Odds of one within 16 years?

With the damper working, such storms came every 67 years on average. Its power is out. Drag up the tower to set your odds.

About 2 in 3: 64%

Each ball is one run of 16 years. If each year is its own 1-in-16 draw, unaffected by the last, all 16 stay calm only (15/16)16 ≈ 36% of the time.
