1 IN N

How often does a storm strong enough to topple Citicorp hit?

Drag the flag: once every how many years? The jar holds one slip per year, and each year the sky draws one.

WHAT TO KEEP“Once every N years” means a 1-in-N chance each year, and it can come any year.
