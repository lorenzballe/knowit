PERFECT NUMBERS

What must the prime powers of an odd perfect number look like?

Tap a prime to change its power. Make the scale balance.

Each σ(pa) = 1 + p + … + pa adds a+1 odd terms, so it is even only when a is odd. But σ(n) = 2n holds the factor 2 just once, so only one σ can be even.

WHAT TO KEEPAn odd perfect number has exactly one prime raised to an odd power.
