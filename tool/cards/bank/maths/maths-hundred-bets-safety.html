LAW OF LARGE NUMBERS

What is the probability of losing money in 100 favorable bets?

Each bet is a coin flip: win $20 or lose $10. One flip is a gamble, but in 100 flips you win roughly 45 to 55 times, and every win counts double. On average you end up $500 ahead, and only 1 in 2300 players ends up down.

Each bet: a coin flip, win $20 or lose $10. Drag the coin to 100 bets.

WHAT TO KEEPOne good bet is a gamble; a hundred of them is almost a sure win.
