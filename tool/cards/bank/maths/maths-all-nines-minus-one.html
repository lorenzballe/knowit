10-ADIC NUMBERS

How can an endless string of 9s equal –1?

Add 1 to …99999999. Tap to carry, one column at a time.

…9999 + 1 = …0000 = 0

The 1 is carried left forever, so every digit turns to 0. A number that gives 0 when you add 1 is –1. The algebra agrees: call it m, then m – 10m gives 9 = –9m, so m = –1.

WHAT TO KEEPIn 10-adics, all 9s is –1: add 1 and the carry never stops.
