CHILD MINERS

Why do children work the narrow tunnels of Potosí's silver mine?

Only the small one fits.

Some tunnels in Potosí's silver mine are too tight for a grown man. Children are small enough to squeeze through, so they are sent in to carry the material out.

Hold the button: both miners walk toward the silver.
