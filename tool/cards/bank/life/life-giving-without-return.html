Both are right, about different people.

For the view: giving again and again to someone who never returns anything costs you, and it teaches them to take. Against it: most trust starts with a gift nobody has repaid yet. Judge it on the first round only, and every act of generosity looks like a bad deal.

ASK YOURSELF When I call a gift wasted, am I judging the person, or only the first round?
