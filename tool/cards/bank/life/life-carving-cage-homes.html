TAP THE WALLS

To carve one old flat into tiny homes, which walls come down?

A sketch of an old flat. Tap every wall the builders would knock down.

Every wall but the load-bearing one.

In huge old blocks like the Monster Building, the walls that aren’t load-bearing come down without authorization, and thinner ones go up. A 30–40 m² studio becomes three homes of about 10 m² each, with a mini kitchen and bath: roughly a prison cell.

WHAT TO KEEP Most of Hong Kong’s tiny flats are illegal: old flats cut up by knocking down walls that aren’t load-bearing and putting up thinner ones.
