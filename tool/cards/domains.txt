# Where a card's claim may and may not come from.
#
# [blocked] is handed to the search and fetch tools as blocked_domains: the
# model never sees these pages. Lists of facts, content farms, social feeds,
# question sites — the places a listicle is born.
#
# [never a reference] may be read on the way to a source and may never be
# the source: a card's reference must be the page that states the claim,
# and an encyclopaedia entry states what somebody else found.
#
# [preferred <topic>] is a suggestion the scout is shown, not a fence: the
# places readers of that subject would trust first. A find from elsewhere
# is fine when it is the page that actually states the thing.
#
# [kinds <topic>] are the kinds of source (schema.json, source_kind) a
# subject's cards are built from; the plan asks for the one the subject has
# least of. A subject not listed may use any kind but arithmetic.
