Space

Averaged over the space inside its event horizon, how dense is a black hole of a billion suns?

Far less dense than water: about 18 kg per cubic metre, about 15 times air. The horizon’s radius grows in step with the mass, so the volume inside grows with its cube, and the average density falls with the mass squared. A hole of one sun averages 80 times denser than a nucleus.

The trap: Picturing every black hole as matter crushed beyond nuclear density.

Bar move

When the container grows faster than its contents, the average thins out.

Source · Carroll, Spacetime and Geometry, 2004

Thinking

A magnitude 8 earthquake against a magnitude 6: how much more energy does the bigger one release?

About 1,000 times. Each whole step on the magnitude scale is ten times the shaking recorded and about 32 times the energy, so two steps are roughly 32 × 32. A magnitude 8 releases in one event what a thousand magnitude 6 quakes would.

The trap: Knowing each step is ten times, and stopping at the shaking.

Bar move

On a scale that counts powers, one step is never one step.

Source · Kanamori, Journal of Geophysical Research, 1977

Nature

If every pollinating animal vanished, by roughly how much would total world crop production fall?

Under a tenth. Three quarters of leading crop types benefit from animal pollination, but the staples that make most of the tonnage, wheat, rice and maize, are wind- or self-pollinated. Aizen and colleagues put the direct loss at 3 to 8% of output. Fruit, nuts and coffee would suffer; bread would not.

The trap: Treating every crop that bees help as a crop that needs them.

Bar move

Before crediting a helper with everything, ask what happens without it.

Source · Aizen et al., Annals of Botany, 2009

Medicine

Of blood groups O, A and AB, which waits longest on average for a kidney from a deceased donor?

Group O. An O kidney is compatible with patients of every group, but an O patient can accept only an O kidney. Left alone, other groups drain the O supply, so allocation schemes now reserve O kidneys mostly for O patients. AB patients can take any group and wait least.

The trap: Picking AB, reasoning that the rarest group is hardest to match.

Bar move

Whoever can give to everyone may be the one left waiting.

Source · NHS Blood and Transplant, kidney transplantation report

Music

A DJ pushes a vinyl turntable’s pitch fader to +8% to match tempos. What happens to the music’s key?

It rises by about 1.3 semitones. A turntable plays faster by spinning faster, so every frequency rises by 8%. A semitone is a ratio of about 1.06, so 1.08 is a semitone and a third. Two records matched in tempo this way can clash in key, so DJs learn to mix by key.

The trap: Tempo and pitch feel like separate controls, as on digital decks.

Bar move

When one dial moves several things, expect all of them to move.

Source · Butler, Unlocking the Groove, 2006

Medicine

A test for a rare disease is 99% accurate. You test positive. How likely is it that you're ill?

About 9%. If one person in a thousand has the disease, the test finds them — and flags about ten healthy people alongside. Most positives are false ones.

Bar move

Before you trust a positive, ask how rare the thing is.

Technology

Face recognition is 99% accurate. It flags a face in a crowd of 100,000. How likely is it that they're on the watchlist?

About 1%. If ten people in that crowd are on the list, the system finds them — and a thousand who aren't. Same maths, different world.
